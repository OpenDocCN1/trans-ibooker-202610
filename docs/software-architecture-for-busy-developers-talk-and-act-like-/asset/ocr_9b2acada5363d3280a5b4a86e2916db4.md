However, there's a noticeable difference between DI and factory patterns. On the one hand, with factory patterns, the consumer class is still triggering the creation process itself, even though it is delegated to other classes. On the other hand, with DI, client classes benefit from the inversion of control (IoC) principle and receive concrete implementations on the fly.
