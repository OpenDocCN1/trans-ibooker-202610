# Environmental Goals and Business Goals Are Not Two Distinct Goal Sets

They are integral to each other each sets goals depend upon the other of Earth’s longevity. Research gains inroads into “use applications and variable cross-media impacts and positive benefits become in outcome based monitoring that balances input to outgo.” Environment and business benefit through keeping product users using products that are cleanly made and reproduced to maintain a healthy environment and the environment benefits by being properly used and energized so that it is wholesome and reproduces rather that becoming extinct. Research progresses in gaining knowledge of how to do more with less and this makes a win-win situation that positively engages all concerned from inquiry to pilot to creation of backing in industrial applications. Careful evaluations include alternative methodology for containment of toxicity, pollution, and resource conservation. Constant and consistent evaluation and monitoring of health and resource retention is wise and imperative.

Global compliance considers not only those who are product users, sources of product materials, but those who design, test, process, and package products. There is the consideration of product viability, value, and depth of application over time.

Testing of design theoretically as well as in action function and pilots are imperative because need is the frequent but not the only driver of product creation. Extrapolation that reveals other needs or interactions that follow or extend the uses that start with the originally identified need then more testing is required. If need becomes a red herring driver then tests lose their ability to balance the integral process and the important ability to have strong responsible action be achieved. Planned obsolescence rarely generates value with longevity. Products that take on inflexible aspects or have no competition to encourage continued positive growth become causes of waste and are self-destructive.

Assembly of products requires knowledge of the philosophy of future needs as well as present needs and how the future helps direct the present. Understanding what drives change and its creative limits also includes what and where support for change comes from and how time is a significant limiting factor. If the answer to a need is created after a need is revealed there is potential for much waste and loss of viable access to environmental support.
