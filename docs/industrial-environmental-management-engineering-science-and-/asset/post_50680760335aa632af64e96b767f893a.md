## 5.14 How much fresh outside air is needed to maintain the workplace air concentration of methyl ethyl ketone below 100 ppm, the TLV–TWA.

During degrease operations, an estimated 3 gal of MEK evaporated in an 8-hour shift, while ventilation quality is rated as average. Also, compute the air required if the operation is carried out in an enclosed hood with an opening of 50 ft² and a face velocity of 100 ft/min. The temperature is 81 °F and the pressure is 1 atm. The specific gravity of MEK is 0.81 and its molecular weight is 72.1. And the k-value is 1/8 (0.125). Use the following equation, calculate required ventilation dilution air.

$$Q_v = \left(\frac{Q_m R_g T}{k C_{ppm} P M}\times 10^6\right)$$

where $Q_v$ is the ventilation dilution air flow in ft³/min, $Q_m$ is the evaporation rate, $M$ is the molecular weight of a volatile chemical, $R_g$ is the ideal gas constant, $T$ is the absolute temperature, $k$ is the ventilation safety factor, and $C$ is the vapor concentration in ppm.
