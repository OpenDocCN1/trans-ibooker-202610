# 7.14.2.1 Electricity from Combustion of Biomass

The capital and operating costs for steam power plants fired with biomass are relatively well known because of significant operating experience with these systems. The capital cost for a new plant ranges between $1400 and $1800/kW capacity. Accordingly, a 50-MW biomass plant based on direct-combustion would cost approximately $80million. On the basis of a target price of $41.90/GJ for biomass, the cost of production for direct-fired biomass power is about $0.06/kWh (Environmental Law & Policy Center 2001).
