Go ahead and click on the word **Disabled**, and now look for a section entitled **Teams**. Click on the **Tasks** button and choose to create a **New team**.

Give your new team an appropriate name and select the NICs that you want to be part of this team. Once finished, you can walk through the same steps as many times as you need to create additional teams with your remaining NICs:
