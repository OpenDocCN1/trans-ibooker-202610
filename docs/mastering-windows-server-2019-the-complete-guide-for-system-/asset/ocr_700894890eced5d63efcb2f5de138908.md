# The Settings menu

Making in-depth modifications to any of your VMs typically means right-clicking on that VM, and then navigating to **Settings...** for that particular VM. Inside **Settings,** you can adjust any aspect of your VM's hardware, which is the most common reason to visit this screen. Immediately upon opening **Settings,** you have the option to **Add Hardware** to your VM. This is the place you would visit to add more hard drive controllers or NICs to your virtual server:
