In the past I have tried to take manual photos of screens using everything from USB cameras to cell phones. I have even tried using a high-end digital camera on a tripod, but the results were just not that good. I would get flare and glare or miss time when I took the photos.

About two years ago, inexpensive USB HDMI video capture devices started to come on the market. Figure 5-1 shows how these devices plug in between a computer and its monitor and then that content is streamed to another computer using a USB cable.

![](bbox=[0.095, 0.333, 0.86, 0.584])

**Figure 5-1.** *USB Capture Device*

Software is required to display and capture the output that is being streamed. After trying a few different software packages like Windows Camera, VLC, and Camtasia, I found Open Broadcaster Software (OBS) to perform the best. It is a free, widely used open source cross-platform streaming and recording program. It is truly a multiplatform software – there are currently versions of OBS Studio available for Microsoft Windows, macOS, and Linux.
