VMware has discussed use cases for ESXi on Arm in the datacenter (vSAN witness nodes, Arm servers, SmartNICs, etc.). They have also discussed edge and IoT uses like wind turbine monitoring, discrete video surveillance, and even stand-alone Arm-based point of sale (POS) devices.

The supported hardware for ESXi on Arm includes datacenter servers such as the Lenovo ThinkSystem HR350A (Ampere eMAG) (Figure 2-4), near-edge devices like the SolidRun HoneyComb LX2 (Figure 2-4), and far-edge devices including the Raspberry RPi 4B - 4GB or 8GB model (Figure 2-5).
