# Cased Builds

The following build kits use a case, power supplies, and new (rather than repurposed) storage devices. While you may be tempted to get a case with a touch screen or other “built-in” features, this would be a waste as ESXi will not support it, and it may even prevent ESXi from installing and/or running correctly.

# Cheap Pi Enclosure

For this Pi enclosure build (Table 4-2), I used one of the cheapest cases I could find. This case is made from black plastic and does come with a large 40mm fan and four heat sinks (see Figure 4-5). The micro-SD card is easily accessible. The fan is powered by the GPIO 3.3V pin for low-speed operation or the 5V pin for high-speed operation.

I was pleasantly surprised with this case. It was well made and included decent instructions. When I put the Pi in, it snapped together without any difficulties.
