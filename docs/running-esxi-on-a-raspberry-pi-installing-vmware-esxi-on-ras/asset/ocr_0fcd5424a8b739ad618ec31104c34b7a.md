# Argon ONE Kit M.2

This Argon ONE case is the same one I used for my first build and one of the more popular cases for ESXi on Arm on Pi builds (Table 4-3). The top of the case is made of cast aluminum and comes with a 30mm fan and three heat sinks. It has an expansion board that plugs into the Pi and provides two full-size HDMI connectors (see Figure 4-6). The instruction manual was also very good.
