# diskAshur M2 High-Security Drive

I tested a 1TB iStorage diskAshur M2 drive (IS DAM2-256-1000). The M2 is a rugged and highly secure drive. I had previously tested its ruggedness by throwing it off a two-story balcony and driving over it with a 3.8-ton truck. For security it requires a passcode to be entered before the contents of the drive can be read. If the wrong passcode is entered after five times, the contents of the drive will be unreadable.
