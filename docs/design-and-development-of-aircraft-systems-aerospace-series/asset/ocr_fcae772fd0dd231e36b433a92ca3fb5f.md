# 3.12 Whole Lifecycle Tasks

In addition to the specific domain engineering tasks in the processes described above, there are other engineering tasks that take place continuously throughout the lifecycle. Some of these tasks are to do with exercising control over the process, whilst others are carried out to impose a consistent approach to certain disciplines across the domains. This is an important integrating activity that ensures that all domain engineers adhere to standards and processes wherever they practice their individual skill. This integration activity was introduced in Chapter 1. In modern projects where engineering tasks may be spread across international partners, such integration is essential for project consistency.
