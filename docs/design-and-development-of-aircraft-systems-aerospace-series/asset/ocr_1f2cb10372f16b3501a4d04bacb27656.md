**Figure 10.10**  Alternative hydraulic power sources. EDP, engine drive pump; Gen, generator; AMP, air motor pump; ACMP, AC motor pump; DCMP, DC motor pump; RAT, ram air turbine.

The discussion so far has concentrated on the loss of the control function. However, systems such as flight control also need ‘muscle’ in the form of hydraulic and electrical systems to enable them to function. In the example shown in Figure 10.10 there are sources of hydraulic power derived from:

+   - engine-driven pumps (EDPs)
+   - air-driven pumps using bleed air (ADPs)
+   - AC-driven pumps (ACMPs)
+   - DC-motor pumps (DCMPs)
+   - ram air turbines (RATs) or even batteries.
