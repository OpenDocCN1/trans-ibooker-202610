It can readily be seen that complex operations relating pilot and system interactions could involve all of the above transaction types sequentially. As an example of the impact of system time delays, if each delay is 10 ms on average then a potential total delay from sensor to warning to demand arriving back at the transfer pump could be as much as 180 ms. To this must be added the time taken for the crew to react to the warning, decide what to do, and then to select transfer. There are some instances where this delay may pose a safety hazard. There are other types of system where such a latency of data will be totally unacceptable and may lead to system instability.
