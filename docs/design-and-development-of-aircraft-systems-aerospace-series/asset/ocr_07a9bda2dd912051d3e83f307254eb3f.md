The modern view of systems engineering seeks to engender a balance of skill, experience, and judgement that removes personal bias in order to achieve a system that meets the needs of the customer and is considered fit for its intended purpose. The creative aspects of systems engineering must not be overlooked. There are links between creativity in art and creativity in engineering (MacDonald 1998) and this should not be underestimated. The discipline of systems engineering is about the creation and realisation of elegant systems solutions in a complex world.
