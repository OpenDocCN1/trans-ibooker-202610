# 2.6 Generic System Definition

An aircraft will be equipped with various combinations of the systems listed above according to its particular role. Some of the systems will be integral to the aircraft, others will be carried as role equipment in pallets or wing-mounted pods. The majority of these engineering systems are similar in their format. +     - Demand (or command) is a conscious input to the system to demand a deliberate response. The demand may be from an operator or from another system. Typically, the demand will result from the operator moving a selection mechanism, e.g. throttle levers, a switch, control column, steering wheel or tiller. Modern techniques have allowed demands to be obtained from direct voice input (DVI) or by cursor control devices such as a mouse or tracker ball. For unmanned or remotely piloted aircraft the demands will be from a ground facility using a data link.
