Although these were simple enough measurements the questions for future projects included:

+   - How many discrete switches are going to be needed and what impact will this have on the control panel area?
+   - How many new items for display are needed and what impact will this have on the panel area?
+   - What alternative technologies are emerging that might lead to even more displays and switches?

These were important questions that eventually led to the decision to make use of colour display screens and ‘soft’ or multi-function switches, as technology made them available of course. Over time this led to the introduction of flat-screen technology and touch screens.

Since that time there have been significant changes in the technology employed which have resulted in reductions in air-crew complement. This has resulted in a reliance on the aircraft systems to perform some routine tasks of automatic flight, systems performance, and failure monitoring.
