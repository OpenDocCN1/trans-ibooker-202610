Persistence almost always pays off. Either you’ll get what you set out for, or, in the process of searching for it, you’ll learn a valuable lesson or land on an even better solution. This is particularly true in start-ups, where, at the onset, a company might go through four or five different strategies as it searches out its niche. In this world, you can have all of the talent in the world, but it won’t mean a single thing if you quit when you hit the first little snag.
