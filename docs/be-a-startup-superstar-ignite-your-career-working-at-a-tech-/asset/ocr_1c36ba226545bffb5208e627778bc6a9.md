# HOW TO FIND A JOB AND QUICKLY  
CLIMB THE RANKS AT A TECH STARTUP,  
EVEN IF YOU’RE NOT A TECHIE

An increasing number of recent college graduates are underemployed or struggling to find a fulfilling career. For many, the well-worn path from an MBA program to the daily corporate grind appears to be the only option—they seem blind to the possibility of working for a company that’s just starting out. Tech startups are springing up everywhere. You don’t have to be a tech wiz to succeed at a tech firm; these up-and-coming companies are desperate for all kinds of talent and creativity from people with new ideas and leadership skills.

Be a *Startup Superstar* is a must-read book for any recent graduate or job-seeker looking to turn their general business know-how into a successful career at a tech startup. In the years since he left his safe corporate job to work in the world of tech startups, author Steven Mark Kahan has successfully helped seven startup companies sell or go public. In this book, Steve shares his insights on getting hired in a fast-paced workplace where you can be creative, make good money, rise to the top, and stay there.
