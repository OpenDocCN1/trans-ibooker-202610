# Discussion

Sorting groups ensure that all members of that group are drawn together, and that no other objects can be drawn between them.

For example, Figures 5-15 and 5-16 both show two characters, each of which is composed of a number of other sprites. These sprites have their sort order configured so that, for example, the head is drawn on top of the body, and one of the arms is drawn behind the body. In Figure 5-15, the two characters have a sorting group on their parent object, while in Figure 5-16, they don’t. This results in the various body parts incorrectly overlapping the other character’s.
