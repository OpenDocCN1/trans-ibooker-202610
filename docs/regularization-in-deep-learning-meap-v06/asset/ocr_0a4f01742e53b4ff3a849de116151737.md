# 1.2 Curse of dimensionality

Why is a modern machine learning model easily over-parameterized and prone to overfitting? The answer has to do with the rise of Big Data. Recent years have witnessed a significant increase in the four Vs. of Big Data: volume, the scale of data; velocity, streaming data moving at high frequency; variety, different forms of data; and veracity, the uncertainty of data. The four Vs. are primarily driven by advances in modern data collection touchpoints: live-streaming surveillance video cameras, tons of web searches and conversations, and ongoing business transactions of all sorts. Together, they present unprecedented and exciting challenges to both storage and modeling approaches.

One immediate challenge is the high dimensionality. The multitudes of features in the data also present the *curse of dimensionality* challenge to modelling, making it difficult to design an algorithm that can effectively learn the underlying pattern in high dimensions. Traditional statistical approaches such as linear regression work best when dealing with a small set of features. In the presence of high dimensionalities, such as image data that easily contain tens of thousands of features, it requires much more complex algorithms to identify, extract, and transform essential features and make good predictions.
