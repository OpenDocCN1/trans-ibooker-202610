# Figure 7.18 Geometric interpretation of RSS and L1 norm in LASSO regression.

Now, when minimizing the LASSO-regularized objective function, we are essentially seeking a solution point on the graph that corresponds to an intersection between an ellipse (the RSS term) and a diamond (the L1 norm), where the hyperparameter lambda controls the size of each shape. The intersection point tends to occur at the pointy edge of the diamond (with some probability), as shown in figure 7.19. Such probability is even higher in a high-dimensional setting, where the solution will likely hit one of the high-dimensional corners.
