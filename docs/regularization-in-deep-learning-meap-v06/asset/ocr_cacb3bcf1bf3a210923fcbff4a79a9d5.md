Figure 3.13 When using up to the 16th degree of polynomial, the model is perfectly interpolating as a smooth curve, which is a result of the roughness penalty. This is the over-parameterized regime in the modern generalization sense.

Just for the sake of experimentation, let us see what happens if we use up to the 29th degree of polynomial. As shown in figure 3.14, the resulting fit does not change much even if the number of polynomial terms is almost doubled compared to earlier. The relatively stable fit shows that the resulting models in the over-parameterized regime are similar in the effective model capacity. Adding more polynomial terms does not make much difference under this regime.
