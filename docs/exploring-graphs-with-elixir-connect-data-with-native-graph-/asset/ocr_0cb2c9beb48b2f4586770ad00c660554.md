The lambda expression we passed as a parameter to the flatMap method returned, for a given file, a Stream of its children (if any). Otherwise, it returned a stream with just the file. The flatMap method gracefully handled that and mapped these streams into a resulting collection of streams and finally flattened it into one final Stream of FileS.

flatMap eliminates so much effort—it nicely combines a sequence of two operations, often called a monadic composition—into one single elegant step.
