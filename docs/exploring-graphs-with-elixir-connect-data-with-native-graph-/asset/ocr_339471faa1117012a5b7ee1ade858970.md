Suppose we approach the business for clarification, and they suggest to print the names when successful and print error details upon failures; we can design for that. But, if they change their mind, we want our design to accommodate that with as little change to the code as possible. For this reason, the place where the error happens is often not the right place to deal with the errors. Most of our code may have to propagate the result and failures to the calling code, and let the code at the edge deal with the display of results or the proper handling of errors, based on the requirements.
