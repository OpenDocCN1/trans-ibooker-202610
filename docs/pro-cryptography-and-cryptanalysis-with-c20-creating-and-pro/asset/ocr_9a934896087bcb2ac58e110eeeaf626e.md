# Conditional Probability

Definition 2.7 [1]. Let’s consider E₁ and E₂ as being two events, with P(E₂) > 0.

The conditional probability for E₁ to give E₂ is written as P(E₁|E₂) and it is expressed as

P(E₁|E₂) = P(E₁ ∩ E₂) / P(E₂).

P(E₁|E₂) measures the probability of how event E₁ will take place, given that E₂ has occurred.
