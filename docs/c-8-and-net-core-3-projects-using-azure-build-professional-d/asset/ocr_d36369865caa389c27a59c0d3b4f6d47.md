# Subscription/resource group

Your subscription is likely to have either been provided by your organization (for example, MyCompanyLtd Dev) or you may be running on a private MSDN subscription. Depending on your organization's size and the size of the product you're working on, you may have a subscription for the product that you're working on, a subscription for the entire company, or somewhere in between. If you're running on an MSDN account, then that will appear here, too.
