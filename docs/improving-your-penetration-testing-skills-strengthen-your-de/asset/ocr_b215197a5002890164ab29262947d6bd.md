In Chapter 5, Detecting and Exploiting Injection-Based Flaws, in the Extracting data with SQL Injection section, we extracted a list of usernames and hashes. Here, we will use John the Ripper (or simply John) and Hashcat to try and retrieve the passwords corresponding to those hashes.

First, retrieve the hashes and usernames in a file in a username:hash format, such as the following:
