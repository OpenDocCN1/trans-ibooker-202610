# Creating a camera in C4D

Adding a camera is done just like it is in After Effects. Simply click on the Camera icon. It will add it to your scene. However, you need to tell C4D to look through the new camera. To do that, follow these steps:

+   1. Add a camera to your scene
+   2. Choose Cameras | Use camera
