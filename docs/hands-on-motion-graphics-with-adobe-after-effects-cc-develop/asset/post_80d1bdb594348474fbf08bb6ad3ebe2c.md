## Using the graph editor

Let's ease our key frames with the key frame assistant. We can further edit the movement of our key frames by selecting the graph editor. To use the graph editor, go through the following steps:

- 1. Ease your key frames.
- 2. Select your key frames.
- 3. Look in the middle of the timeline above the lock icon. Click on the graph editor icon.
- 4. Click on the graph type.
- 5. Choose the Edit Speed Graph.
