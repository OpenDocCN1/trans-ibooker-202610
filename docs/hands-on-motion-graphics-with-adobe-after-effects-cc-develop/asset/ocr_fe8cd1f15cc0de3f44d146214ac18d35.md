# Building a 3D C4D Lite Logo Project

Give yourself a pat on the back for making it to the Cinema 4D (C4D) Lite chapter; you're going to love it! After Effects and C4D Lite give you professional-grade tools at your fingertips that let you add 3D elements to your compositions. C4D Lite and After Effects together is a powerful combination! Just like with any of the Adobe software, these two programs work well together. The workflow is seamless; you can easily jump back and forth between the programs.

+   - Creating a C4D layer in After Effects

+   - Importing Illustrator files into C4D
+   - Creating text in C4D
+   - Creating an eye with sweep nurbs
+   - Modeling a logo star
+   - Creating a camera in C4D
+   - Rendering C4D project from After Effects
