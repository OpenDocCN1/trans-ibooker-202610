Objects

When you click on the Cube icon, the cube automatically appears in your project. To move or scale any object in your scene, use the move tools in the upper-right corner. You have access to the move tool, which allows you to move your object on the xyz axis. The scale tools allow you to increase the size of your object, and the rotation tool allows you to rotate your object. Just like in After Effects, these objects rotate around the axis point.
