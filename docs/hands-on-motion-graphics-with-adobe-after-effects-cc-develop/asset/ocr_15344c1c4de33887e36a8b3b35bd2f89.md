Rotate tool

Notice the colored bars; these represent the different parameters you can rotate your object around. Let's take a look at the navigation tools. Jump all of the way over to the upper-right corner of the project screen. These four little buttons enable you to navigate around your scene and to look at your project in different views. You are able to do the following:

+   - Move
+   - Zoom
+   - Rotate around in your project
+   - Look at different views
