{f^n(⊥) | n ≥ 0} and since ∪({f^n(⊥) | n ≥ 0}) is the least upper bound of {f^n(⊥) | n ≥ 0}, we obtain ∪({f^n(⊥) | n ≥ 0}) ⊆ d.
