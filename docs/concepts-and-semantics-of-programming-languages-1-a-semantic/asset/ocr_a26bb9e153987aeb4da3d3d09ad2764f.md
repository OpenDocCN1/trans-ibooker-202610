$f_0(\text{Mem}) \neq \text{undef}$ and since $f_1 \sqsubseteq f_2$, we obtain $f_1(f_0(\text{Mem})) = f_2(f_0(\text{Mem}))$, allowing us to conclude. Now, we must prove that $F$ is continuous:

# PROPOSITION 4.7.-- The functional $F : f \mapsto \text{cond}([\![e]\!]_{\text{Env}}, f \circ [\![c]\!]_{\text{Env}}, id)$ is a continuous function.

PROOF.-- Taking $F_1 : f \mapsto \text{cond}([\![e]\!]_{\text{Env}}, f, id)$ and $F_2 : f \mapsto f \circ [\![c]\!]_{\text{Env}}$, we have $F(f) = F_1(F_2(f)) = F_1(f \circ [\![c]\!]_{\text{Env}}) = \text{cond}([\![e]\!]_{\text{Env}}, f \circ [\![c]\!]_{\text{Env}}, id)$ hence $F = F_1 \circ F_2$. According to proposition 4.5, function $F_1$ is continuous, and according to proposition 4.6, function $F_2$ is continuous. Lemma 4.6 thus gives the conclusion.
