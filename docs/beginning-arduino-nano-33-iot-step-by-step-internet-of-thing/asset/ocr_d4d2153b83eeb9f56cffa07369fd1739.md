# CHAPTER 6　BLUETOOTH LOW ENERGY (BLE)

First, open Serial Monitor to see program output from the LED program. Now you can open the nRF Connect for Mobile application from your platform. You should see the BLE service on this application, as shown in Figure 6-7. Tap the CONNECT button to connect to Arduino Nano 33 IoT.

![](bbox=[0.343, 0.254, 0.615, 0.645])

**Figure 6-8.** *Showing the BLE service characteristics*

After connecting, you will obtain a form, as shown in Figure 6-8. You can expand the BLE service characteristics. We have two properties: READ and WRITE.

Tap the WRITE property icon. Then, set a value 15 to turn on the LED, as shown in Figure 6-9. Tap SEND to send this value. You should see the LED lighting on Arduino Nano 33 IoT. You also can send 00 to turn off the LED on the BLE service WRITE, as shown in Figure 6-10.
