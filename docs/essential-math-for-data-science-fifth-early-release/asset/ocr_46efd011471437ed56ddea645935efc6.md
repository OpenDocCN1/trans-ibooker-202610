Notice that I am finding the area bounded between the line and the x-axis, and in the x range 0 to 1.0. If you recall basic geometry formulas, the area A for a triangle is A = \frac{1}{2}bh where b is the length of the base and h is the height. We can visually spot above that b = 1 and h = 2. So plugging into the formula we get for our area 1.0 as calculated below:

A = \frac{1}{2}bh
A = \frac{1}{2}*1*2
A = 1
