# IS RANDOMNESS BAD?

If this randomness feels uncomfortable where you get a different answer every time you run a piece of code, welcome to the world of machine learning, optimization, and stochastic algorithms! Many algorithms that do approximations are random-based, and while some are extremely useful others can be sloppy and perform poorly as you might expect.

A lot of people look to machine learning and AI as some tool that gives objective and precise answers, but that cannot be further from the truth. Machine learning produces approximations with a degree of uncertainty, often without ground truth once in production. Machine learning can be misused if one is not aware of how it works, and it is remiss to not acknowledge their nondeterministic and approximate nature.

While randomness can create some powerful tools, it can also be abused. Be careful to not use seed values and randomness to p-hack a “good” result, and put effort into analyzing your data and model.
