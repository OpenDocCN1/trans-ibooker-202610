# # BASIS VECTORS IN 3D AND BEYOND

You might be wondering how we think of vector transformations in 3 dimensions or more. The concept of basis vectors extends quite nicely. If I have a 3-dimensional vector space, then I have basis vectors \hat{\imath}, \hat{\jmath}, and \hat{k}. I just keep adding more letters from the alphabet for each new dimension (Figure 4-19).
