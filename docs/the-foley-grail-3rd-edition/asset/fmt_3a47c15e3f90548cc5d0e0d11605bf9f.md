## ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Vanessa Theme Ament is a former Foley artist and sound editor who has performed Foley in films and television since 1980. She worked on many notable films including *Predator*, *Die Hard*, *Noises Off*, *A Goofy Movie*, *Dolores Claiborne*, *Malice*, *RoboCop2*, and *Platoon*, which won the Academy Award for Best Sound in 1986. In 1989 she demonstrated Foley on *Late Night with David Letterman*. She completed her PhD in Moving Image Studies at Georgia State University and has recently written chapters on film sound in several edited books. Her book *Divergent Tracks: How Three Film Communities Revolutionized Digital Film Sound* was released in 2021. Vanessa is the former Edwin F. and Virginia B. Ball Endowed Chair in Telecommunications at Ball State University, Muncie, Indiana.

## ABOUT THE ILLUSTRATOR

David Stone is a sound editor and a veteran of over 100 feature films, including *Gremlins*, *Top Gun*, *Die Hard*, and *Ocean’s Eleven*. He was the supervising sound editor for *Predator*, *Edward Scissorhands*, *Beauty and the Beast*, and *Batman Returns*, among others. He is the winner of five Golden Reel Awards, and he won the 1993 Academy Award for Sound Effects Editing for his supervising work on *Bram Stoker’s Dracula*. Dave worked as a commercial artist, hoping for a career as an animator, before his addiction to sound editing began. He has illustrated the two previous editions of *The Foley Grail* as well as this third edition, and along with Vanessa Theme Ament, he wrote *Hollywood Sound Design and Moviesound Newsletter: A Case Study of the End of the Analog Age*.
