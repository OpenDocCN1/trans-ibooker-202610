# AI Act: EU lawmakers sign off to regulate artificial intelligence

The European Parliament approved the Artificial Intelligence Act on Wednesday, a landmark regulation that will govern the use of AI in Europe. The law takes a risk-based approach, imposing stricter rules for high-risk AI systems.

+   - Prohibited uses: real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces
+   - High-risk categories: critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services
+   - Obligations: risk management, data governance, transparency, human oversight

The regulation will enter into force gradually, with most provisions applying by 2026.
