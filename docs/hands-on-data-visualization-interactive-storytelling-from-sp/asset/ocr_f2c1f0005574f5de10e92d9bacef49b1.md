Histograms are designed to show broad patterns of data distribution, not individual values. The preceding histogram shows that while most nations have an average daily supply around 2,800 calories per capita, 8 nations have fewer than 2,000, and 11 nations have more than 3,500. Without annotations, histograms don’t tell us the names of those particular countries. But they do offer a better sense of the shape of the data distribution.
