Meanwhile, the role of the core network is to facilitate information sharing, amplify and support the work of its members, and catalyze action on a broader scale. Like the core team of an action network, core networks provide a leadership role to steward the larger whole.

The Agribusiness Market Ecosystem Alliance (AMEA) is an example of a movement network working at both global and local levels to accelerate the development of professional farmer organizations, reaching millions of smallholder farms in eighty countries. AMEA’s core network consists of twenty-seven member organizations, including Heifer International, Mercy Corps, and the Rainforest Alliance, who are collaborating to create systemic change in the agricultural sector. Simultaneously, the core network also catalyzes and supports regional networks to create thriving local ecosystems for agribusiness stakeholders. AMEA currently includes five regional networks, embedded within the countries of Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Ivory Coast, and Honduras, with plans to support the growth of additional networks in Latin America and West Africa in the years ahead. The regional networks are free to make decisions and take actions within their local context, provided they agree to a set of shared principles that guide the movement network as a whole.
