+   - **1—Caution:** I have major concerns about the proposal creating harm and would like to discuss further.
+   - **0—Oppose:** The proposed course of action is outside a range of tolerance and requires changes before any action is taken.

The proposal goes forward as outlined if there are only 2s and above. If there are 0s or 1s, options include having real-time discussions to resolve objections or scheduling time for the 0s and 1s to meet with the 4s and 5s to resolve issues and find a way to move forward.

When we use consent-based decision-making, proposals are encouraged to proceed as long as they are consistent with the network’s purpose and principles and are unlikely to cause harm. Consequently, the process gives autonomy to those who volunteered to lead the implementation of an idea they’re excited about. This creates a bias toward action, with a responsibility to consider the impact of the whole. Consent thus encourages people across the network to step into leadership roles and pursue their varied, creative interests. This kind of diversity of action fosters a culture of experimentation and empowerment, and it makes the network a much more fun and innovative place to be.

# Embedding Evaluation

Evaluation is central to good network leadership. Any impact network will benefit from embedding evaluation practices, as the data collected will generate valuable learnings and help guide its evolution. Therefore, evaluation should be incorporated from the very start, not later after the network has begun to advance specific projects.¹¹

A good evaluation protocol provides regular assessments of the network’s health and functioning, including opportunities for participant feedback. This data helps network leaders highlight areas of strength and opportunities for improvement, guide collective decision-making, and inform the design of network activities and convenings. In addition, the data is likely to help make the case that the network is progressing, even before tangible project outcomes have been realized.
