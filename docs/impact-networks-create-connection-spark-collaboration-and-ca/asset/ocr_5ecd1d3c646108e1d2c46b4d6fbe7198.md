Reflecting on the growth of the Santa Cruz Mountains Stewardship Network, Dylan Skybrook, the network’s coordinator and only full-time staff member, sees relationships as foundational to its success. The power of relationships is shown most clearly when the network holds conversations on controversial topics and when people go the extra mile to support each other’s work, says Skybrook, but what might be most important is that relationships are now just the norm: “I don’t think people even notice anymore that they trust each other and have relationships; it’s just the way it is. Everyone is constantly engaging with each other and finding ways to work together. It creates an invisible weave across the whole region for better land stewardship.”¹²

It is because of the relationships that the whole system now works in tandem. Information is shared freely between organizations, institutions, and government agencies. One network member expressed with amazement that they could now “just pick up the phone and talk” when they had an issue with how another organization was conducting its business.
