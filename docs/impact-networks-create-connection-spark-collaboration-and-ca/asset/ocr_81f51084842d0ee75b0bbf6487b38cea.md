One of the most valuable qualities of impact networks is that many of their benefits are likely to endure even after the network’s formal life cycle ends. By engaging in a network, participants develop a greater awareness of the interconnectedness of the system of which they’re a part and a deeper appreciation for the needs of others. Even without continued engagement, that shift is likely to sustain. Likewise, though relationships may fade, the foundation of trust that bonds people together will never fully disappear. As a result, those involved in a network will often continue to call on each other for information, guidance, and support for years to come, and resources will continue to flow more freely than they did before. By strengthening the fabric of our social systems, we become more resilient to change and more responsive in times of urgent need.
