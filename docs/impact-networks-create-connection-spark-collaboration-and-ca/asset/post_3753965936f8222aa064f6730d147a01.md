# About the Author

![](img/4854536a9a7bcce4fd5250605cae8350_232_0.png)

**David Ehrlichman** is cofounder and coordinator of Converge ([converge.net](http://converge.net)). With his colleagues, he has supported the development of dozens of impact networks taking action on issues as diverse as economic mobility, human rights, access to science, and health care reform.

Prior to catalyzing Converge, David was a consultant with Monitor Institute, where he was first introduced to the power of networks. He lives near Seattle, Washington, on the traditional land of the Coast Salish peoples, specifically the Stillaguamish and Suquamish.

When he’s not writing or working with networks, you can usually find him spending time with family and friends, out in nature, or playing music. He lives with his wife, Nabetse; two goofy dogs; and his newborn daughter —three months old at the time of this book’s publication.

You can reach David at **ehrlichman@converge.net**.

# About Converge

![](img/4854536a9a7bcce4fd5250605cae8350_233_0.png)

**Converge** is a network of systems strategists, designers, facilitators, educators, and evaluators committed to co-creating positive impact. We specialize in supporting purpose-driven individuals and organizations to operationalize collaboration through a network approach.

As a network ourselves, we are a living laboratory of practitioners dedicated to network leadership. To stay informed of our latest writings, resources, and learning opportunities, you’re invited to visit converge.net and sign up for our email list and a library of free tools and facilitation guides to support your network.

To connect with Converge, write to us at connect@converge.net. We would be grateful to learn about inspiring examples of networks, beautiful practices you use in your work, and how we can help spread the word about the power of impact networks.
