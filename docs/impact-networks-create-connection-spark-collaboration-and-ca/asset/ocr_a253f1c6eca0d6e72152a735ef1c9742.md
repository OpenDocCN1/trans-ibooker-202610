# Learning Network

FIGURE 3.2. The basic structure of a learning network, featuring bounded participation, learning circles, and the consistent flow of information in, out, and across the network.

Action networks build on all the traits of learning networks by accelerating the flow of information in, out, and across the network, while also taking the next step to actively spark collaboration. One of the ways they foster collaboration is through project teams (sometimes called working groups or task forces), as seen in figure 3.3. Project teams exist to advance a specific body of work related to the network’s purpose. Some learning networks also form project teams—as noted earlier, the forms of impact networks are often fluid—but it is less common.
