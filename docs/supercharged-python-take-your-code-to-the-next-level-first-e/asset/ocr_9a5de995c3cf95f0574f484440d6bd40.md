With this graph, there’s even more you can do. Within this time period, there is a day that Microsoft stock had its highest volume of sales. By moving the mouse pointer to the apex of the line, you can see that this high volume occurred in late December, and that the number of shares traded that day was more than 110,000,000: a hundred and ten million shares, worth more than eleven billion dollars.
