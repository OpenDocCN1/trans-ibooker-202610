The _rate_ is the interest rate expressed as a floating-point number rather than a percentage. For example, a rate of 0.065 would amount to an interest rate of 6.5 percent. Because this is the rate for each individual payment period, you’d need to divide by 12 to get the monthly interest rate from the yearly rate.

The _nper_ argument is the total number of payment periods. You need to multiply the number of years by 12 if the payments are to be monthly rather than yearly.

The _pv_ argument is the present value. This is the amount of money being borrowed.
