Can we come up with intuitive, real-world examples that show the usefulness of a dot product? They abound in certain kinds of math and physics, such as three-dimensional geometry. But there are simpler applications. Let’s say you own a pet shop that sells three kinds of exotic birds. Table 13.5 shows the prices.
