Sport, across all levels, needs to create and foster partnerships more than ever. We have the power to connect a diverse set of partners who share the same vision of “building a better world through sport.” Only when we are united will we reap the true potential sport has as an enabler of sustainable development.

This handbook strives to raise that conversation and foster partnerships with the SDGs. Thus, it is an essential piece in the puzzle to advance sustainable development in and through global sport.
