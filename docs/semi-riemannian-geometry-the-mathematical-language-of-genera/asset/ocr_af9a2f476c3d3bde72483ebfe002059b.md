# 5.3 Representation of Tensors

Let $V$ be a vector space, let $\mathcal{H} = (h_1, \dots, h_m)$ be a basis for $V$, let $(\theta^1, \dots, \theta^m)$ be its dual basis, and let $s \ge 1$ be an integer. Following Section B.5 and Section B.6, we denote by $\mathrm{Mult}(V^s, V)$ the vector space of $\mathbb{R}$-multilinear maps from $V^s$ to $V$. Let us define a map

$\mathfrak{R}_s : \mathrm{Mult}(V^s, V) \longrightarrow \mathcal{T}_s^1(V),$

$\mathfrak{R}_s(\Psi)(\eta, v_1, \dots, v_s) = \eta\bigl(\Psi(v_1, \dots, v_s)\bigr)$ \quad (5.3.1)
