The second term in the last line of (18.2.8) can be expressed as

∑_{ijk} ∂x^i/∂x̃^p ∂x̃^k/∂x^i ∂²x^j/∂x̃^k∂x̃^q ∂/∂x^j = ∑_{jk}[ (∑_i ∂x̃^k/∂x^i ∂x^i/∂x̃^p) ∂²x^j/∂x̃^k∂x̃^q ∂/∂x^j ] = ∑_{jk} ∂x̃^k/∂x̃^p ∂²x^j/∂x̃^k∂x̃^q ∂/∂x^j = ∑_j ∂²x^j/∂x̃^p∂x̃^q ∂/∂x^j = ∑_k ∂²x^k/∂x̃^p∂x̃^q ∂/∂x^k,

so (18.2.8) becomes

∇_{∂/∂x̃^p} (∂/∂x̃^q) = ∑_k (∑_{ij} ∂x^i/∂x̃^p ∂x^j/∂x̃^q Γ^k_{ij} + ∂²x^k/∂x̃^p∂x̃^q) ∂/∂x^k. (18.2.9)

Comparing (18.2.5) and (18.2.9) shows that

∑_{ij} ∂x^i/∂x̃^p ∂x^j/∂x̃^q Γ^k_{ij} + ∂²x^k/∂x̃^p∂x̃^q = ∑_l Γ̃^l_{pq} ∂x^k/∂x̃^l.

Multiplying both sides of the preceding identity by ∂x̃^r/∂x^k and summing over k yields

∑_{ijk} ∂x^i/∂x̃^p ∂x^j/∂x̃^q ∂x̃^r/∂x^k Γ^k_{ij} + ∑_k ∂²x^k/∂x̃^p∂x̃^q ∂x̃^r/∂x^k = ∑_{kl} Γ̃^l_{pq} ∂x̃^r/∂x^k ∂x^k/∂x̃^l = ∑_l Γ̃^l_{pq} (∑_k ∂x̃^r/∂x^k ∂x^k/∂x̃^l) = ∑_l Γ̃^l_{pq} ∂x̃^r/∂x̃^l = Γ̃^r_{pq}.

We see from Theorem 15.6.3 and Theorem 18.2.3 that, despite appearances, the Christoffel symbols are not the components of a tensor. In classical terminology it is said that the Christoffel symbols “do not transform” like the components of a tensor.
