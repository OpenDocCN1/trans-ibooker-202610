(d) \(\frac{\nabla (J \circ g)}{du}(u) = \frac{dg}{du}(u) \frac{\nabla J}{dt}(g(u)).\)

Let $(M,\mathfrak{g})$ be a regular surface in $\mathbb{R}^3_{\nu}$, let $(U,\varphi)$ be a chart on $M$, and let $(H_1,H_2)$ and $G$ be the corresponding coordinate frame and coordinate unit normal vector field. Let $\lambda:(a,b)\longrightarrow M$ be a smooth curve such that $\lambda((a,b))\subset U$, and let $J$ be a vector field in $\mathfrak{X}_M(\lambda)$. By Theorem 10.1.17 and Theorem 11.2.8, the map

$\mu = \varphi^{-1}\circ\lambda = (\mu^1,\mu^1):(a,b)\longrightarrow U \quad (12.5.2)$ is smooth. Then $J(t)$ can be expressed as

$J(t)=\sum_i \alpha^i(t) H_i|_{\mu(t)}, \quad (12.5.3)$

where the $\alpha^i$ are uniquely determined functions in $C^\infty(U)$, called the components of $J$ with respect to $(U,\varphi)$. The right-hand side of $(12.5.3)$ is said to express $J$ in local coordinates with respect to $(U,\varphi)$.

(a) $\frac{dJ}{dt}(t)=\sum_k\left(\frac{d\alpha^k}{dt}(t)+\sum_{ij}\alpha^i(t)\frac{d\mu^j}{dt}(t)\Gamma^k_{ij}(\mu(t))\right)H_k|_{\mu(t)}+\left(\sum_{ij}\alpha^i(t)\frac{d\mu^j}{dt}(t)\vartheta_{ij}(\mu(t))\right)G_{\mu(t)}.$
