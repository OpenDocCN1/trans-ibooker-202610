# Theorem 7.3.1. Let V and W be vector spaces, let A : V ⟶ W be a linear map, and let η, ζ and ξ be multicovectors in Λ^s(W) and Λ^s'(W), respectively. Then:

+   (a) A*(η + ζ) = A*(η) + A*(ζ).
+   (b) A*(η ∧ ξ) = A*(η) ∧ A*(ξ).

Proof. (a): Straightforward.
(b): We begin with a preliminary result. Let ζ^1,...,ζ^{s+s'} be (not necessarily distinct) covectors in Λ^{s+s'}(W). For vectors v1,...,v_{s+s'} in W, we have
A*(ζ^1 ∧ ... ∧ ζ^{s+s'})(v1,...,v_{s+s'}) = ζ^1 ∧ ... ∧ ζ^{s+s'}(A(v1),...,A(v_{s+s'})) = det([ζ^i(A(v_j))]) [Th 7.2.8] = det([A*(ζ^i)(v_j)]) = A*(ζ^1) ∧ ... ∧ A*(ζ^{s+s'})(v1,...,v_{s+s'}). [Th 7.2.8]
Since v1,...,v_{s+s'} were arbitrary,
A*(ζ^1 ∧ ... ∧ ζ^{s+s'}) = A*(ζ^1) ∧ ... ∧ A*(ζ^{s+s'}). (7.3.2)
Now for the main proof. Let (φ^1,...,φ^n) be the dual basis corresponding to a basis for W. By Theorem 7.2.12(a), η and ξ can be expressed as
η = Σ_{I∈ℐ_{s,n}} a_I φ^I and ξ = Σ_{J∈ℐ_{s',n}} b_J φ^J.
Then
η ∧ ξ = (Σ_{I∈ℐ_{s,n}} a_I φ^I) ∧ (Σ_{J∈ℐ_{s',n}} b_J φ^J) = Σ_{I∈ℐ_{s,n}, J∈ℐ_{s',n}} a_I b_J φ^I ∧ φ^J,
hence
A*(η ∧ ξ) = Σ_{I∈ℐ_{s,n}, J∈ℐ_{s',n}} a_I b_J A*(φ^I ∧ φ^J) = Σ_{I∈ℐ_{s,n}, J∈ℐ_{s',n}} a_I b_J A*(φ^I) ∧ A*(φ^J) = (Σ_{I∈ℐ_{s,n}} a_I A*(φ^I)) ∧ (Σ_{J∈ℐ_{s',n}} b_J A*(φ^J)) = A*(η) ∧ A*(ξ),
where the second equality follows from (7.3.2).
