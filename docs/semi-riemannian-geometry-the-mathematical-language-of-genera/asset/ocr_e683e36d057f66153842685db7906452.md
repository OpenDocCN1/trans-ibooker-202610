# Theorem 7.3.2 (Pullback of Multicovector).

Let V and W be vector spaces of dimensions m and n, respectively, let ℋ and ℱ be respective bases, and let (θ¹, …, θᵐ) and (φ¹, …, φⁿ) be the corresponding dual bases. Let A : V ⟶ W be a linear map, and let I be a multi-index in ℐ_{s,n}, where s ≤ min(m,n). Then

A*(φ^I) = Σ_{J∈ℐ_{s,m}} det( ([A]^ℱ_ℋ)^I_J ) θ^J.

Proof. Let ℋ = (h₁, …, h_m). By Theorem 7.2.13(a),

A*(φ^I) = Σ_{J∈ℐ_{s,m}} A*(φ^I)(h_J) θ^J. (7.3.3)

Let [A]^ℱ_ℋ = [a^i_j]. Then (2.2.2) and (2.2.3) give

φ^i(A(h_j)) = φ^i( Σ_k a^k_j f_k ) = Σ_i a^k_j φ^i(f_k) = a^i_j,

hence

[A]^ℱ_ℋ = [φ^i(A(h_j))]. (7.3.4)

Let I = (i₁, …, i_s) and J = (j₁, …, j_s). We have

A*(φ^I)(h_J) = A*(φ^{i₁} ∧ ⋯ ∧ φ^{i_s})(h_{j₁}, …, h_{j_s}) = A*(φ^{i₁}) ∧ ⋯ ∧ A*(φ^{i_s})(h_{j₁}, …, h_{j_s}) [Th 7.3.1(b)] = det( [A*(φ^{i})(h_j)]_{(i₁,…,i_s)}^{(j₁,…,j_s)} ) [Th 7.2.8] (7.3.5) = det( [φ^i(A(h_j))]_{(i₁,…,i_s)}^{(j₁,…,j_s)} ) = det( ([A]^ℱ_ℋ)^I_J ). [(7.3.4)]

Substituting (7.3.5) into (7.3.3) gives the result.

Special cases of Theorem 7.3.2 provide a number of useful identities.

# Theorem 7.3.3.

Let V and W be vector spaces of dimension m, let ℋ = (h₁, …, h_m) and ℱ = (f₁, …, f_m) be respective bases, and let (θ¹, …, θᵐ) and (φ¹, …, φᵐ) be the corresponding dual bases. Let A : V ⟶ W be a linear map, and let η be a multicovector in Λ^m(W). Then:

(a)

A*(φ¹ ∧ ⋯ ∧ φᵐ) = det( [A]^ℱ_ℋ ) θ¹ ∧ ⋯ ∧ θᵐ.

(b)

A*(η)(h₁, …, h_m) = det( [A]^ℱ_ℋ ) η(f₁, …, f_m).
