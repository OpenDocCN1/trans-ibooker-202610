# 7.3 Pullback of Multicovectors

The pullback of covariant tensors was briefly considered in Section 5.2. The corresponding theory for multicovectors is far richer.

Before proceeding, we pause to consider multi-index notation, which was discussed in Section 2.1 in the context of matrices. Let V be a vector space, let (h1,…,hm) be a basis for V, and let (θ1,…,θm) be its dual basis. For an integer 1 ≤ s ≤ m, let I = (i1,…,is) be a multi-index in ℐs,m, and let us denote

(h i1,…,h i s) by h I

and

θ i1 ∧ … ∧ θ i s by θ I.

In this notation, the unordered basis for A s(V) in Theorem 7.2.12(a) can be expressed concisely as {θ I : I ∈ ℐ s,m}, and the identity in Theorem 7.2.13(a) becomes

η = Σ I∈ℐ s,m η(h I) θ I.

If we order ℐ s,m in some fashion, for example, lexicographically, then the basis {θ I : I ∈ ℐ s,m} of Theorem 7.2.12(a) can be similarly ordered, yielding an ordered basis for Λ s(V), which we denote by

(θ I : I ∈ ℐ s,m).

Kronecker’s delta can be generalized to the multi-index setting. Let J = (j1,…,js) be another multi-index in ℐ s,m, and define

δ J I = { 1 if I = J 0 if I ≠ J. }

Then the conditional identity in Theorem 7.2.9 is simply θ J(h I) = δ J I. As discussed in Appendix A, the complement of the multi-index (i) in ℐ 1,m is

(i) c = (1,…,î,…,m) = (1,…,i−1,i+1,…,m)

for i = 1,…,m. In this notation, the multicovectors comprising the basis in Theorem 7.2.12(e) can be expressed as η i = (−1) i−1 θ (i) c. We will find multi-index notation of great utility in what follows.

Let V and W be vector spaces, and let A : V ⟶ W be a linear map. Pullback by A (for multicovectors) is the family of linear maps

A* : Λ s(W) ⟶ Λ s(V)

defined for s ≥ 1 by

A*(η)(v1,…,vs) = η(A(v1),…,A(vs)) (7.3.1)
