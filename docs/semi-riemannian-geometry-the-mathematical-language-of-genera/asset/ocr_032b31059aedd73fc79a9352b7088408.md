**Proof.** We have

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_X(f)(p) &= X(f)(p) &&\text{[(16.4.1)]}\\
&= X_p(f) &&\text{[(15.1.2)]}\\
&= d_p(f)(X_p) &&\text{[Th 14.5.5]}\\
&= d_{\Phi_p(0)}(f)\left(\frac{d\Phi_p}{dt}(0)\right) &&\text{[(16.10.1)]}\\
&= \frac{d(f\circ\Phi_p)}{dt}(0) &&\text{[Th 14.7.4(b)]}\\
&= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{(f\circ\Phi_p)(t)-(f\circ\Phi_p)(0)}{t}\\
&= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{(f\circ\Phi_t)(p)-f(p)}{t} &&\text{[(16.10.3)]}\\
&= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{(\Phi_t)^*(f)(p)-f(p)}{t}. &&\text{[(15.9.2)]}
\end{aligned}$$

# **Theorem 16.10.5 (Lie Derivative of Vector Field).**

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold, let $X,Y$ be vector fields in $\mathfrak{X}(M)$, and let $\Phi:D\to M$ be the maximal flow of $X$. Then

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_X(Y)_p &= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{d\Phi_t(p)(\Phi_{-t})_*(Y_{\Phi_t(p)})-Y_p}{t}\\
&= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{(\Phi_t)^*(Y)_p-Y_p}{t} && (16.10.4)\\
&= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{Y_p-(\Phi_{-t})_*(Y)_p}{t}.
\end{aligned}$$

for all $p$ in $M$.

**Proof.** We begin by showing that the three right-hand sides of (16.10.4) are equal. It follows from

$$\begin{aligned}
d\Phi_t(p)(\Phi_{-t})_*(Y_{\Phi_t(p)}) &= (\Phi_{-t})_*(Y)_p &&\text{[Th 15.10.1, Th 16.10.3(d)]}\\
&= (\Phi_t)^*(Y)_p &&\text{[(15.10.2), Th 16.10.3(d)]}
\end{aligned}$$

that the first and second right-hand sides are equal, and also that

$$\begin{aligned}
\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{Y_p-(\Phi_t)^*(Y)_p}{t}
&=\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{Y_p-(\Phi_{-t})_*(Y)_p}{t}\\
&=\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{(\Phi_{-t})_*(Y)_p-Y_p}{-t}\\
&=\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{(\Phi_t)^*(Y)_p-Y_p}{t},
\end{aligned}$$

hence the second and third right-hand sides are equal.
