# Example 15.12.3 (Polar Coordinates).

Let $(r,s)$ be standard coordinates on $\mathbb{R}^2$, and let $(\rho,\phi)$ be polar coordinates.

$$U=\{(\rho,\phi)\in\mathbb{R}^2:\rho>0,\ 0\leq\phi<2\pi\}.$$

Let $F:U\to\mathbb{R}^2$ be the smooth map given by

$$F(\rho,\phi)=(\rho\cos(\phi),\rho\sin(\phi)),$$

and consider the tensor field

$$\mathbf{e}_{(r,s)}=d(r)\otimes d(r)+d(s)\otimes d(s)$$

in $T^0_2(\mathbb{R}^2)$. Setting $(U,(x^i))=(U,(\rho,\phi))$ and $(V,(y^j))=(\mathbb{R}^2,(r,s))$ in Theorem 15.12.2, and observing that $\mathbf{e}_{11}\circ F=\mathbf{e}_{22}\circ F=1$ and $\mathbf{e}_{12}\circ F=\mathbf{e}_{21}\circ F=0$, yields

$$\begin{aligned}
F^*(\mathbf e)_{(\rho,\phi)}={}&\left(\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\rho}+\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\rho}\right)d(\rho)\otimes d(\rho)\\
&+\left(\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\phi}+\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\phi}\right)d(\rho)\otimes d(\phi)\\
&+\left(\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\rho}+\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\rho}\right)d(\phi)\otimes d(\rho)\\
&+\left(\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(r\circ F)}{\partial\phi}+\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(s\circ F)}{\partial\phi}\right)d(\phi)\otimes d(\phi)\\
={}&\left(\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\rho}+\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\rho}\right)d(\rho)\otimes d(\rho)\\
&+\left(\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\phi}+\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\rho}\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\phi}\right)d(\rho)\otimes d(\phi)\\
&+\left(\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\rho}+\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\rho}\right)d(\phi)\otimes d(\rho)\\
&+\left(\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(\rho\cos(\phi))}{\partial\phi}+\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\phi}\frac{\partial(\rho\sin(\phi))}{\partial\phi}\right)d(\phi)\otimes d(\phi)\\
={}&[\cos^2(\phi)+\sin^2(\phi)]d(\rho)\otimes d(\rho)\\
&+[-\rho\cos(\phi)\sin(\phi)+\rho\sin(\phi)\cos(\phi)]d(\rho)\otimes d(\phi)\\
&+[-\rho\sin(\phi)\cos(\phi)+\rho\cos(\phi)\sin(\phi)]d(\phi)\otimes d(\rho)\\
&+[\rho^2\sin^2(\phi)+\rho^2\cos^2(\phi)]d(\phi)\otimes d(\phi)\\
={}&d(\rho)\otimes d(\rho)+\rho^2d(\phi)\otimes d(\phi).
\end{aligned}$$
