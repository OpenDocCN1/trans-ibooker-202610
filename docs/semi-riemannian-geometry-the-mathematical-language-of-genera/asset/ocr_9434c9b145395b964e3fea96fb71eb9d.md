# Theorem 7.2.10. Let V be a vector space, let η¹,...,η^s be covectors in V*, and let σ be a permutation in S_s. Then

# Theorem 7.2.11. Let V be a vector space, let H = (h1,...,hm) and F be bases for V, and let (φ¹,...,φ^m) be the dual basis corresponding to F. Then
