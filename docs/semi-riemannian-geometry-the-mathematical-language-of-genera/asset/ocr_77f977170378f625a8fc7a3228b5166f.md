# 20.1 Hodge Star

Let $(M, \mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{O})$ be an oriented semi-Riemannian $m$-manifold of index $\nu$, and let $\Omega_M$ be its volume form. Hodge star is the family of linear maps

```
$\star : \Lambda^s(M) \longrightarrow \Lambda^{m-s}(M)$
```

defined for $0 \le s \le m$ that is the manifold version of Hodge star for scalar product spaces, as discussed in Section 8.5. Thus, for each form $\omega$ in $\Lambda^s(M)$, we have a unique form $\star(\omega)$ in $\Lambda^{m-s}(M)$ such that

```
$\omega \wedge \xi = \langle \star(\omega), \xi \rangle^{\Lambda} \Omega_M$
```

for all forms $\xi$ in $\Lambda^{m-s}(M)$.

# Theorem 20.1.1. Let $(M, \mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{O})$ be an oriented semi-Riemannian $m$-manifold of index $\nu$, let $\Omega_M$ be its volume form, and let $\omega, \xi$ be forms in $\Lambda^s(M)$. Then:

+   (a) $\star(1_M) = (-1)^\nu \Omega_M$.
+   (b) $\star(\Omega_M) = 1_M$.
+   (c) $\star^2(\omega) = (-1)^{s(m-s)+\nu} \omega$.
+   (d) $\omega \wedge \star(\xi) = (-1)^\nu \langle \omega, \xi \rangle^{\Lambda} \Omega_M$.

Proof. This is the manifold version of Theorem 8.5.3.

# Theorem 20.1.2. Let $(M, \mathfrak{g}, \mathfrak{O})$ be an oriented semi-Riemannian $m$-manifold of index $\nu$, let $\Omega_M$ be its volume form, and let $X$ be a vector field in $\mathfrak{X}(M)$. Then

```
$i_X(\Omega_M) = (-1)^\nu \star(X^\flat)$.
```
