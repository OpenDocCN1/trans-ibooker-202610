## ### Theorem 19.8.5 (Symmetries of Riemann Curvature Tensor).

If $(M,g)$ is a semi-Riemannian manifold and $X,Y,Z,W$ are vector fields in $\mathfrak{X}(M)$, then $\mathcal{R}$ satisfies the following symmetries:

- [S1] $\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,W)=-\mathcal{R}(Y,X,Z,W)$.
- [S2] $\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,W)=-\mathcal{R}(X,Y,W,Z)$.
- [S3] $\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,W)=\mathcal{R}(Z,W,X,Y)$.
- [S4] $\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,W)+\mathcal{R}(Y,Z,X,W)+\mathcal{R}(Z,X,Y,W)=0$.

**Proof.** [S1]: This follows from Theorem 18.7.1(a).

[S2]: Since

$$\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z+W,Z+W)$$
$$=\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,Z)+\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,W)+\mathcal{R}(X,Y,W,Z)+\mathcal{R}(X,Y,W,W),$$

it suffices to show that $\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,Z)=0$ for all vector fields $X,Y,Z$ in $\mathfrak{X}(M)$. Using (19.3.1) gives

$$\langle\nabla_X(\nabla_Y(Z)),Z\rangle=X(\langle\nabla_Y(Z),Z\rangle)-\langle\nabla_Y(Z),\nabla_X(Z)\rangle$$

$$\langle\nabla_Y(\nabla_X(Z)),Z\rangle=Y(\langle\nabla_X(Z),Z\rangle)-\langle\nabla_X(Z),\nabla_Y(Z)\rangle$$

$$\langle\nabla_Y(Z),Z\rangle=\frac{1}{2}Y(\langle Z,Z\rangle)$$

$$\langle\nabla_X(Z),Z\rangle=\frac{1}{2}X(\langle Z,Z\rangle)$$

$$\langle\nabla_{[X,Y]}(Z),Z\rangle=\frac{1}{2}[X,Y](\langle Z,Z\rangle),$$

hence

$$\mathcal{R}(X,Y,Z,Z)=\langle\mathcal{R}(X,Y)Z,Z\rangle$$
$$=\langle\nabla_X(\nabla_Y(Z))-\nabla_Y(\nabla_X(Z))-\nabla_{[X,Y]}(Z),Z\rangle$$
$$=\langle\nabla_X(\nabla_Y(Z)),Z\rangle-\langle\nabla_Y(\nabla_X(Z)),Z\rangle-\langle\nabla_{[X,Y]}(Z),Z\rangle$$
$$=X(\langle\nabla_Y(Z),Z\rangle)-Y(\langle\nabla_X(Z),Z\rangle)-\langle\nabla_{[X,Y]}(Z),Z\rangle$$
$$=\frac{1}{2}\{X(Y(\langle Z,Z\rangle))-Y(X(\langle Z,Z\rangle))-[X,Y](\langle Z,Z\rangle)\}=0.$$

[S4]: This follows from Theorem 19.8.3(b).

[S3]: This follows from [S1], [S2], [S4], and Theorem 6.6.1.

Let $(M,g)$ be a semi-Riemannian $m$-manifold of index $\nu$. We say that $M$ is flat if for every point $p$ in $M$, there is an isometry $F_p:(V,g|_V)\to(U,\tilde g|_U)$, where $V$ is a neighborhood of $p$ in $M$, and $U$ is an open set in $\mathbb R^m_\nu=(\mathbb R^m,\tilde g)$. # **Theorem 19.8.9 (Riemann’s Theorem).**

Let $(M,g)$ be a semi-Riemannian manifold, and let $\mathcal R$ be the Riemann curvature tensor. Then $\mathcal R$ has constant value $0$ if and only if $M$ is flat.

*Proof.* Suppose $M$ has dimension $m$ and index $\nu$.

$(\Rightarrow)$: Let $p$ be a point in $M$, let $V$ be a normal neighborhood of $p$ in $M$, and let $\exp_p|_{\widetilde V}:\widetilde V\longrightarrow V$ be the corresponding diffeomorphism. Since $(T_p(M),g_p)$ and $(M,g)$ are semi-Riemannian $m$-manifolds of index $\nu$, by Theorem 19.1.2, so are $\widetilde V$ and $V$. We claim that $\exp_p|_{\widetilde V}$ is an isometry.

Let $v,w_1,w_2$ be vectors in $\widetilde V\subseteq\mathcal E_p$, and let $\rho_{p,v}(r)=\exp_p|_{\widetilde V}(rv):[0,1]\longrightarrow M$ be the radial geodesic with starting point $p$ and initial velocity $v$. By Theorem 18.5.1, there are parallel vector fields $\mathcal J_i$ in $\mathcal X_M(\rho_{p,v})$ such that $\mathcal J_i(0)=w_i$ for $i=1,2$. Let us define vector fields $\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i$ in $\mathcal X_M(\rho_{p,v})$ by $\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i(r)=r\mathcal J_i(r)$. Since $\nabla\mathcal J_i/dr$ has constant value $0$, we have

$$\frac{\nabla\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i}{dr}(r)=\mathcal J_i(r)+r\frac{\nabla\mathcal J_i}{dr}(r)=\mathcal J_i(r),$$

hence

$$\frac{\nabla^2\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i}{dr^2}(r)=\frac{\nabla\mathcal J_i}{dr}(r)=0$$

for all $r$ in $[0,1]$. By Theorem 19.8.2, the Riemann curvature tensor $\mathcal R$ has constant (real number) value $0$ if and only if the Riemann curvature tensor $R$ has constant (vector) value $0$. It follows that $\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i$ satisfies Jacobi’s equation (18.11.1) and is therefore a Jacobi field. Let us observe that $\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i(0)=0$ and $(\nabla\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i/dr)(0)=w_i$. By Theorem 18.11.1, $\widetilde{\mathcal J}_i$ is the unique Jacobi field in $\mathcal X_M(\rho_{p,v})$ satisfying the preceding two identities.
