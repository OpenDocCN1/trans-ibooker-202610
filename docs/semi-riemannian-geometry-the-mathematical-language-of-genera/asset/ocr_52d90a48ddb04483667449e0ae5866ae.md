Let \((U,(x^i))\) be a chart on \(M\), let \(1\le i_1<\cdots<i_r\le m\) be integers, and let

\[
d(x^{i_1})\wedge\cdots\wedge d(x^{i_s}):U\longrightarrow \Lambda^s(U)
\]

be the map defined by the assignment

\[
p\longmapsto d_p(x^{i_1})\wedge\cdots\wedge d_p(x^{i_s})
\]

for all \(p\) in \(U\). Suppose \(\omega\) is a form in \(\Lambda^s(M)\). Then \(\omega|_U\) can be expressed as

\[
\omega|_U=\sum_{1\le i_1<\cdots<i_s\le m}\alpha_{i_1,\ldots,i_s}\,d(x^{i_1})\wedge\cdots\wedge d(x^{i_s}),\tag{15.8.4}
\]

where the \(\alpha_{i_1,\ldots,i_s}\) are uniquely determined functions in \(C^\infty(U)\), called the **components of \(\omega\)** with respect to \((U,(x^i))\). For brevity, we denote \(\omega|_U\) by \(\omega\).

The right-hand side of (15.8.4) is said to express \(\omega\) in **local coordinates** with respect to \((U,(x^i))\).

# **Theorem 15.8.1.**

Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold, let \(\omega,\omega^1,\omega^2\) and \(\xi,\xi^1,\xi^2\) be forms in \(\Lambda^s(M)\) and \(\Lambda^{s'}(M)\), respectively, and let \(f\) be a function in \(C^\infty(M)\). Then:

(a) \((\omega^1+\omega^2)\wedge\xi=\omega^1\wedge\xi+\omega^2\wedge\xi.\)

(b) \(\omega\wedge(\xi^1+\xi^2)=\omega\wedge\xi^1+\omega\wedge\xi^2.\)

(c) \((f\omega)\wedge\xi=f(\omega\wedge\xi)=\omega\wedge(f\xi)\).

*Proof.* This is the manifold version of Theorem 7.2.2.

# **Theorem 15.8.2.**

Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold, and let \(\omega\) and \(\xi\) be forms in \(\Lambda^s(M)\) and \(\Lambda^{s'}(M)\), respectively. Then:

(a) \(\omega\wedge\xi=(-1)^{ss'}\xi\wedge\omega.\)

(b) If \(s=s'=1\), then \(\omega\wedge\xi=-\xi\wedge\omega.\)

(c) If \(s=1\), then \(\omega\wedge\omega=0.\)

*Proof.* This is the manifold version of Theorem 7.2.3.

# **Theorem 15.8.3.**

Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold, let \(\omega^1,\ldots,\omega^s\) be covector fields in \(\mathcal X^*(M)\), and let \(X_1,\ldots,X_s\) be vector fields in \(\mathcal X(M)\). Then:

(a)

\[
\omega^1\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega^s(X_1,\ldots,X_s)=
\det\begin{pmatrix}
\omega^1(X_1)&\cdots&\omega^1(X_s)\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
\omega^s(X_1)&\cdots&\omega^s(X_s)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(b) If \(\omega^i=\omega^j\) for some \(1\le i<j\le s\), then \(\omega^1\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega^s=0\).

*Proof.* This is the manifold version of Theorem 7.2.6 combined with the manifold version of Theorem 7.2.8.
