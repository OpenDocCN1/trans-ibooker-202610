For the second term, Theorem 15.4.7 and Theorem 16.4.7 give
\[\mathcal{L}_X\big(d(x^{i_l})\big)=d(\alpha^{i_l})=\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\partial\alpha^{i_l}}{\partial x^{k}}d(x^{k}),\]

hence
\[\sum_{l=1}^{r}\mathcal{A}\Big(d(x^{i_1}),\dots,\mathcal{L}_X\big(d(x^{i_l})\big),\dots,d(x^{i_r}),\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_1}},\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_s}}\Big)\]
\[=\sum_{l=1}^{r}\mathcal{A}\Big(d(x^{i_1}),\dots,\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\partial\alpha^{i_l}}{\partial x^{k}}d(x^{k}),\dots,d(x^{i_r}),\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_1}},\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_s}}\Big)\]
\[=\sum_{l=1}^{r}\Big\{\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\partial\alpha^{i_l}}{\partial x^{k}}\mathcal{A}\Big(d(x^{i_1}),\dots,d(x^{k}),\dots,d(x^{i_r}),\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_1}},\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_s}}\Big)\Big\}\tag{16.4.4}\]
\[=\sum_{l=1}^{r}\Big(\sum_{k=1}^{m}\mathcal{A}^{i_1\dots i_{l-1}\,k\,i_{l+1}\dots i_r}_{j_1\dots j_s}\frac{\partial\alpha^{i_l}}{\partial x^{k}}\Big).\]

For the third term,
\[\left[X,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_l}}\right]=\sum_{k=1}^{m}\left[\alpha^{k}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{k}},\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_l}}\right]\qquad[\text{Th 15.3.2(b)}]\]
\[=\sum_{k=1}^{m}\left(\alpha^{k}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{k}},\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_l}}\right]-\frac{\partial\alpha^{k}}{\partial x^{j_l}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{k}}\right)\qquad[\text{Th 15.3.2(d)}]\]
\[=-\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\partial\alpha^{k}}{\partial x^{j_l}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{k}},\qquad[\text{Th 15.3.3(b)}]\]

hence
\[\sum_{l=1}^{s}\mathcal{A}\Big(d(x^{i_1}),\dots,d(x^{i_r}),\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_1}},\dots,\left[X,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_l}}\right],\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_s}}\Big)\]
\[=\sum_{l=1}^{s}\mathcal{A}\Big(d(x^{i_1}),\dots,d(x^{i_r}),\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_1}},\dots,-\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\partial\alpha^{k}}{\partial x^{j_l}}\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{k}},\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_s}}\Big)\]
\[=-\sum_{l=1}^{s}\Big\{\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{\partial\alpha^{k}}{\partial x^{j_l}}\mathcal{A}\Big(d(x^{i_1}),\dots,d(x^{i_r}),\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_1}},\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_k}},\dots,\frac{\partial}{\partial x^{j_s}}\Big)\Big\}\]
\[=-\sum_{l=1}^{s}\Big(\sum_{k=1}^{m}\mathcal{A}^{i_1\dots i_r}_{j_1\dots j_{l-1}\,k\,j_{l+1}\dots j_s}\frac{\partial\alpha^{k}}{\partial x^{j_l}}\Big).\tag{16.4.5}\]

Substituting (16.4.3)–(16.4.5) into (16.4.2) gives the result.
