The **interior of $S$ in $X$** is denoted by $\operatorname{int}_X(S)$ and defined to be the union of all open sets in $X$ contained in $S$. The **exterior of $S$ in $X$** is denoted by $\operatorname{ext}_X(S)$ and defined to be the union of all open sets in $X$ contained in $X \setminus S$. The **boundary of $S$ in $X$** is denoted by $\operatorname{bd}_X(S)$ and defined to be the set of all points in $X$ that are neither in $\operatorname{int}_X(S)$ nor in $\operatorname{ext}_X(S)$. Thus, $X$ is the disjoint union

$X = \operatorname{int}_X(S) \cup \operatorname{ext}_X(S) \cup \operatorname{bd}_X(S)$.

The **closure of $S$ in $X$** is denoted by $\operatorname{cl}_X(S)$ and defined to be the intersection of all closed sets in $X$ containing $S$.
