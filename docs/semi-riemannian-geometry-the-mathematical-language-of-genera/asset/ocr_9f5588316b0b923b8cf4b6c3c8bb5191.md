**Theorem 2.2.5.** Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces, let $\mathcal H$ and $\mathcal F$ be respective bases, and let $A:V\to W$ be a linear isomorphism. Then:

+   (a) $$[A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal F}=[\operatorname{id}_W]_{A(\mathcal H)}^{\mathcal F}$$
+   
+   (b) $$[A^{-1}]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal H}=\left([A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal F}\right)^{-1}.$$

**Remark.** By Theorem 1.1.13(a), $A(\mathcal H)$ is a basis for $W$, so the assertion in part (a) makes sense.

**Proof.** (a): Let $\mathcal H=(h_1,\ldots,h_m)$, so that

$$[A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal F}=\left[\,[A(h_1)]_{\mathcal F}\quad\cdots\quad[A(h_m)]_{\mathcal F}\,\right] = [\operatorname{id}_W]_{A(\mathcal H)}^{\mathcal F}.$$

(b): By Theorem 2.2.2(c),

$$I_m=[\operatorname{id}_V]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal H}=[A^{-1}\circ A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal H}=[A^{-1}]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal H}[A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal F},$$

from which the result follows. □

**Theorem 2.2.6 (Change of Basis).** Let $V$ be a vector space, let $\mathcal H$ and $\mathcal F$ be bases for $V$, and let $A:V\to V$ be a linear map. Then

$$[A]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal F}=\left([\operatorname{id}_V]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal H}\right)^{-1}[A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal H}[\operatorname{id}_V]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal H}.$$

**Proof.** By Theorem 2.2.2(c),

$$[A]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal F}=[\operatorname{id}_V\circ A\circ\operatorname{id}_V]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal F}=[\operatorname{id}_V]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal F}[A]_{\mathcal H}^{\mathcal H}[\operatorname{id}_V]_{\mathcal F}^{\mathcal H}.$$

The result now follows from Theorem 2.2.5(b). □

**Theorem 2.2.7.** Let $V$ be a vector space, let $\mathcal H$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal H}$ be bases for $V$, let $\Theta$ and $\widetilde{\Theta}$ be the corresponding dual bases, and let $A:V\to V$ be a linear map. Then

$$[A^*]_{\widetilde{\Theta}}^{\Theta}=\left([A]_{\mathcal H}^{\widetilde{\mathcal H}}\right)^T,$$

where $A^*$ is the pullback by $A$.
