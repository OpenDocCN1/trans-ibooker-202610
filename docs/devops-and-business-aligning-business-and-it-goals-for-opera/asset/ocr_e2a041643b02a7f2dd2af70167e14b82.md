The metrics described in this chapter provide a base set of performance benchmarks that bridge the gap between technological and business goals. All of them, although technical or procedural in nature, support business-centric KPIs such as revenue and cost control. However, as an organization, there are many more core metrics that you should collect across different functional groups (which we discuss more in Chapter 6) that are essential to supplement high-level KPIs.

The bottom line is this: if you’re going through DevOps transformation, you need metrics not only to make sure you’re changing in the correct direction, but also to help with acquiring buy-in from different groups and aligning KPIs to work toward the shared goals. Without metrics, you won’t have any way of knowing whether your initiatives are beneficial to the adopter group or organization as a whole.
