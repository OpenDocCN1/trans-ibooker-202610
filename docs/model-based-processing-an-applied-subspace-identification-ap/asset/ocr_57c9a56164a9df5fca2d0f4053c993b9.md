As pointed out by Ljung and Soderstrom [2, 24], it is important to realize that the EKF is suboptimal as a parameter estimator compared to the recursive prediction error (RPE) method based on the Gauss–Newton (stochastic) descent algorithm (see Section 5.5). Comparing the processors in this context, we see that if the gradient term [∇_θ K(Θ)]e(t) is incorporated into the EKF (add this term to A_θ), its convergence will be improved approaching the performance of the RPE algorithm (see Ljung [24] for details).
