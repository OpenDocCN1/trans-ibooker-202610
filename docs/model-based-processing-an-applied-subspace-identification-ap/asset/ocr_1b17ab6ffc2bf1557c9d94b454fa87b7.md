Substituting these relations (Eqs. (8.70), (8.80), and (8.81)) into Eq. (8.78), and assuming a Markovian process as before, we obtain

$$\Pr[\Theta_M\mid T_M]=\bigl(\Pr[\tau_m\mid\Theta(\tau_m)]\times\Pr[T_{M-1}\mid\Theta_{M-1}]\bigr)\\\times\frac{\Pr[\Theta(\tau_m)\mid\Theta(\tau_{m-1})]\times\Pr[\Theta_{M-1}]}{\Pr[\tau_m\mid\tau_{m-1}]\times\Pr[T_{M-1}]}$$

(8.82)

Now grouping the terms, the desired posterior distribution becomes

$$\Pr[\Theta_M\mid T_M]=\left(\frac{\Pr[\tau_m\mid\Theta(\tau_m)]\times\Pr[\Theta(\tau_m)\mid\Theta(\tau_{m-1})]}{\Pr[\tau_m\mid\tau_{m-1}]}\right)\\\times\left(\frac{\Pr[T_{M-1}\mid\Theta_{M-1}]\times\Pr[\Theta_{M-1}]}{\Pr[T_{M-1}]}\right)$$

(8.83)

or simply (replacing $\tau_m\to T_M$)

$$\Pr[\Theta_m\mid T_m]=W(\tau_m)\times\Pr[\Theta_{m-1}\mid T_{m-1}]$$

(8.84)

where

$$W(\tau_m):=\frac{\Pr[\tau_m\mid\Theta(\tau_m)]\times\Pr[\Theta(\tau_m)\mid\Theta(\tau_{m-1})]}{\Pr[\tau_m\mid\tau_{m-1}]}$$

(8.85)

which is the sequential Bayesian solution for the dynamic parametric model (random walk/constant). A particle filter is an implementation of this recursion [39, 40].

# 8.3.8  Particle Filter for Fission Processes

Particle filtering is a technique that evolves from the “importance sampling” approach to statistical sampling of data. The key idea is to select particles (or parameters in our problem) from the regions of highest probabilities or equivalently regions of highest importance. Once the resulting importance weight is determined, the desired posterior distribution is approximated by a nonparametric probability mass function (PMF) as

$$\hat{\Pr}[\Theta_M\mid T_M]=\sum_i w_i(\tau_m)\,\delta(\Theta(\tau_m)-\Theta_i(\tau_m))$$

(8.86)

where $\mathcal{W}_i$ is the normalized weighting function given as the ratio of the posterior at interarrival time $\tau_m$ and the designed importance distribution $q$ as

$$W_i(\tau_m):=\frac{\Pr[\Theta(\tau_m)\mid T_m]}{q[\Theta(\tau_m)\mid T_m]}=\frac{\Pr[\tau_m\mid\Theta(\tau_m)]}{q[\tau_m\mid\Theta(\tau_m)]}\\\times\frac{\Pr[\Theta(\tau_m)]}{q[\Theta(\tau_m)\mid\Theta_{m-1},\tau_m]}$$

(8.87)
