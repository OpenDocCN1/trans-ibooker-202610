# 8.5.4 Summary

We have developed a model-based approach to processing a windowed chirp and an FSK signal in noisy data. We applied the UKF technique of Section 4.3 to perform the MBID of the critical chirp and FSK signal parameters from noisy synthesized data and demonstrated that the MBID-approach could be used to extract these signals from noisy measurements.
