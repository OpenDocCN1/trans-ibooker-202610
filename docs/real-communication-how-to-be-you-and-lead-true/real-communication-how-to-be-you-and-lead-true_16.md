### 第 10 章 面对现实

大约 30 年前，当我在澳大利亚国家银行工作时，大约晚上 11 点，我即将结束班次。我正在进行一些例行系统检查，不小心输入了错误的命令，导致银行整个销售点和 ATM 系统开始关闭。一切都变得慢动作，我的心开始怦怦直跳。

我知道在呼叫中心被愤怒客户的电话淹没之前，我只有几分钟时间，这些客户无法取款。我也知道其他计算机操作员会开始在监控的屏幕上收到红色警报消息。

我花了几分钟停止咒骂，开始出汗，然后采取行动。我立即打电话给呼叫中心经理说，‘我搞砸了，让系统瘫痪了。你即将被电话淹没，但大约 15 分钟后系统应该会恢复正常。’

然后我跑到经理 Rohan 的办公室，边跑边对同事们大喊，说我错误地让全国系统瘫痪了。我立刻告诉经理，‘我犯了一个大错误’。然后我解释了我做了什么。我告诉他，我已经通知了呼叫中心和所有受我大搞砸影响的人。

系统恢复了，Rohan 令人惊讶地似乎很平静。事实上，他走过来感谢我承担全部责任，并迅速采取行动。

值得注意的是，我与 Rohan 有着良好的工作关系，所以我毫不犹豫地告诉他发生了什么。他是一位真诚的领导者，创造了一种问责文化，而不是指责和羞耻的文化。

关键是，失误会发生。它们是让我们变得真实的一部分。它们也是学习的机会。

当你愿意在错误发生时迅速承担全部责任和问责时，人们往往会做出更好的反应。真诚的领导力是有勇气和信心接受错误并真诚道歉。

> _我们如何回应和处理失误，才是当今世界重要的事情。_

### 现实世界中的问题

Miranda Kovacic 是 ANZ 的员工体验领域负责人。她与我分享了她团队中某人犯的一个错误：向 11,800 名 ANZ 员工发送了一封电子邮件，称他们将停止备份电子邮件的系统。这引起了很多担忧，电话、电子邮件和短信很快涌向 Miranda。

Miranda 知道她必须采取行动，觉得自己有三个选择。她可以忽略这封电子邮件，但她知道这不是很好的领导力；她可以撤回电子邮件，但这也不是一个好信号；或者她可以发送一封跟进邮件。在她的沟通顾问的帮助下，她发送了以下电子邮件：

最近我们向您发送了一封关于撤销您个人存储表的电子邮件。坦白说，这有点不合时宜。正如人们所指出的，这封更新来自员工体验领域，这颇具讽刺意味。

我写信给您——以及所有收到那封电子邮件的人——是因为我和我的团队认识到，那种电子邮件并不特别有帮助。

我们希望您知道，我们正在努力引入人本设计，来自 ANZ 首席执行官 Shayne Elliott：

> 哇——谢谢你，Miranda 和团队——这是一个多么棒的态度，愿意接受反馈并承诺改进。
> 我赞扬你的行动和信息。
> 谢谢！
> Shayne

Miranda 告诉我，她最初的反应是，‘哇，他怎么回应得这么快？’她的第二个反应是，她很高兴他花时间亲自且积极地回应。

Miranda 告诉我，她希望这封电子邮件传达 ANZ 正在进行的文化变革。直到今天，她也不知道是她团队中的哪个人发送了原始电子邮件——因为对她来说这不重要。对她来说重要的是‘我们如何回应’。

当你在一个错误会导致负面后果的文化中工作时，人们会非常不愿意承认错误。他们要么试图隐瞒发生的事情，要么编造借口——而不是迅速承认并尝试修复它们。

### 处理借口

还记得 2017 年那个视频吗，一名男子在联合航空航班超售后被强行拖下飞机？David Dao，一名 69 岁的肯塔基州医生，是该航班上的乘客，当时联合航空要求志愿者放弃座位。航空公司表示需要座位来安排额外的机组人员。两名乘客放弃了座位，但当要求 Dao 时，他拒绝了。

于是航空警察将 Dao 从座位上拖走，沿过道拖出。其他乘客拍摄了视频，视频在社交媒体上疯传。Dao 在事件中脑震荡，鼻子骨折，两颗牙齿脱落。

公司的第一份公开声明中写道，‘我们为超售情况道歉’，但没有提及 Dao 博士或视频。

当天晚些时候，首席执行官 Oscar Munoz 发表声明，称此事是‘一件令人不安的事件’。他向飞机上的其他乘客道歉，但没有提到 Dao 被强行拖走。Munoz 说：

> 这对我们在联合航空的每个人来说都是一件令人不安的事件。我为不得不重新安排这些乘客座位而道歉。我们的团队正以紧迫感行动，与当局合作，并对所发生的事情进行详细审查。我们也正在联系这位乘客，直接与他交谈，进一步解决和处理此事。

当晚，Munoz 给联合航空员工的一封信被发送，后来被泄露并公开。在信中，他告诉员工他支持他们，并概述了航班情况，描述了工作人员如何先寻求志愿者放弃座位，然后进行‘非自愿拒绝登机流程’，即工作人员挑选谁需要离开飞机，在此案例中还提供了 1000 美元。

> 我无法过分强调首席执行官或领导者在创造一种文化方面的重要性，在这种文化中，面对现实——无论是错误还是其他情况——都受到鼓励和奖励。

While offering excuses rather than apologies is often hugely tempting, blaming the victim in your excuses rarely ends well. In this case, the damage to brand, both personally to Munoz and to the company, would have been lessened if the last statement was the company’s first.

## — 真诚沟通 —

Authentic leadership and dealing with what’s real is not about plastic apologies—you know, the kinds of apologies that normally start with something like, ‘If anyone was offended by my comments then I apologise’. Dealing with mistakes authentically is all about genuine apologies and communicating very clearly what action will be taken to fix the problem. Some people will feel so wronged and outraged that no amount of apologising will suffice. But the vast majority of people will respond positively to a genuine, heartfelt apology, especially when it is followed by a clear path of action.

> Playing the blame game does more harm than good.

## #这可不是板球

In March 2018, the Australian men’s cricket team was touring South Africa on a test series. During the third test match, Cameron Bancroft, a relatively new member to the Australian team, was caught on camera rubbing the cricket ball with a small yellow object he had pulled out of his pocket. When Bancroft realised he had been seen, he hid the object down the front of his trousers.

When the umpires approached Bancroft and asked to see what was in his pocket, he responded by showing them the cloth used to clean his sunglasses, not the foreign object he had just placed down the front of his trousers.

At the press conference at the end of the day’s play, Bancroft was accompanied by Australia’s captain, Steve Smith. Both players acknowledged they had broken the rules by trying to alter the condition of the ball, but claimed they had used nothing more than adhesive tape with dirt stuck on it.

Both Smith and Bancroft seemed to underestimate the growing public reaction to what they had done. Bancroft was charged by the referee with a Level 2 offence, which included three demerit points and a fine of 75 per cent of his match payments. Smith was charged by the International Cricket Council with ‘conduct of a serious nature that is contrary to the spirit of the game’ and was banned for the next test match and forfeited 100 per cent of his match fee. Many fans in Australia—outraged by what they saw as straight-out cheating—thought more was needed.

It was not until five days later, after an investigation by Cricket Australia, that Bancroft admitted the foreign object was not just some adhesive tape, but actually sandpaper. As captain of the team, Smith admitted that he knew about the plan, which was hatched up during lunch by the ‘leadership group’. Smith refused to name who was involved in this group.

Still seeming not to have grasped the seriousness of the event, Smith admitted it was a mistake, but said he had no plans to stand down as captain. Resentment and anger among the cricketing community then grew even further.

Cricket Australia launched a preliminary investigation, which resulted in an announcement that Smith, David Warner (the other player involved and vice-captain of the team) and Bancroft had been charged with bringing the game into disrepute and would be suspended from playing the final test match of the series and sent home.

The result of the full investigation? David Warner was found to be the plan’s main instigator, instructing Bancroft on how to tamper with the ball. While on the field, Smith was found to have told Bancroft to hide the sandpaper in his trousers.

Warner was also found to have failed to prevent the plan being implemented, and to have misled match officials by concealing his knowledge of the plan and not voluntarily reporting his involvement. He received a 12-month suspension from all international and domestic cricket, with no consideration for team leadership positions in the future.

Smith was also found guilty of knowing about the plan and failing to take any action to prevent it. He misled match officials and made misleading public comments regarding the nature of the plan and who was involved. He received a 12-month suspension from all international and domestic cricket, with no consideration for team leadership positions until a minimum of 12 months after his suspension.

Bancroft also received a 9-month suspension from all international and domestic cricket, with no consideration for team leadership positions until a minimum of 12 months after the conclusion of his suspension.

## — 真诚沟通 —

The same day, the Board of Control for Cricket in India announced that neither Smith nor Warner would be able to play in the 2018 International Premier League. While in England, Somerset, where Bancroft had been recruited to play in the County Championship season, announced that he would no longer be playing for them.

Upon their return home to Australia, all three players held emotional press conferences, breaking down and apologising unashamedly for their actions. The seriousness of what they had done seemed to have finally sunk in.

As captain of the Australian cricket team, Smith took full responsibility, and said he had made a serious error in judgement, adding, ‘It was a failure of leadership, my leadership’. As well as apologising to his ‘teammates, to fans of cricket all over the world and to all Australians who are disappointed and angry,’ he specifically referred to the effect the incident had had on his parents. He added, ‘I know I will regret this for the rest of my life. I’m absolutely gutted. I hope in time I can earn back respect and forgiveness’.

Australia’s coach Darren Lehmann was cleared by the investigation of playing any part in the scandal. He said he was embarrassed and disappointed, but he would not resign. However, after viewing the press conference of the players, he announced that he would step down from his role as coach.

The commercial fallout of the ball-tampering scandal was unprecedented—for both Cricket Australia and the individual players concerned. One of Cricket Australia’s major sponsors, the Magellan Financial Group, terminated its three-year sponsorship agreement in response. The contract had two more seasons to run, and was estimated to be worth approximately AU$20 million.

On an individual sponsorship level for the players in question, LG announced that they would not renew their personal sponsorship of Warner. The sporting apparel company ASICS terminated their commercial arrangements with Warner and Bancroft. The Commonwealth Bank and Sanitarium both terminated their sponsorship of Smith.

> While the three players involved will most likely play cricket for Australia again, it is hard to see their reputation ever fully recovering from this incident.





有些人会认为，所受到的反应和罚款与罪行不相称。我建议，公众和澳大利亚板球协会（Cricket Australia）之所以反应如此激烈，是因为这些球员背叛了澳大利亚公众的信任。

这里有一个我们所有人都应该学到的重要教训。

> 作为领导者，我们的行为和决策始终会受到评判——而这伴随着代价。

这种代价可能是直接的、即时的财务成本，但你也必须考虑对品牌造成的整体损害。这种无形的成本很难挽回。

### 一次彻底的搞砸

我在上一章中介绍了凯特·休斯（Kate Hughes）。在担任皇家墨尔本理工大学（RMIT）现职之前，她是澳大利亚最大的一家电话和移动网络提供商的首席风险官。一天晚上，在晚间新闻期间，凯特正在电视直播中接受采访，谈论过去几个月中发生过多次的一起重大安全漏洞。

她所在的公司无意中泄露了一些客户的私人电话号码。这些客户中，有些人曾逃离家庭暴力，正躲避施暴的伴侣。

凯特回答采访者问题的方式有几个方面让我印象深刻。他们问的第一个问题是：“发生了什么？”

凯特回答说：“我们搞砸了。而且我们不是搞砸了一次，而是搞砸了好几次。”

接着，她谈到了这次泄露有多么不可接受。她谈到了自己如何与受影响的客户交谈，以及他们如何改变系统和流程，以确保此事不再发生。

第二天，我给凯特打电话，称赞她真诚的回应，尤其是关于“搞砸”的评论。结果发现，她的首席执行官当时不在国内，公司的媒体负责人认为凯特是面对媒体的最佳人选。

凯特告诉我，她同意接受采访有一个条件：她不会为所发生的事情找借口，因为“这次是一次彻底的搞砸”。媒体负责人看着她说：“好吧，但你能不能不要说‘彻底的’，也不要说‘搞砸’？”

> 面对现实并不是要拆除所有界限，而是要做出明智且有意识的选择。

凯特通过愿意面对现实并承认搞砸，展现了勇气。她与自己的个人价值观保持一致。她告诉我，她和丈夫（也是一名首席风险官）之间有一个约定，始终说实话。

### 你的脚步需要跟上你的嘴

处理丑闻通常需要从一次真诚且发自内心的道歉开始。取决于丑闻的程度或不当行为的数量，面对现实可能是一个长期的过程。加拿大最大的工程公司SNC-Lavalin几年前就经历了这种情况。

SNC-Lavalin成立于1911年，在50多个国家设有办事处，在160多个国家开展业务。从2011年到2013年，该公司经历了一系列腐败指控，包括：

- 2011年：一名副总裁因一系列腐败和金融欺诈指控被调查（他最终认罪）。
- 2012年：时任首席执行官皮埃尔·迪梅（Pierre Duhaime）辞职，9个月后被魁北克当局逮捕，指控包括欺诈、共谋欺诈和使用伪造文件。（撰写本文时，他的案件仍在法院审理中。）
- 2013年：前高管被指控向麦吉尔大学健康中心（McGill University Health Centre）首席执行官提供不当的2200万美元付款，与新医院建设的投标过程有关。该首席执行官在出庭受审前去世，但他的妻子因洗钱罪认罪。

SNC-Lavalin的一些高管还与利比亚穆阿迈尔·卡扎菲（Muammar Gaddafi）政权建立了密切关系，当政权垮台时，他们据称在利比亚银行留下了2300万美元，以及与卡扎菲家族本身腐败交易的证据。

这听起来很糟糕，事实也确实如此，但情况变得更糟了。2013年，由于与孟加拉国帕德玛大桥（Padma Bridge）项目相关的调查，世界银行将250家公司列入黑名单，禁止其参与全球项目投标。在这250家被列入黑名单的公司中，117家是加拿大公司，SNC-Lavalin及其附属合作伙伴占了117家中的115家。

毫不奇怪，该公司的股价在2016年1月跌至37美元的低点，而2011年初曾高达61美元。

在接受SNC-Lavalin诚信计划负责人帕特丽夏·阿莱恩（Patricia Alleyn）的采访时，她将那两年的事件描述为“丑闻的龙卷风”。最终，SNC-Lavalin的所有员工都觉得，他们因少数几名前高管的行为而受到牵连。他们不仅来自媒体和社交媒体的审查和批评，还来自朋友和家人的审查和批评——例如在后院烧烤和孩子们的足球比赛上。

在新设立的首席合规官（Chief Compliance Officer）职位的领导下，该公司转向国际法规，制定并实施了一个全面的道德与合规（Ethics & Compliance）计划。该计划在全公司范围内推广，包括修订后的行为准则、培训、热线和其他措施，覆盖每一名员工，并着重于透明度和做正确的事。SNC-Lavalin计划所坚持的高标准，得到了去年夏天透明国际（Transparency International）进行的一项评估的证明。透明国际（英国）是一个与政府和企业合作以帮助打击腐败的全球组织，在评估之后，他们指出SNC-Lavalin已采取行动，变得比许多同行更加透明，例如制定了一份关于游说和政治活动的年度报告，并在其网站上分享。

正如帕特丽夏向我解释的那样，真实的沟通和领导力并非体现在单一行动中；它需要持续且不断演变。

> 道歉并承担责任需要快速，但仅此还不够——你需要表明你正在采取行动，以确保此事不再发生。

作为诚信计划办公室（Integrity Program Office）负责人，帕特丽夏继续通过实施公司范围的举措来完善该计划，例如行为准则培训，员工在培训中会了解公司和行业内的各种真实案例，以帮助指导他们未来的行为和选择。

虽然这项培训在全公司范围内进行，但其他强制性培训则侧重于中层管理人员和业务发展人员，因为这里交付成果的压力可能更大，不当行为也最容易发生。这些有针对性的培训提高意识，即作为个人，这些人有责任做出道德选择，并最终对其负责。他们被提醒，面临风险的不仅是公司的声誉，还有他们自己的声誉。

公司文化支持员工报告任何他们认为未遵守行为准则的情况。为了忠实于其透明沟通的承诺，SNC-Lavalin会定期更新培训内容，将基于这些情况的内部案例纳入其中，以便培训与业务现状保持相关，并塑造潜在问题领域的行为。

员工，包括高管，也在根据其对行为准则的遵守情况进行评估，在某些情况下，因未达到标准而被解雇。公司还要求业务合作伙伴遵守行为准则，并已停止与那些行为不符合行为准则的组织进行交易。

帕特丽夏解释说，这些行动对于确保丑闻不再发生至关重要。她解释了法语谚语“vos pieds doivent suivre votre parole”，基本翻译为“你的脚步需要跟上你的嘴”。其他人会说“事实胜过雄辩”或“言行一致，不要光说不练”。无论怎么看，处理丑闻现实的关键在于承担责任，然后采取行动，并以透明和真实的方式进行沟通。这种方法的影响是，在2017年与员工开展确定公司价值观的练习后，诚信（Integrity）被选为四个核心价值观之一。

在寻求关于是否说实话的建议时，我们可以始终参考马克·吐温（Mark Twain）的智慧，他写道：“拿不准时，就说真话”。虽然原文中没有，但许多来源补充道：“这会让你的敌人困惑，让你的朋友惊叹”。

### 立即付诸实践（Get REAL now）

- 犯错后，摒弃敷衍的道歉，改为快速且真诚的道歉。
- 任何道歉之后都要跟上真诚的行动。
- 创造一种将错误视为学习机会的文化，让团队成员感到安全，敢于承认错误。
- 了解一旦信任失去，重建信任是多么困难。
