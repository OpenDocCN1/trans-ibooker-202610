# # Evolution of expectations

The communications on the day, however, were also swift and transparent
from the senior leaders at the bank. Anthony Healy, Chief Customer
Officer—Business & Private Banking, took to Twitter during the outage to
apologise for the outage via a personal video he filmed at home.

A few weeks later, when everything had been resolved and it was no longer
making news, Healy posted an article on LinkedIn that was noticeable for
its personal and transparent nature. It read:
