of my clients, who would be classified as gen Y, told me about being headhunted for a senior role, and insisting on meeting with the Executive General Manager, even though she would not be reporting to him. Businesses have no choice but to meet the evolving expectations of all their employees and customers, at all points of connection and communication, and regardless of their age or background, if they want to attract, retain and engage them.
