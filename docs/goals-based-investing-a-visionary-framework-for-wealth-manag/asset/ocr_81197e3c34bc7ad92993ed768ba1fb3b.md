# PUTTING THEORY INTO PRACTICE

HNW and UHNW families have multiple issues to contend with. Wealth advisors should consider cash-flow needs and estate-planning issues before developing an asset allocation strategy. They should evaluate the tax consequences and asset locations, and may need to determine how to fund charitable activities. These issues are interrelated.
