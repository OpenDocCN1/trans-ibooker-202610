The Investments & Wealth Institute later introduced the Certified Private Wealth Advisor (CPWA) program to hone the skills of advisors serving HNW investors, and the Retirement Management Analyst (RMA) program to help advisors serving retirees. The CFA Institute offers the CFA certificate for members who pass a comprehensive program designed to help advisors in portfolio management. CAIA introduced the CAIA Charter to provide advanced education regarding the role and use of alternative investments. The FPA administers the Certified Financial Planning (CFP) designation.
