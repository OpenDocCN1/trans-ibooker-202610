# FAMILY GOALS

HNW and UHNW families are typically solving for multiple goals simultaneously and are not focused solely on maximizing returns. Their goals may include preserving wealth, transferring wealth efficiently, and funding causes that matter to the family. A family’s wealth may determine priorities, and it may be instructive to review the hierarchy of financial needs (Figure 9.3).
