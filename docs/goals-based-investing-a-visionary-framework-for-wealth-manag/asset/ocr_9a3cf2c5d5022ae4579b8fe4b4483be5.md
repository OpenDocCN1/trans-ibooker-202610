Sustainable investing presents an opportunity for advisors to demonstrate their value as educators and knowledgeable sources of information. Investors want help in understanding these strategies and better aligning their values and portfolios. As previously mentioned, women, millennials, Gen Z, and many HNW families care deeply about sustainable investing, and they need help navigating the confusing terminology and vast differences between one strategy and the next.

Wealth advisors need to become better versed in these strategies. They need to learn how to evaluate available options and help their clients align their portfolios with their purpose and values. In short, advisors need to help their clients navigate the various types of strategies and identify the most appropriate solution(s).

HNW investors who are interested in learning more about sustainable investing, or who want to align purpose and portfolio, may want to select a wealth advisor who understands and incorporates sustainable investing. Investors may want to inquire about the wealth advisory teams’ experience in screening and investing in sustainable funds, and how many of their clients use some form of sustainable investing. If this is important to you, you should select an advisory team that values it as well.
