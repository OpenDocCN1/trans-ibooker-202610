to understand the role of a particular type of investment.
Consequently, we sometimes scare investors away from what they
should be embracing. The term alternative investments is a good
example of how industry jargon gets in the way and limits the integration of these strategies into investor portfolios. The name alone
conjures up fear and confusion.
