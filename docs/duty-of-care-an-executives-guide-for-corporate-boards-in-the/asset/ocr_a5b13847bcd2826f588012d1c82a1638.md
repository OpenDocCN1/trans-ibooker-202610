# # Factor for Speed

Your computers manage large volumes of data and transactions at very high speed. Technology and speed are often presented to boards as risks, but according to Gartner, 56% of CEOs who are investing in digital transformation are seeing measured profit gains.3 It is imperative that boards be aware of the risks, but they also need to look for the opportunities.

As evidence of the impact of speed, technology is frequently the driver in what are termed black swan events – that is, random events or occurrences that deviate from what is normally expected. A black swan event is unanticipated, but often, a little discussion and forethought would have made the situation significantly less random.
