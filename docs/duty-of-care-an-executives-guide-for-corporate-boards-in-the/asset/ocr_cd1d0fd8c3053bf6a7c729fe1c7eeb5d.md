+   • Diamond and gold companies (including Berkshire Hathaway Inc.’s Richline Group) are working with IBM to develop a block-chain-based system—called TrustChain— which will track the origin of jewelry to ensure that it is ethically sourced. It is expected to be available to the public by the end of 2018.

+   • A private, nonprofit organization is working on building a global decentralized identity system based on block-chain technology. Partners include US-based IBM, as well as Deutsche Telecom AG’s innovation group (Telekom Innovation Laboratories). The result will be immutable identity and citizenship records, which will benefit both security and immigration processes.

+   • Container shipping lines have teamed up with technology companies to upgrade the vast databases tracking cargo, moving away from reliance on millions of paper documents. The industry last undertook significant change in the mid-twentieth century, with the move to standardized containers. Success in cargo tracking will demand participation by manufacturers, banks, insurers, brokers, and port authorities around the world. If it works, documentation that currently takes days will be done in minutes, in most instances without the need for human intervention.
