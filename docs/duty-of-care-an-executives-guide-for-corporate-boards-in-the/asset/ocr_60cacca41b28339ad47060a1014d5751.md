There are ways to prevent most cybersecurity exposures.
The challenge for the board is to know that they are in use.

# Planning

The most difficult cybersecurity decision you need to make
is whether you want to just do what you need to do, or
whether you want to proactively plan for the risk to your
business. Global research shows that more than half of com-
panies experienced a global data breach in the past five years,
and half of those breached companies had more than one
incident.⁴

The nature and breadth of exposures to plan for grows
every year, as exemplified by the following notes. Not being
proactive will put you at risk.
