The point of inflection came when Facebook was found to be selling behavioral analytics for its 87 million users. Until then, most social users seemed to be unaware or unconcerned about their right to privacy.

Facebook's sale of users' personal and behavioral data will drive fundamental changes for many business models. Regulatory requirements for transparency and consent will have a dramatic impact. As a director, you need to be prepared to discuss the risks. The Smart Questions later in this chapter are designed to help you balance the opportunities and the emerging issues in social media.

The final consideration in social media is your company's own presence. Even if you are not actively using social media, your Corporate Relations work should include knowing (and managing) what is being said about your company, your executives and your fellow directors.
