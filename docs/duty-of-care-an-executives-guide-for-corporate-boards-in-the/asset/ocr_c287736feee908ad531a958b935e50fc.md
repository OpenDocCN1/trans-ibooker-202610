n today’s fast-paced digital age it is no longer acceptable for executives and board members to merely rely on experts to help them with difficult decisions concerning technology. Duty of Care: An Executive’s Guide for Corporate Boards in the Digital Era offers a hands-on guide for board directors and leaders who need to understand how digital technology affects their organizations. By improving their knowledge about technology, leaders are better able to make the right decisions about digital investment and deployments. Written in accessible terms, the book offers practical advice for protecting organizations from risks ranging from massive cyber security breaches to digital infrastructure investment mistakes.

Author Alizabeth Calder, an expert in cyber security, offers a range of illustrative case studies from such well-known organizations as Amazon, Home Depot, Yahoo, Equifax, and others. These studies clearly reveal what their boards got right, where they went wrong, and provide a helpful takeaway from each example.
