# Aligning Your Investment Level with Where You Want to Be

There isn’t a right or a wrong digital maturity; there is only alignment between where you want to be, and your investment level. Alignment lets you avoid gaps between intention and reality. If your intentions are clear and aligned with how you are investing, you can reduce the risk that you will be surprised by an industry disruption or that your shareholders’ expectations will not be met.
