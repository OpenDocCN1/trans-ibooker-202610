+   - Have we challenged management to include board review and approval in our technology strategy? Do we receive updates on how that strategy is progressing?
+   - What decisions are we delegating that we should be more involved in?
+   - Does technology make it possible for us to do something new or different?
+   - Are we effectively discussing the opportunities to enable value creation?
+   - What changes are we making that could impact our policies?
+   - Are we truly accountable for our decisions relating to IT?
+   - How much are we or our leadership team expecting outside experts to be accountable for technology decisions?
+   - Are the decisions we delegate appropriate? What is the risk?
+   - Do we provide effective oversight of risk in relation to digital investments?
