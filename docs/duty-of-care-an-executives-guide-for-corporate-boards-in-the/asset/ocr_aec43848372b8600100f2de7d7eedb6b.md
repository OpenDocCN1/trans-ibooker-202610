# # Prevention and Detection

Cybersecurity best practice combines and balances security mechanisms, automation, and education. You need to ask questions that are specific enough to either confirm that controls are in place or to take a call to action for improvement. The right things to do fall into three categories: mechanisms, automation, and education.

**Mechanisms** – There is a well-defined set of things that you need to have in place, in use, and effectively monitored or enforced. Specific things to look for include:

| Mechanism | What You Need to Know |
|---|---|
| Policies and procedures | Policies and procedures are clear standards for internal behaviour and the employee's role in protecting business assets from outside factors. Look for policies that are simple, clear, and straightforward, so they will be well understood. You should be able to read the policy and understand it. |
| User authentication and access control | User authentication and access control should be a routine part of how your systems are managed. That needs to include regular monitoring for any unusual activity and periodic audit to verify that the rules are being followed. Oversight needs to include:

1. Both physical and logical access.

2. Specific levels of control, so any sensitive data is explicitly restricted to the people who need it. |
