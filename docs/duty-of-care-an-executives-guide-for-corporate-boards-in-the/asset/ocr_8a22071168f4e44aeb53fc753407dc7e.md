**Brain–computer interfaces (BCI)** – BCI uses encoded information from a computer to send messages that can be understood as human neural activity. The most prominent example is cochlear implants, which convert sound into electrical signals that can be recognized by the brain. More dramatic examples use implanted electrodes, such as devices implanted in the arm of accident victims to generate hand and arm movement.

Thousands of Parkinson's patients are benefiting from BCI implants for deep-brain stimulation therapy. Advanced
