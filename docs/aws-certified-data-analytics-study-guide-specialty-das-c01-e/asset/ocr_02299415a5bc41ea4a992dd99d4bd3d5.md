HBase was modeled after Google’s Bigtable by Chat Walters and Jim Keller and released as a part of Hadoop 0.15 in October 2007, which eventually spun into a separate sub-project in May 2010.

While HBase is used across industries for a range of use cases, the most popular of them being Facebook’s messaging infrastructure being run over HBase and Twitter’s use of HBase across its entire Hadoop cluster.

# HBase Architecture

HBase follows a master-slave architecture typically with four major components:

+   - Master
+   - Region server
+   - ZooKeeper cluster
+   - Storage (/HDFS/S3)
