## Introduction
Welcome to Fashion Design Workshop: REMIX—where fashion design, illustration, and creativity intersect with inclusivity, empowerment, and individuality! In this companion to my first book, Fashion Design Workshop (Walter Foster Publishing), we dive deeper into creative expression through learning the art of fashion design, including experimenting with a variety fun and engaging techniques across a variety of media, while simultaneously learning to cultivate a uniquely personal style. Fashion Design Workshop: REMIX is a celebration of design and how we express ourselves through fashion regardless of body type, physical ability, or gender.
