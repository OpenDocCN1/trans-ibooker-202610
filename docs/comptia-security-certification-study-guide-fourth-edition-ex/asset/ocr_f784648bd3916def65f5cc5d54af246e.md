# Prioritize Threats

Once you have identified all of the threats that could occur against each asset, you must prioritize the threats based on their impact and probability of occurring (also known as likelihood of occurrence) so that you can deal with the more serious threats first.

# Risk Register

A risk register is a tool that is used to plot a description of the different threats, along with their impact and probability. The tool could be something as simple as a spreadsheet listing the details of the risk along with the impact and probability, or it could be a third-party software application. The risk register typically plots the

category the risk applies to, a description of the threat, the impact (maybe a number from 1 to 5), the probability (again, maybe a number from 1 to 5), the risk score (impact multiplied by probability), and the mitigation steps. The risk score helps you prioritize the threats. Here is an example of a risk register:

Project: Public Web Site

| Category | Num | Name of Threat | Probability | Impact | Risk Score | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| External | 1.1 | Denial of Service | High | High | 9 | Firewall protecting the web server |
| External | 1.2 | Defacing Web Site | Low | Medium | 2 | Firewall and authentication services |
| Environmental | 2.1 | Flood in Datacenter | Low | High | 3 | Ensure equipment is up off the floor |
| Environmental | 2.2 | Loss of Power | Medium | High | 6 | Generators and UPS |
| Environmental | 2.3 | Overheating | Low | Medium | 2 | Air conditioning/hot aisle-cold aisle |
| Internal | 3.1 | Accidental Deletion of File | Medium | Medium | 4 | Permissions/backups |
| Internal | 3.2 | Malicious Update | High | Low | 3 | Permissions/backups |

Scale: Low=1, Medium=2, High=3
