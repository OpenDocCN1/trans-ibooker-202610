OSINT You can find a wealth of information about a company on the Internet, such as e-mail addresses as well as servers or devices connected to the Internet. Using Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) tools, you can easily retrieve information about a company that is available publicly. Maltego is an example of an OSINT tool that can collect e-mail addresses, files, phone numbers, DNS records, IP addresses, and geo-locations of assets for a company.

# Exercise Types

When performing a penetration test, you can use different types of testing. You could be performing the pentest so that you can attempt to hack into company assets (the goal of a red team), or maybe your goal is to see if the defensive team (that blue team) can detect that the attack is occurring and stop it. The following are the different security teams your company may employ:

+   - Red team The red team is the cybersecurity team that takes a methodological approach to attacking systems and attempting to bypass security controls. They are the attackers.
+   - Blue team The blue team is the cybersecurity team responsible for detecting an attack and protecting company assets by stopping the attack in its tracks. They are the defenders.
+   - White team The white team in cybersecurity is the team that oversees the security test and records the progress of both the red team and blue team and assigns the scores. The white team also reviews the security reports created by both the red team and the blue team to rate them based on their accuracy.
+   - Purple team A purple team in cybersecurity is a team that performs the functions of both the red team and the blue team.

> exam Watch For the exam remember that the red team is responsible for performing attacks against the systems during a security test (the bad guys), while the blue team is responsible for detecting the attack and preventing a compromise (the good guys).
