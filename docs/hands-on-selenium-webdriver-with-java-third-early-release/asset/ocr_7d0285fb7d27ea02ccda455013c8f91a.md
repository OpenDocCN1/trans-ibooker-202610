# # Improving Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs Through Semantic Chunking

Zihan Wang, Yunxuan Li, Mintong Kang, Jun Li, Zhangyang Wang, Rahul G. Krishnan, Terrence J. Sejnowski, Yixuan Li

University of California, San Diego | University of Texas at Austin | Salk Institute

# ## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) have shown remarkable performance on a wide range of natural language processing tasks. However, they still struggle to reason over long contexts. In this paper, we propose semantic chunking, a method that partitions documents into semantically coherent chunks to improve long-context reasoning. Experiments demonstrate that our approach outperforms fixed-length chunking on various benchmarks.

# ## 1 Introduction

Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have enabled them to process long documents. Yet, reasoning over long contexts remains challenging because information is often distributed across many paragraphs. We investigate how chunking strategies affect LLM performance and show that semantic chunking can substantially improve retrieval and reasoning.
