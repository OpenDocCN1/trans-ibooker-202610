| `NoAlertPresentException` | Dialog (alert, prompt, or confirm) not available | Trying to perform an action (e.g. `accept()` or `dismiss()`) into an unavailable dialog |

| `NoSuchFrameException` | Frame or iframe not available | Trying to switch into an unavailable frame or iframe |
