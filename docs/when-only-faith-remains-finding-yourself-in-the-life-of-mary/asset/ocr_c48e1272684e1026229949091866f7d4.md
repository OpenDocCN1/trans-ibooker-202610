# Chapter Two

# Engaging in the Unexpected

> "For I know the plans I have for you," declares the Lord, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you a hope and a future."
> 
> —Jeremiah 29:11

Getting engaged is one of the most exciting and memorable moments of a woman's life. Most girls dream of finding a husband to provide, protect, and love her just the way she is—fearfully and wonderfully made, yet perfectly imperfect.

My own engagement did not disappoint. My boyfriend, much to my surprise, proposed to me on Labor Day weekend at my childhood summer home. Although my parents were away camping, they had invited us and another couple to enjoy the last days of summer at their log cabin on the lake, fully equipped with a hot tub and ski boat. The lake was one of my favorite places on earth, filled with memories of sunrise skiing on glassy water, catching and cleaning fish to fry, sailing, and watching fireworks on the Fourth of July from the boat.
