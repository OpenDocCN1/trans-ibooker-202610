# Chapter Fourteen

# Lead Me to the Cross

> When Jesus saw his mother standing there beside the disciple he loved, he said to her, “Dear woman, here is your son.”
> 
> —John 19:26 NLT

March 5, 2015, began like any other day for my family. My alarm clock woke me, I got ready, then I woke all three children. They got ready, and we managed to pull it all together, pile into the Suburban, and get to school on time. I proceeded to go to work and tackle a never-ending list, and so began our normal, mundane, at-least-it’s-almost-spring day. Then my family got the call. That SOS from the crystal waters of the Bahamas would echo in our souls for days, months, and years to come.

My brother Jonathan and his girlfriend had embarked on the adventure of a lifetime, sailing to the Bahamas in January.
