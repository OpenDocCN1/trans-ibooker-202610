# 5.1.3 Chapter Objectives

CBD-based signatures are not ideal: instead, they contain noise, they are distorted, they contain errors, and so on. Although we may have, for example, an intelligent sensor that is robust enough to attach to the output node of an SMPS – to sample data; perform Bessel, Butterworth, or Chebyshev filtering; digitize analog data; perform digital signal processing; and extract a condition indicator such as the amplitude of the ripple voltage (V_R, a type of FD) at the output node of that SMPS – we might discover that our signature is so noisy, it cannot be used without further signal conditioning.
