# 5.2.2 Example Target for Prognostic Enabling

For illustrative purposes, the capacitance of the output filter of an SMPS is selected as the prognostic target, and the output ripple voltage is selected as the CBD feature to be extracted as a condition indicator and processed as a signature. That power supply uses a design incorporating a sufficiently large value of output filtering capacitance that the amplitude of the ripple voltage is dependent on the resistivity of the load and not on the amount of filtering capacitance. Degradation effects that result in the loss of filtering capacitance will cause the amplitude of the output ripple voltage to increase (Erickson 1999; Judkins et al. 2007; Singh 2014).
