Therefore,

$$P_0/(FD_i/FD_0)^2 = P_0 - dP_i$$

and, rearranging terms,

$$P_0(FD_0)^2/(FD_i)^2 = P_0 - dP_i$$

or

$$(FD_0/FD_i)^2 = (P_0 - dP_i)/P_0 = 1 - dP_i/P_0$$

And finally,

$$DPS_i = dP_i/P_0 = 1 - (FD_0/FD_1)^2 \quad (3.15)$$
