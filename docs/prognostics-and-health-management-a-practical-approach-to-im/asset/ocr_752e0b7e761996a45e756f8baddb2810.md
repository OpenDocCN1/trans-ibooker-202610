$$\sum_{j=1}^{m_k} x_{kj} \ge 1 \qquad (k=1,2,\ldots,K)$$

and in general

$$\sum_{j=i+1}^{i+m_k} x_j \ge x_{ki} \qquad (i=1,2,\ldots,N-m_k;\ k=1,2,\ldots,K) \tag{1.116}$$

If $x_{ki}=0$, then this constraint has no restriction. And if $x_{ki}=1$, then at least one of $x_{k,i+1},\ldots,x_{k,i+m_k}$ must be equal to 1.

+   - Let
+   
+     $$z_i=\begin{cases}1 & \text{if at least one maintenance is done at period }i\\0 & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}\quad (i=1,\ldots,N)$$

Then we require that

$$z_i \le \sum_{k=1}^{K}x_{ki} \le Kz_i \qquad (i=1,\ldots,N) \tag{1.117}$$

If no maintenance is made in period $i$, then all $x_{ki}=0$, so $z_i=0$. If at least one maintenance is done in period $i$, then $\sum_{k=1}^{N}x_{ki}\ge1$, so $z_i$ must not be 0, and the right-hand side of this inequality allows it to be equal to 1.

+   - The maximum number of maintenance actions in each time period must not exceed $M$:
+   
+     $$\sum_{k=1}^{K}x_{ki}\le M \qquad (i=1,\ldots,N) \tag{1.118}$$

The objective function is to minimize the number of time periods when maintenance is done:

$$\text{Minimize}\quad \sum_{i=1}^{N}z_i \tag{1.119}$$

This problem is a linear binary optimization problem, which can be solved by routine methods.

We also might minimize the total cost:

$$\text{Minimize}\quad \sum_{i=1}^{N}Cz_i+\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{k=1}^{K}c_{ki}x_{ki} \tag{1.120}$$

where $C$ is the setup cost and $c_{ki}$ is the maintenance cost of machine $k$ in time period $i$.

A comprehensive summary of the most important maintenance models and methods is presented, for example, in (Valdez-Flores and Feldman 1989), (Nakagawa 2006, 2008), (Jardine 2006), and (Wang 2002).
