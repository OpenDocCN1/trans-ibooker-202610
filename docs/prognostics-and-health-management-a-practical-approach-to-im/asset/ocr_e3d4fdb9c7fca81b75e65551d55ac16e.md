This chapter presented two quantitative measures for assessing the nonlinearity of an FFS curve: point-by-point nonlinearity and total nonlinearity, using Eqs. (3.23) and (3.24):

```
FNL_i = FFS_i - IDEAL_FFS_i
FNL_E = max(abs({FNL_i}))
```

In the next chapter, you will learn that signatures can be effectively modeled by a limited number of characteristic curves: 10 power functions and 4 exponential functions. You will also learn that a heuristic approach to modeling signatures is sufficiently accurate for prognostics: formal modeling based on traditional methods such as FMEA and PoF is not necessary.
