The design of a prediction algorithm to produce accurate prognostic information is greatly facilitated when FFS data is the input. For example, at any relative sample time (ST), the corresponding sample amplitude (SA) is used to calculate an estimated failure time (FT), RUL, and SoH using the following equations:

FT = 100 ST / SA = failure time (1.103)

RUL = FT − ST = remaining useful life (1.104)

SoH = 100 RUL / FT = state of health (1.105)
