+   8. Where could I be an agent for change or make a significant difference?
+   9. What would make my life more meaningful, joyful, and fully engaged?
+   10. What inner need must I satisfy in order to feel like I’m making a contribution?
+   11. Does the need or the opportunity for change tap into my passion and excitement?
+   12. What values, beliefs, or principles are most important to me to advance or pursue?
+   13. Do my values and conscience inspire me to take action and become involved?
+   14. Do I possess the right skills, talent, and resources to make the contribution I want to make?
+   15. What does my desired future success or contribution look like?
