# 6. Rediscover Who—Your Talent: Identify the Right People, in the Right Seats

> *If you pick the right people and give them the opportunity to spread their wings, then you almost don’t have to manage them.*
> —Jack Welch, former chairman and CEO of General Electric

The “who” is about your talent and your leaders. Who has what roles and responsibilities? Each team must have an accountable leader supported by team members who share the same goal and are rewarded to achieve it. Coaches can help clearly define the “who”: who is responsible for getting the work done? Who is responsible for supporting the team? Who can produce the resources needed? Who can remove obstacles? And who can leverage the strength needed to produce great performance and results?
