# SHARING IS CARING: WIRELESS POWERSHARE

A great deal of attention is paid to keeping your phone charged. Sometimes your phone has plenty of power, but another device is running low. Your S10 has a cool new feature called Wireless PowerShare. You go into Settings, turn this capability on, and the back of your S10 becomes a wireless charger!
