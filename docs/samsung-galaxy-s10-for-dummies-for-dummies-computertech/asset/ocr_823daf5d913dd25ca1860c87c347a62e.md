# Sending an Attachment with a Text

What if you want to send something in addition to or instead of text? Say you want to send a picture, some music, or a Word document along with your text. Easy as pie, as long as the phone on the receiving end can recognize the attachment. Here is the recipe:

+   1. From the Home screen, tap the Messages icon.
+   2. Either tap the New Message icon and enter the number of the intended recipient or pick up on an existing conversion.
