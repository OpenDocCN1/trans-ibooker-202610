# Entering your fingerprints

Using your fingerprint to unlock your phone is very convenient and very effective. There are a few hoops you need to make this happen. You need to give your phone enough views of the finger you will use, which can be whichever finger you like, so that it is sure that it is you. It also wants to make sure that you, and only you, have stored a fingerprint. Your phone can store multiple fingers. Any of your fingers will work, so have at it.

Here’s what you do:

+   1. Tap the toggle option next to the Fingerprints option shown in Figure 17-5.
+   
+      The leftmost screen in Figure 17-10 appears if you have an S10 or S10+. It's a little different if you have an S10E, where the Power button is the fingerprint reader.
+   2. Press the fingerprint icon on the screen if you have an S10 or S10+. Run your finger over the Power button on the right side if you have an S10E.
+   
+      Keep following the directions on the screen for how it wants you to move until you reach 100 percent. The screen in the middle of Figure 17-10 will eventually turn completely green, and then you're at 100 percent.
+   
+      Enjoy this keen sense of accomplishment, because in a moment, you will be returned to a screen like the one shown in Figure 17-4, but now has one stored fingerprint.

Your fingerprint is now in memory and ready to let you get to your home page with a quick press or swipe. Give it a try. It is very slick!
