If you don’t already have a Facebook account, you can create an account here. Toward the bottom of the screen is the option to sign up for Facebook. Tap the Sign Up for Facebook link, and you see the screen shown in Figure 8-9.

+   » **Mobile number:** Your mobile telephone number will auto-populate for this question. You may want to use another number. Your choice.
