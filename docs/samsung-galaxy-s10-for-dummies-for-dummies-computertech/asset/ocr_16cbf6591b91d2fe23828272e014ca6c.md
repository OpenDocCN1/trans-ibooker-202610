The themes take it to a different level. In addition to the background, the phone will change the fonts and many of the basic icons. You can select different themes, as shown in Figure 16-10, that follow different color and font combinations. There are lots of options.

A few samples also come on your phone. You may be a pink kind of person or a space nut. If these options aren't to your taste, tap the Store link at the top of the page to see a range of options.
