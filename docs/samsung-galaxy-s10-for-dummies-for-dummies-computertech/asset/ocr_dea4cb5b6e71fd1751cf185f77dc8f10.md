# 4. Tap your choice of file type, and your phone presents you with the options that fall into that category.

Get ready to be amazed. Underneath the text box, you have five choices. These include the Gallery for photos, a shot you can take now with the camera, a doodle, a map with your location, or other categories. Figure 4-7 shows the Other category. This is for any file you want to attach that is already on your phone. It can be your electronic business card. It could be a video.
