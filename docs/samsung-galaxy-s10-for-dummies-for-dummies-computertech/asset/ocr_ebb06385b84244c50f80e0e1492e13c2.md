The Contacts Database when you tap a profile.

I've tapped on the contact for my friend, Ludwig, and I have choices:

+   - Tap the green telephone next to the number to dial that number.
+   - Tap the blue message icon to send a text.
+   - Tap the green video call icon to have a video chat.
+   - Tap the gray envelope icon to send an email.

About the only thing that tapping on a data field won’t do is print an envelope for you if you tap the mailing address! At the bottom of the contact is a button that says History. This is a running log of all your communications with that contact regardless of the media you used. Figure 6–13 shows an example.
