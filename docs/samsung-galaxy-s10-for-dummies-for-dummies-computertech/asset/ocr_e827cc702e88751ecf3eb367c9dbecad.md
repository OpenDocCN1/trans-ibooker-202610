At the top of the screen are three links:

+   » **Attach:** Tap this hyperlink to attach a file of any variety to your email.
+   » **Send:** Tap this icon that looks like a paper airplane to send the email to the intended recipient(s).
+   » **More (three vertical dots):** Tap this option, and you get the pop-up shown in Figure 5-15. This may give you some of the following options:
+     - **Save in Drafts:** This allows you to complete the email later without losing your work.
+     - **Send email to myself:** This is an alternative to saving the email in your Sent mail folder.
+     - **Priority:** This option signifies that this is more important that the average email to your recipients.
+     - **Security options:** You have some fancy, 007 options here. You can sign the document or encrypt it.
