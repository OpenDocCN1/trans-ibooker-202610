+   - **Panorama:** This setting lets you take a wider shot than you can with a single shot. Press the Camera button while you rotate through your desired field of view. The application then digitally stitches the individual photos into a single wide-angle shot.
+   - **Pro:** This mode gets you to all kinds of camera settings that someone who knows what he or she is doing would want to control rather than let the camera make the choice. This includes setting the exposure, shutter speed, ISO sensitivity, white balance, focal length, and color tone. If you don't know what these mean, this is not the mode for you.
+   - **Live focus:** This setting lets you get within 20 inches of an object, which is too close for the normal autofocus. On top of that, you can change the focus after taking the photo. (You read that right!)
+   - **Photo:** Taking a single photo at a time is the default setting, which assumes average light. It's a good place to start. It's also a good place to stay most of the time.
+   - **Video:** This is how you take videos with your phone. More on this option later in this chapter.
+   - **Super slow-mo:** This switches you to video mode to slow down really fast shots. The exceptional thing is that your S10 does this is in full 4K resolution, so you're getting both high speed and high resolution with no compromise!
+   - **Slow motion:** This is plain-old, boring slow motion. It's still cool.
+   - **Hyperlapse:** You can use this setting to create a time-lapse video. The video camera will adjust the speed of the shots based upon the movement of the phone.

Choose the option that sounds right by flicking through the options, and snap away.
