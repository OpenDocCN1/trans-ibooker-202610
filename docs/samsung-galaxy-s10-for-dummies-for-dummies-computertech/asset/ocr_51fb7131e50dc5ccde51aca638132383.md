If you're particularly interested in one card, you can tap Pin to Top. This will move that card to the top of the list. You can also ask it to refresh itself. That will cause it to check if there is new information.

The Bixby page is very handy when you're actively using your phone. It makes whatever you consider to be most important readily available with two quick presses of a button or a quick swipe to the right from the Home page.
