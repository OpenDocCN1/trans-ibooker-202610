After you set up the receiving part of email, the other important side is composing and sending email. At any time when you’re in an email screen, simply tap the Menu button to get a pop-up screen. From the pop-up menu, tap the Compose icon to open the email composition screen.

Here’s the logic that determines which email account will send this email:

- If you’re in the inbox of an email account and you tap the Compose icon after tapping Menu, your phone sends the email to the intended recipient(s) through that account.

### Managing Your Contacts

You're probably familiar with using contact databases. Many cellphones automatically create one, or at least prompt you to create one. You also probably have a file of contacts on your work computer, made up of work email addresses and telephone numbers. If you're kickin' it old school, you might even keep a paper address book with names, addresses, and telephone numbers.
