# Bringing Up Bixby

Bixby Home is all about getting you summaries of the information you want to see the most in the most efficient way for you. Bixby Home gives you summaries of information from a variety of different apps. Each summary is called a Bixby card.

The simplest way to get to Bixby Home, shown in Figure 14-1, is to swipe to the right from your phone’s home page. I talk more about voice interaction with Bixby later in this chapter.

Bixby Home is made up of “cards” that relate to popular applications you have on your phone. The cards give you a quick highlight of what’s up. You can have as many or as few cards as you want on Bixby Home. You can see all the options for setting up cards by tapping the Bixby Options icon, shown in the upper-right corner of Figure 14-1. This brings up a list of Bixby options (see Figure 14-2).

When you tap Cards, you can see all the applications that you could potentially set up as cards on your Bixby page (see Figure 14-3).

When you tap Apps under Home Cards, you see the apps you’ve already downloaded, as well as apps that you may want to download. This list will continue to change as more apps support the Bixby card. If you see an app is supported, but you haven’t downloaded it yet, simply tap the Download button, and you’ll be taken to the page within the Google Play Store so you can add it immediately.

If you want to delete a card, you simply slide the card to the right. Figure 14-4 shows Bixby Home after you’ve swiped to the right.

You have some flexibility in the placement of the cards. Each card has two horizontal lines in the upper-right corner.
