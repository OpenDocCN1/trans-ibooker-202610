# The Digital Camcorder in Your Pocket

Your Samsung Galaxy S10 Camera application can also function as a digital camcorder.

# Starting the camcorder

All you need to do to use your phone as a video recorder is to put your camera into Camcorder mode. From the camera viewfinder, you tap the icon that looks like the record button of a movie camera in the upper-right corner, and you switch from photographer to videographer.

![](bbox=[0.17, 0.84, 0.22, 0.88])

At this point, recording video automatically starts. You get the notification that says “Rec” in red and the timer from when it started.
