You could always put your phone on the seat next to you, but that's for amateurs. Instead, offer your phone a place of honor with a car docking station. It makes accessing your phone while you drive safer and easier. Some see getting a car mount as a luxury, but my view is that if you need to upgrade your navigation software, you need a vehicle mount.

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.18, 0.18, 0.27])

Be sure to put away the docking station when you park. Even an empty docking station is a lure for a thief.

Figure 16-4 shows a typical vehicle mount. There are a great variety of options. Some mounts grip the phone, while other involve magnets. Some mounts use suction cups to hold onto the inside of your windshield, some clip on air vents, and still others you can screw into your dashboard. Many allow you to plug in a car charger, while others offer wireless charging. They cost $30 to $50. You can get these at your carrier's store, at Best Buy, or at Amazon.

More economical vehicle mounts are available that do not include a wireless charging pad. Figure 16-5 shows an example from SlipGrip that costs closer to $30.

This version is special because it is specifically designed to work with larger protective cases. It can be a hassle to have to remove the protective case from your phone to insert it in a vehicle mount. In addition to a car mount, SlipGrip also offers a mount for your bicycle.

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.8, 0.18, 0.88])

When shopping for a car mount or a bike mount, be sure to consider the extra girth associated with a case. If you don't use a case (which is a mistake), you just need to be sure that the mount is compatible with a Galaxy S10. If you have a case on your phone (which is anything but a mistake), be sure the mount will work with the Galaxy S10 and the case.
