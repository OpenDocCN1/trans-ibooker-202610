# Getting and Using Directions

You probably want to get directions from your map application. I know I do. You can get directions in a number of ways, including:

+   » Tap the Search text box and enter the name or address of your location — for example, Seattle Space Needle or 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield, IL.
+   » Tap the Explore icon (refer to Figure 11-1), tap the Attractions icon (refer to Figure 11-4), and select your location.

Any of these methods lead you to the map showing your location.

It might seem intuitive to expect that when you search for a specific attraction (such as the Seattle Space Needle), you get only the Seattle Space Needle. Such a result, however, is sometimes too simple. A given search may have multiple results, such as for chain stores. Google Map gives you several choices, and you may need to choose your favorite. Typically, the app will give you the closest option.
