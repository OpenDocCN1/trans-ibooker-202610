After you save an event, you can edit it, share it, or delete it by tapping it on any of your calendars. It appears as shown in Figure 13-8. Now you can

+   - **Edit:** This brings up the information as entered. Make your changes and tap Save.
+   - **Share:** You can send this event as an attachment to anyone as a text.
+   - **Delete:** To delete an event, you simply tap the garbage can icon at the bottom of the screen and the event is gone.
