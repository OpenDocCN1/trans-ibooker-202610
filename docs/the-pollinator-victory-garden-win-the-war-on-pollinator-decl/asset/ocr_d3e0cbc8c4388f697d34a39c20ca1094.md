# Maintain the meadow for longevity

After establishment (once plants have all rooted and filled in), meadows and meadowscapes must be periodically cut back, typically once a year in early spring. If this cutting is not done, woody plants may soon encroach on the meadow. Keep the perennials and grasses standing through winter to provide habitat and seeds for wildlife.

Meadows of all kinds are slow to start in the spring; most meadow species, especially native grasses, are warm-season plants. In spring, for larger meadows, consider cutting smaller areas back over a period of weeks, allowing hibernating species to wake up and leave without injury.
