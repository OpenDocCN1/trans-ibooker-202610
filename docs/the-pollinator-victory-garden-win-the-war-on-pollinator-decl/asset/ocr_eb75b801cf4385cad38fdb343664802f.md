# # Win the war on pollinator decline!

Pollinators are critical to our food supply and responsible for the reproduction of the vast majority of all flowering plants on our planet. Pollinators include not just bees but also beetles, bats, birds, moths, flies, wasps, and other species. Many pollinators are in trouble and the reality is that most of our landscapes have little to offer them.

By planting a bit differently and tweaking your landscape aesthetic, you can transition your landscape into a pollinator haven.
