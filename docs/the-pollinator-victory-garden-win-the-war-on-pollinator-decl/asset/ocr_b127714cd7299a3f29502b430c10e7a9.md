+   Serviceberries (Amelanchier species) offer early blooms for pollinators.

however, does not make the serviceberry a bad pollinator plant compared to a coneflower. The serviceberry flowers early in the season when not much else is around to provide nectar. It's an important food source for early emerging mining bees and sweat bees as well as a larval host plant. Duration

of bloom is not the sole factor when choosing pollinator plants.

It can take some time to establish a consistent succession of bloom in a pollinator garden, but your observation and planting will pay off.
