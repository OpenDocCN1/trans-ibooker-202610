# # Plant Lists

Remember that not all pollinators use every plant. Always emphasize plants that are native to your region and appropriate for your landscape conditions. Use these lists in combination with other recommended resources to select the best possible pollinator plants for your Pollinator Victory Garden.

For plant lists by region and a list of larval host plants, visit www.ecobeneficial.com/PVG.
