# # 3 Providing Pollinators with Food to Eat

Habitat consists of a place for pollinators to live as well as
a place for them to eat and get the nutrition they need to
flourish. These habitats may be the same, can be overlapping,
or may be more disconnected as is the case for honey bees,
which will fly several miles, if necessary, to obtain food. In
managed landscapes, the place to live and the place to eat
(both habitats) should overlap and are equally important. In
most cases, you can provide pollinators with both a place to
live and a place to eat in the same general area.
