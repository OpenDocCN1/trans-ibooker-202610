# Pollination Effectiveness of Native Bees

Native pollinators don’t always get the credit they are due for the pollination services they provide. Bumble bee pollination of blueberries creates twice as much fruit as honey bee pollination; native bees account for 90 percent of pollination of watermelons; and native bee pollination improves fruit production in apples and tomatoes. Squash and gourds are best pollinated by two species of native squash bees.

Blue orchard mason bees are incredible pollinators of apples and almonds. It is thought that only 250 females are needed to pollinate 1 acre (0.4 ha) of apples, compared to 15,000 to 20,000 foraging honey bees. Research is under way to diversify almond pollination with these native bees, which is currently completely done using honey bees.
