# Understand Plant Regionality

Regional plant lists can be a helpful starting point when selecting plants for your Pollinator Victory Garden. But the way regions are defined can vary based on the source. As a start, see if your state is included in the region being discussed.

Don't be surprised if you see the same plant genus (collective name for a group of related plants) occurring in multiple regions of North America. For example, the genus Solidago (goldenrod) is found in every region of the United States, accounting for more than 100 species. But not every species of goldenrod occurs in every region or in the same geographic conditions. Some species have very large ranges and are adapted to a variety of ecological regions (ecoregions).
