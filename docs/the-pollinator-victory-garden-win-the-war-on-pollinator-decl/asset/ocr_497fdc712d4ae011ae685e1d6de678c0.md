# Pollinator-Friendly Lawn Alternatives

Reducing or eliminating turfgrass lawns in favor of pollinator gardens could be the greatest ecological achievement of our time. You can help start the trend! To be fair, no native pollinator-friendly lawn alternatives function exactly like turfgrass lawns. If you have lawn that you really use for pets or recreation, keep what you need and lose the rest. Whatever lawn you keep, manage it organically (pesticide free).

# Determine sight lines and plant heights

Lawn replacements don’t have to look like lawn, and they don’t have to be short. When deciding on lawn alternatives, consider plant heights and the sight lines of your landscape. What views do you have that you don’t want to obscure? What views would you like to create with plantings—perhaps a beautiful view of flowering plants from a patio? Do practical considerations, such as needing a clear view when backing out of a driveway, limit plant height in certain areas? Your tastes and practical limitations will dictate whether lawn alternatives are short, tall, or somewhere in between.
