The survey from CreatorIQ, referenced earlier in this chapter, revealed another valuable insight: What influencers believe the brands they work with care about the most. Content quality came in as the most cited answer (40%), followed by engagement rates (37%), follower count (16%), brand alignment (12%), and audience demographics (6%).
