# The power of the long tail

Amazon, Spotify, and Google have all built their companies on the premise of the long tail. The concept, in the context of business, was popularized in 2004, when Wired editor-in-chief Chris Anderson published an article entitled “The Long Tail.”¹¹

In his article, Anderson argues that our culture and economy is shifting away from a focus on a relatively small number of hits (the head of the demand or distribution curve) toward an infinite, or at least rapidly expanding, number of niches in the tail (collectively with a larger market share than the head).
