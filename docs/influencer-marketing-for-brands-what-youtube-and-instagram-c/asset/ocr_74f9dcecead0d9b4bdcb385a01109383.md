# The disconnect between marketing objectives and KPIs

It is one thing to set objectives and another to measure the outcome and understand the results. In early 2019, my company interviewed 351 marketing teams⁹ to get a better sense of what their primary influencer marketing objectives were and how they measured their campaign results. The purpose of the research was twofold: First, to gain better knowledge on why they run influencer marketing campaigns, and secondly how they define their success.

Fifty percent of respondents said that their primary objective for their influencer marketing was awareness or consideration, followed by sales (34%). Content production came in at first place, with 13% of respondents (Figure 7-1).
