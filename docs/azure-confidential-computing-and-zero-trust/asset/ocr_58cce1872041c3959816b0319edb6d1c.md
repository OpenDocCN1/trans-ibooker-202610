# Confidential AI

Confidential AI is a collection of hardware-based technologies such as general-purpose CPUs and GPUs that enable the establishment of trusted execution environments (TEEs), designed to safeguard data and models throughout the AI lifecycle, even while data and models are in use. The objective is to secure data throughout all phases of AI data processing, including data collection, preprocessing, training, and deployment of AI models. Confidential computing plays a crucial role in facilitating confidential AI solutions, as it enables access to sensitive datasets while addressing security and compliance concerns with minimal performance overhead when compared to technologies like homomorphic encryption. Confidential computing enables data providers to authorize the use of their datasets for very specific purposes, such as training or adjusting an agreed-upon model while maintaining the confidentiality of the data throughout the computation. Data providers can also request proof via attestation that their datasets are only being processed in a TEE.
