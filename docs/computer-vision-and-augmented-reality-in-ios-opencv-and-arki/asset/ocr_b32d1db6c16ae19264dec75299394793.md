Although scene reconstruction using vision-only techniques could be sufficient in some cases, in many cases visual data coming from handheld mobile phones is distorted due to the excessive motion and unpredictable lighting conditions, leading to a large error margin and in some cases failing to achieve scene reconstruction. In this case, Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) that can be found in most mobile phones today would be used alongside the visual data to result in the best scene reconstruction possible.
