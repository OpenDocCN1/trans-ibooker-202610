This would have not been possible without extremely fast computers and datasets of millions of cat and dog images. You may wonder why deep learning would require fast computers and a huge amount of data. Well, the answer lies behind what deep learning is and how it really works. Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that is largely established on Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs), a computing model inspired by how the human brain operates. Similar to the human brain, an Artificial Neural Network consists of several computing “neurons.” An artificial neuron in a Neural Network performs a simple computing operation that communicates with other connected neurons in the network to make a decision. Each neuron connection to another neuron has a certain strength. The strengths of connections between neurons are called weights (see Figure 1-10).
