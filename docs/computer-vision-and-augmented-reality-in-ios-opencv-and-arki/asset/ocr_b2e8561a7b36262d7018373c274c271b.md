The method of following a single point of interest, or several objects, in a given scene is known as object tracking (Figure 3-11). It has historically been used in video and real-world encounters where findings are made after the initial identification of an object. It is now important for autonomous driving applications such as Uber and Tesla’s self-driving cars.
