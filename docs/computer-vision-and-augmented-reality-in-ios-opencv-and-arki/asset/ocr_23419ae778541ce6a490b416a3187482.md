# Figure 2-2. Single-Lens Camera

Single-lens cameras provide visual data of a scene from one angle, which helps in detecting and tracking simple features of a surface in a scene. Multi-lens cameras, on the other hand, provide visual data from two different angles, which helps in detecting and tracking the depth in a scene, and depth tracking enables a more accurate real-world distance measuring. Accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers are all part of a unit called Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), which enables measuring a device’s acceleration and estimating its velocity, distance, orientation, and gravitational force. Geolocation sensors provide information about the device’s location and geographic coordinates, which enables augmenting and “pinning” digital objects in location-specific areas (Figure 2-3).
