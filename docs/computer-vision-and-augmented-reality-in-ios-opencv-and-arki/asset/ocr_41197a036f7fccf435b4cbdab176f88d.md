# Overview

In computer vision, simulating the human eye enables the computer to process and recognize things in videos and photographs in the same way as human beings, from scenery to objects, motion, and many more. Computer vision has only operated in a small capacity until recently. Today, deep learning has empowered the advanced computer vision we see in many pieces of software we use on a daily basis. In fact, one of the most popular branches of deep learning is computer vision; therefore, deep learning plays a crucial role to achieve and perform complex computer vision operations.
