In the simple DSG above the application is tasked with maintain a room tempurature between 18 and 22 degress celsius. The temp is monitored every fifteen minutes and, if needed adjusted accordingly. Of course the service used by this client doesn’t know what the client has in mind, it just responds with the information about the requested room when asked.
