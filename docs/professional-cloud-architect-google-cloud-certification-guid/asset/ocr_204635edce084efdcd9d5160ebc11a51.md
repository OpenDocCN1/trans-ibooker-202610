As you know, Google has the biggest datasets in the world. For this reason, it developed TensorFlow to be highly scalable. Google uses it for services such as Gmail, Google Search, and Photos. You can have a look at how TensorFlow works using TensorFlow Playground, which is available at the following link: https://playground.tensorflow.org:
