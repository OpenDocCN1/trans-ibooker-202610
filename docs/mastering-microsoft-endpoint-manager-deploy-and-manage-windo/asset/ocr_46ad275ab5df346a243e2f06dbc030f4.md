Enter in the application-specific requirements. The publisher's name is the certificate CN (for example, Contoso) of the organization. Make sure that the certificate is injected into your image, otherwise, the application cannot register and will fail.

> **Important Note**
> If the CN is detected correctly, you'll see the **Subject of the certificate provided** notification. The certificate could be self-signed, public, or internally created via a root CA. The private key and CN must be matching later in the process.
