# Customizing your baselines

Change your baselines to your own values via the Settings menu, as you could have higher or lower principles as the default settings. This would allow organizations to adjust the scorings to standards that match the expectations of their environment and applications.

Baselines define the score and whether indicators show up in green or red in Endpoint analytics. Be careful when defining your own baselines to ensure the quality and performance level of your Windows 10 physical and cloud PCs.
