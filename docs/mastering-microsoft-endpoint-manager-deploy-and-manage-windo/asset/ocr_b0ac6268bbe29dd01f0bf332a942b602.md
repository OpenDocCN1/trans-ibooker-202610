You can also see the status of all the other connector types. There are three different statuses a connector can have, as outlined here:

+   - **Unhealthy:**
+     - The certificate or credentials have expired.
+     - The last synchronization was 3 or more days ago.
+   - **Warning:**
+     - The certificate or credentials will expire within 7 days.
+     - The last synchronization was more than 1 day ago.
+   - **Healthy:**
+     - The certificate or credentials won't expire within the next 7 days.
+     - The last synchronization was less than 1 day ago.
