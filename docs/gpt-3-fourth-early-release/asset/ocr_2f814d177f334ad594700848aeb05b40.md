Artificial intelligence has the potential to improve ordinary people’s lives in countless ways. Democratizing access to AI will make it possible for this transformative technology to benefit everyone.

The authors of this book believe that businesses and research facilities working in the field of AI have a big role to play in making AI more accessible—by sharing the outcomes of their research and development with a broader audience, much as OpenAI has done with GPT-3 in the form of its publicly available API. Making such a powerful tool available at marginal cost to users in important fields can have a long-lasting positive impact on the world.
