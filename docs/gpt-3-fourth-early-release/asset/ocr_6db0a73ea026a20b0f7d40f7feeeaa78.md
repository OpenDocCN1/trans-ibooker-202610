Bakz Awan is a developer turned entrepreneur and influencer. He has a YouTube channel “Bakz T. Future” and a podcast where he discusses the GPT-3 phenomenon, being one of the major voices in the OpenAI API developer community. He notes that despite all the attention the model received so far, GPT-3 is actually still underhyped:

> “If you had told me to get all the things that GPT-3 can do, I would imagine you have to go in a room and the computer is going to be the size of the room and it runs on a specialized version of Linux where I have to type all these commands and I have to do training in order to use. That’s what I would expect from GPT-3’s current abilities today. And the fact that it’s not only available today, it’s usable. You just need a browser. If you have ever filled out a signup form online to register for any website, you can use the OpenAI Playground - that’s how straightforward it is. I think GPT-3 is underhyped. You know, I think all of these models are underhyped for how usable and friendly and fun and powerful they really are. It’s almost shocking, it’s been over a year and I’m still just as shocked.”.

In a brief period of time, we have seen GPT-3 do pretty much everything from writing articles, stories, screenplays, reciting poems, generating advice on the fly, summarizing complex documents to creating artistic ad copies. GPT-3 has grown from just a tool for research to something that’s creating innovation on the ground. Unlike any other AI system before it has empowered the general audience with little to no background in AI to take their ideas to completion!

Within just 9 months of the launch of GPT-3 API, according to OpenAI, people were building more than 300 businesses with it. Despite this suddenness, some experts argue that the excitement isn’t exaggerated.
