# Deployment Automation

It’s important to acknowledge that deployment automation can be a journey that should be realized in small steps. Starting with components that are easy to automate and reduce the amount of manual steps, and slowly progressing to automate more complex components. Looking at deployment automation and testing, there is one factor that plays an important role there – architecture. The best processes and tools used for continuous deployment can not help us if our architecture introduces significant limitations and is a tightly coupled design.

# Team culture

Full cooperation between operations and development teams is required to automate build, testing, deployment and infrastructure. This ensures the entire process is fully understood by all parties and does not introduce unnecessary complexity. It is not an easy process. Very often it requires long hours spent together to recreate architecture for existing processes.

# Built-in Security DevSecOps

It is well known that prevention is better than cure. Same applies for software delivery and challenges related to security. Shortening feedback loop for teams during software delivery is known as the shifting left approach. Same approach is used to introduce security processes early in the development process and across the entire continuous delivery flow. This approach enable teams to build a development stack that is based on security pre-approved standardized tools and policies[4]. This tooling helps teams address security requirements as part of regular development and delivery activities.
