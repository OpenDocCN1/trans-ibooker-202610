+   - Modern CI/CD platforms introduced unified tools. They built knowledge, awareness on expectations and way of working across all involved parties.
+   - Unified toolset improves cooperation in the shared responsibility model that platform introduced into organization.
+   - Processes, operating models, learning and communication paths must be adapted to fit into that new model. It benefits in reduced lead time for changes, increased deployment frequency and significant drop in service recovery time.
