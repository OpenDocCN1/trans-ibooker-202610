# 11.4 Summary

In this chapter, we examined some classical asymmetric algorithms in use today. These algorithms are widely used in e-commerce, online banking, and many other applications. None of these algorithms is resistant to quantum computing–based attacks. For this reason, it is important that newer, quantum-resistant algorithms are tested for use. The next four chapters will explore the issues of quantum computing and cryptography. It is important that you have an understanding of how current algorithms function and why they are not resistant to quantum computing–based attacks.
