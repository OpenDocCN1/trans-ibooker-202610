# 14.6 Summary

This chapter focused on multivariate cryptography. Some of the mathematics might have been new for some readers. The algorithms in this chapter are all viable candidates for quantum resistant cryptography and thus worthy of study. Combining this chapter with Chapter 13, “Lattice-based Cryptography,” and Chapter 15, “Other Approaches to Post-Quantum Cryptography,” will provide you with a solid general understanding of quantum computing–resistant cryptographic algorithms.
