# 5.5 Summary

Information theory is important for understanding quantum computing. In this chapter, you were introduced first to basic probability and set theory. Then you were given a tour of information theory, which applies probability and set theory. The most important concepts from this chapter are how to calculate probabilities and information entropy. You will find these concepts revisited later in a quantum context. We also covered some basic concepts of quantum information theory.
