# Summary

This chapter discussed the practical implications of quantum computing on cryptographic implementations. Specific algorithms were explored, including the impact that quantum computing will have on the use of those algorithms. Perhaps most importantly, common applications of cryptography were explored, and the necessary changes to accommodate quantum-safe cryptography were discussed.
