+   - **Footprinting**: Footprinting is the process that hackers would use to map out the entire network, including employees, computers, IP Addresses, the versions of operating systems, open ports, services, and vulnerabilities. They try to gather as much information on their intended target.
+   - **Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)**: This is intelligence collected legally from the public domain, such as social media or websites on the internet. It is used in law enforcement and business intelligence to help identify the source of attacks. It is only used for non-sensitive data.

> **Exam tip**
> 
> Lateral movement is used by attackers to make their detection by security teams much harder.
