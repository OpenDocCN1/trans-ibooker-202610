+   - **Distance Considerations**: We need to consider the type of data, how critical it is, and how quickly it would need to be recovered. We also need to account for travelling distance, including traffic flows, to ensure that the data can be recovered in a reasonable time. Using a cloud provider might be a good solution as they ensure the availability of the data. It is offsite and easy to connect to.

# Secure Data Destruction

It is very important that we dispose of data in the most secure manner to ensure that it cannot be stolen. We are going to look at sanitizing data on paper followed by data on media.

# Paper Data

When we are disposing of data, the best way of doing this, in order, is as follows:

+   - **Burn**: When we burn paper, we might have an incinerator on site or might get a third party carry out this task and provide us with a destruction certificate.
+   - **Pulping**: If burning is not available, pulping, which turns the data into paper mache, is the best option.
+   - **Shredding**: This is the third best way of disposing of data; a cross-cut shredder is best.

# Media Data

When disposing of data that is stored on media such as a hard drive or DVD/CD ROM, the best ways to dispose of the data are as follows:

+   - **Shredding**: You can shred a metal hard drive into powder, and this is the best method.
+   - **Pulverizing**: This is where you take a sledge hammer and smash it into small pieces.
+   - **Degaussing**: This is where an electrical charge is sent across the disk.

All of the above will render the media unusable. If you want to reuse the media, you need to choose wiping or formatting as the method of sanitization.

# Third-Party Solutions

Some Small to Medium Enterprises (SMEs) may not have incinerators or the proper facilities to carry out data destruction. Therefore, they have a contract with a third party who has the facilities for secure data destruction. The data destruction company will provide a proof of destruction certificate so that the SME can provide proof if audited by the regulators.
