Critical systems could fail if the temperature gets too hot, and the security teams will know whether offices are occupied according to the HVAC system usage register. If the CO2 levels get too high employees could become drowsy and in extreme cases die.

# Specialized Devices

+   - **Medical Devices**: These can include infusion devices that measure fluids that are given to patients in hospitals. Ambulances will carry life-support systems, such as *defibrillators*, that are used to save a person's life if they have just suffered from cardiac arrest.
