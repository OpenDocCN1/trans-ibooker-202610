A variation of the slow temperature ramp includes constantly varying (up and down) vibration (Table 8.8 and Figure 8.16). The temperature limits for this test should be 10°C below the high and low temperature failure limits determined in temperature step stress (basically the same temperature limits used for the combinational temperature and vibration step stress).
