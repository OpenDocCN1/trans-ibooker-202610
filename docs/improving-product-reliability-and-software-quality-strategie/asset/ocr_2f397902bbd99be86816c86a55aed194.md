The rate of reliability growth is based on a value α, which is the slope of the reliability growth curve. If the value of α is closer to 0.1, then the reliability effort to improve product reliability is small and is having little effect. If the value of α is closer to 0.6, then the reliability effort is very ambitious and is having significant effect on improving product reliability.
