On those trials, the models estimated the other as a new other player to allow for flexible learning so late in the task phase. All model estimates were constrained between 0 and 100 (1 for control), similar to the possible rating range for participants.

In the Ignorant learner model, all trials (including AD trials) were treated as normal trials. This means that on every trial, the estimates of self, other and control were updated as follows (similar to equation 7 in the Active learner model but applied to all trials):

$$\mathcal{N}\left(\text{self} * \text{control} + \text{other} * (1-\text{control}),\n\sqrt{\sigma^2_{\text{self}} * \text{control}^2 + \sigma^2_{\text{other}} * (1-\text{control})^2}\right)$$\n\hfill feedback(all trials, normal and AD)~ \hfill (9)

In the Passive learner model, our goal was to mimic someone who gets confused by AD trials and discards them from learning. Therefore, the Passive learner model was fit to normal trials only and AD trials were removed from the data. Crucially, this meant that the parameters about self, other and control were only updated on the remaining normal trials (and not on AD trials), as follows (same as equation 7 in Active learner model):

$$\mathcal{N}\left(\text{self} * \text{control} + \text{other} * (1-\text{control}),\n\sqrt{\sigma^2_{\text{self}} * \text{control}^2 + \sigma^2_{\text{other}} * (1-\text{control})^2}\right)$$\n\hfill feedback(normal)~ \hfill (10)

**Model parameters.** We extracted the mean parameters for self, other and control for all three computational models; these are referred to as estimates throughout the manuscript. Additionally, we used features of the Active learner model to extract a trial-wise measure of uncertainty in the task. For this, we first extracted the 90% credible intervals around the self, other, and control mean estimates on every trial. Trial-wise uncertainty was then defined as the average across these individual uncertainties on each trial, according to the task phase:

$$\text{uncertainty}=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\text{uncertainty}_{\text{self}}+\text{uncertainty}_{\text{other}}}{2}, & \text{Self-Other phase}\\[6pt]\dfrac{100 * \text{uncertainty}_{\text{control}}+\text{uncertainty}_{\text{other}}}{2}, & \text{Control-Other phase}\end{cases}$$\n\hfill (11)

where the trial-wise *uncertainty* was the average across self and other uncertainties in the Self-Other phase, and across control and other uncertainties in the Control-Other phase. For reference, Supplementary Figure 7 shows the correlation of the extracted uncertainties of the Active learner model with the task schedule.
