# Dual Stacking

In dual stacking, a device runs both protocol stacks: IPv4 and IPv6. Of all the transition methods, this is the most common one. Dual stacking can be accomplished on the same interface or different interfaces of the device. The top part of Figure 14-7 shows an example of dual stacking on a router, where NetworkA has a mixture of devices configured for the two different protocols, and the router is configured in dual stack mode. Older IPv4-only applications can still work while they are migrated to IPv6 by supporting newer APIs to handle IPv6 addresses and DNS lookups with IPv6 addresses.
