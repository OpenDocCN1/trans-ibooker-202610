# Changing Port States

After the designated ports are chosen, the switches will move their root and designated ports through the various states—blocking, listening, learning, and forwarding—whereas any other ports will remain in a blocking state. Figure 10-11 shows the ports in a blocking state designated by an X. Remember that on Switch-2, only port F (the root port) is in a forwarding state: port E will remain in a blocking state. In this example, two ports are left in a blocking state: Switch-2’s port E and Switch-4’s port G.
