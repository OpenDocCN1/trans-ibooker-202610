**NOTE** The naming nomenclature discussed here applies to IOS images that are either included on your IOS device when you buy it from Cisco or applied when you download them from Cisco’s web site. For instance, you can download an image from Cisco and rename it myciscoos.bin, and this will have no impact on the IOS device’s performance.
