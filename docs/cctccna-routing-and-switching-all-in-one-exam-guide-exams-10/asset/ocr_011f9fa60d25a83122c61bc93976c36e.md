Note that ComputerA is connected to port 5 of VAN-SW1, while ComputerB is connected to port 15 of VAN-SW1. ComputerC is connected to port 5 of VAN-SW2, while ComputerD is connected to port 15 on VAN-SW2.

+   1. Ensure that both switches are powered up.
+   2. Ensure that ComputerA and ComputerD have IP addresses of 12.0.0.5 and 12.0.0.15, respectively, and that ComputerB and ComputerC have IP addresses of 14.0.0.5 and 14.0.0.15, respectively.
+   3. Connect port 1 on VAN-SW1 to port 1 of VAN-SW2 using a crossover cable.
+   4. Connect port 24 on VAN-SW1 to port 24 of VAN-SW2 using a crossover cable. Wait a minute for the green link lights to show. Notice that one link light will stay amber due to loop prevention features of the switch (more on that in the next chapter).
