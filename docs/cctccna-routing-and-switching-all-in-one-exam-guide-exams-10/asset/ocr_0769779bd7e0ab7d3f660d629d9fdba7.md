**EXAM TIP** You need to know these administrative distances (ADs) for the exam: remember that connected routes have an AD of 0, static routes have an AD of 1, EIGRP has an AD of 90, OSPF has an AD of 110, and RIP has an AD of 120. The route with a lower administrative distance is preferred over a route with a higher administrative distance.
