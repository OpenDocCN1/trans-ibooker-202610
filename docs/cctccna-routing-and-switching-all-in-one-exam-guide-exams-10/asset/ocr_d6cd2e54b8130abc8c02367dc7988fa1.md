A straight-through Ethernet UTP cable has pin 1 on one end of the cable connected to pin 1 on the other end of the cable, pin 2 to pin 2, and so on. A straight-through cable is used to connect dissimilar devices together, for example, between a computer and a switch or a switch and a router.

**EXAM TIP** The CCT and the CCNA certification exams will test you on when to use a straight-through cable, and here is the golden rule to remember: Use a straight-through cable to connect dissimilar devices, such as connecting a computer to a switch, a computer to a hub, a switch to a router.
