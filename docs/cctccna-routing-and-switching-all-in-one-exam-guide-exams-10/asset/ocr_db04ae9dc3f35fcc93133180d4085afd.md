# Console Port

You connect your administrative workstation to the console port to administer the Cisco device locally—which means you are sitting at the device to perform administrative tasks. You would use a console cable (typically light blue in color), aka rollover cable, to connect the serial port of your computer to the console port on the Cisco device. Then you would launch a terminal emulator program to administer the device from the command line interface (CLI).
