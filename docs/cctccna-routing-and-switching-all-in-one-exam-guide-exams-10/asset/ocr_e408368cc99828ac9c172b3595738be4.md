RIPv2 is being used to generate and receive updates on these two interfaces. You can see the two networks specified with the **network** commands: 192.168.1.0 and 192.168.2.0. In this example, this router received an update 22 seconds ago from a neighboring router: 192.168.2.2. For the second gateway, 192.168.3.2, the router hasn’t seen an update from it in 210 seconds. Given that the flush timer is 240 seconds, if the local router doesn’t receive an update from 192.168.3.2 within 30 seconds, 192.168.3.2 and its associated routes are removed from the local router (flushed). And last, the default administrative distance of RIP is 120.
