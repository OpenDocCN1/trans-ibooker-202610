### What about the Pi Zero?

While our powerful Pi 4 will diligently perform the job of polling the status of the attached water sensor, dedicating this hardware to such a simple, non-CPU-intensive task seems like a gross underutilization of the Pi 4’s hardware capabilities. Fortunately, for these kinds of low-CPU-powered projects that need a Linux OS, the Raspberry Pi Foundation has a special kind of Pi called the Pi Zero W.[36] This ten-dollar gumstick-sized computer has just enough compute capacity to execute projects such as these while still offering most of the features, such as WiFi and Bluetooth connectivity, found in its larger siblings.
