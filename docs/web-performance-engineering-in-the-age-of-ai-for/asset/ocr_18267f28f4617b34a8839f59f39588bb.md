The key is to make performance a first-class concern in your development workflow—treating performance budgets as seriously as functional tests.

The MCP server does not replace expert analysis. It accelerates the “find and iterate” loop and gives you a reproducible, measurable path from suspected problem to verified improvement.

# Testing on Real Devices: The Ultimate Validation

Even after all this profiling and tooling, one fundamental truth remains: the best way to know if your app performs well is to test it on a real device under real conditions. Lab measurements and AI analyses are immensely helpful for pinpointing and fixing issues, but they must be corroborated in the real world. Especially for performance, differences in device speed, network quality, and user behavior can reveal issues that synthetic tests don’t.

If your AI-generated app is meant to run on a variety of devices, be sure to try it on a typical low-end smartphone, possibly on a throttled network connection (DevTools can simulate slow 3G, but an actual budget phone on a spotty network often gives a fuller picture). See how the app *feels*. Does it take too long to load? Is scrolling smooth? Does tapping buttons give instant feedback? Also, check for any obvious accessibility flows. Try using it with a screen reader or only a keyboard to navigate, to catch things the AI might have missed (like focus not moving into a modal, or missing form labels).
