Biomarker of `PCOS` includes ovarian and adrenal steroid hormone, steroidogenesis actions, insulin secretion and action, energy homeostasis, gonadotropin action and regulation, and chronic inflammation \[57\]. As soon as the insulin binds to its receptor, the tyrosine gets phosphorylated and the insulin receptor substrate-1 (`IRS-1`) and `IRS-2`, and two intracellular substrates also get phosphorylated.
