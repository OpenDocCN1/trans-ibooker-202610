# A Brief Reflection on Your Data Journey

The goal of this book is to support you on your data journey. But before you jump on the bandwagon, ensure you have a data strategy in place. Whether you’re starting small or have a large set of use cases to implement, without a plan, as you learned in Chapter 1, you’re doomed to fail. I see countless enterprises fail because they’re unable to bring everybody onboard or to articulate their strategy; because they don’t include business users or lack support from senior leadership. I can’t emphasize this enough, but before you start implementing any change, ensure you have a balcony view and a clear map guiding you in the right direction.
