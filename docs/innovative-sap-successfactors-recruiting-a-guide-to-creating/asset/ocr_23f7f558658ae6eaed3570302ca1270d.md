This might work in most situations; however, the Move Candidate action will not work for the following reasons:

+   1. A hardstopStatus may be configured on the assessment that will prevent moving the candidate(s) to a desired stop. Please refer to Chapter 8 on assessment integrations in this book for more information.
+   2. The customer may have unskippable statuses with required fields. In such cases, the only option is to move the candidate(s) to the last skippable status in the applicant workflow.
