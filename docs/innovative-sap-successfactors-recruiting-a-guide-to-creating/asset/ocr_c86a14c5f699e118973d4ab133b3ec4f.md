Here are some discussion points to have with the customer to make your RPA bot for managing offer letters efficient:

+   - Is the pain point for customers for mass hiring situations or for more complex jobs? The decision will drive the design of your RPA bot.
+   - Does the customer have multiple job offer templates for the same position or one template for a mass hiring position? Naturally, the fewer the job offer templates, the easier will be the design of the RPA bot.
+   - Does the customer have multiple versions of the job offer template based on the locale and country/region combination? If that’s the situation, you may end up with multiple versions of the RPA bot per locale and job requisition combination.
+   - Does the customer want to automate the entire job offer process or just for mass hires? Automation of the entire job offer process may not be easily achievable because of the inherent complexity in dealing with various combinations.
