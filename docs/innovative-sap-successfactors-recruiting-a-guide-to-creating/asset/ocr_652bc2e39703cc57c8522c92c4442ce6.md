The third-party matching solution will pass one or more job requisition IDs. A list of application IDs and resumes will then be returned. Note the following:

+   - The full structured extract of the application and candidate profile may be also passed to the matching solution. We are providing just the resume as an example.
+   - The resume will need to be extracted as a second call to SAP SuccessFactors. The reason is that documents aren’t directly stored on the job application or candidate profile. Only a document reference is stored.
