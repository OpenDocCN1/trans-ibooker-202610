We will now use the WordPress Ultimate Category Excluder to remove job postings from the home page. From the WordPress admin, click Settings on the left side as shown in Figure 4-22. You will then see additional Settings options on the left side. Click Category Excluder below Settings, as shown in Figure 4-22.

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.2, 0.82, 0.57])

Figure 4-22. WordPress General Settings and Settings Menu Options

Figure 4-23 shows the Category Excluder options. During our import, we set anything from the job posting XML feed to be set to category “JobPosting.” We will use that category to exclude the postings from the home page as shown by selecting the option Exclude from Front Page? for the JobPosting row and leaving the other options unselected in that row. We will leave the postings in the other areas such as search results.
