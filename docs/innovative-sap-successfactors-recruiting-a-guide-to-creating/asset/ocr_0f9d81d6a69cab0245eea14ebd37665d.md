Once the job is finalized, you have to post the job requisition. Detailed steps are covered in Chapter 4.

# Step 11: Candidate – Undergoing an Assessment

If a job requisition with an associated assessment is posted, the candidate will follow the standard process to register and apply for the job on the customer’s career portal. If the job requisition is configured to have an associated assessment in the “Brand New Application” (see Figure 8-12), the candidate is expected to undergo the assessment as soon as the job application is submitted.
