# CONCLUSION

The hallmarks of the organisms, ideas, and technologies that have managed to survive in the cosmic big bang have something in common: endurance, agility, inspiration, wisdom, a willingness to evolve, and most importantly, an externally enforced obedience to the laws of science (“you can’t fool Mother Nature”). In our data-centric future, the science of cybersecurity should serve as the foundation. But it also requires that we recognize the moment to ensure that our species doesn’t get left behind and forgotten in the dust of the digital big bang.
