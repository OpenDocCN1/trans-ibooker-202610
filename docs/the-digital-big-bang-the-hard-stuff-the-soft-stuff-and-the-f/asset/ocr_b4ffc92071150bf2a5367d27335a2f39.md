# ABOUT THE CONTRIBUTOR

Tim Crothers – VP of Cyber Security, Target

Tim Crothers is a seasoned security leader with over 20 years of experience building and running information security programs, large and complex incident response and breach investigations, and threat and vulnerability assessments. He has deep experience in cyberthreat intelligence, reverse engineering, computer forensics, intrusion detection, breach prevention, and applying six sigma and lean processes to information security. He is the author or coauthor of 15 books to date, as well as having regular training and speaking engagements at information security conferences.

Currently Crothers is VP of Cyber Security for Target. There he has built and leads the Cyber Fusion Center, where the company is working on using innovative and new techniques and technologies to push the envelope of cybersecurity intel, detection and response, and red teaming.
