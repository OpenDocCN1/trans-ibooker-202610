However, the accelerating speed and power of innovation can erode the protective power base of mathematical truths. What we once considered unshakable must now be reexamined. With quantum computing on the horizon—some estimates predict its arrival in 20 years or sooner—the deployment of formulas such as Shor’s algorithm and Grover’s algorithm could make asymmetric encryption far less secure.

As a science, practice, and discipline, cybersecurity has only a few silver bullets. For now, cryptography is the rare exception, a simple, powerful way to provide substantial protection against the torrential downpour of cyberattacks. But though cryptography remains one of cybersecurity’s most powerful tools today, we must always prepare for the risks we will face tomorrow.
