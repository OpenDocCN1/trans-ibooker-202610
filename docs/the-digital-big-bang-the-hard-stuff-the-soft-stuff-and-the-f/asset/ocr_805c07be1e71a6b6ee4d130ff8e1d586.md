# ABOUT THE CONTRIBUTOR

# Dan Boneh - Computer Science, Stanford University

Dan Boneh is a professor of computer science at Stanford University, where he heads the applied cryptography group and co-directs the computer security lab. Boneh’s research focuses on applications of cryptography to computer security. His work includes cryptosystems with novel properties, web security, cryptography for blockchains, and cryptanalysis. He is the author of over 150 publications in the field and is a recipient of the 2014 ACM prize, the 2013 Godel prize, the RSA award in mathematics, and six best paper awards. In 2016 he was elected to the National Academy of Engineering.
