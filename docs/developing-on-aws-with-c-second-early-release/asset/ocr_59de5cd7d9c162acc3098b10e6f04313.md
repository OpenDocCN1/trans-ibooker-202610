The *N. Virginia* tab circled shows the AWS Region currently used. It is common to toggle to different regions to launch virtual machines or try services in another region. Often, but not always, new services first appear in the N. Virginia region, so depending on what region your organization is using, they may need to toggle to N. Virginia to try out a new service and then back again. Likewise, suppose a new service appears in a different region like US West (Oregon). You may find yourself trying that new service out in that region and then needing to toggle back to your primary region.

Finally, the *Recently visited* tab shows services recently used. After prolonged use of AWS, it is common to have most of the daily services appear in this section, providing a helpful shortcut menu to use AWS. Another thing to note is that you can “star” an item in the console to add it to the favorites tab, as shown in Figure 1-4. This process can be a convenient way to access services you use often.
