Handling duplicates, NULLs, and missing data is a fundamental part of preparing data for analysis and reporting. Duplicates can inflate counts or totals, leading to misleading insights. NULLs and missing data represent unknown or incomplete information that requires attention, and simply replacing them without understanding the context can negatively impact your data’s meaning.

In practice, it’s important to identify why data is missing or duplicated before taking action. This involves working closely with data owners, source system experts, or business users to gain a thorough understanding of the data.
