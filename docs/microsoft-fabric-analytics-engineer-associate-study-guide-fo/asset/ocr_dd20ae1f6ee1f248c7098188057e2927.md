If you can’t see the “Deployment pipelines” button, click the three-dot context menu at the bottom. Clicking either one will bring up the splash screen shown in Figure 8-32, unless you’ve already created deployment pipelines. In this case, you’ll see a list of them instead of the splash screen.

![](bbox=[0.22, 0.22, 0.76, 0.53])

Figure 8-32. Splash screen you’ll see if you haven’t created a deployment pipeline

The splash screen details the four steps you must go through to build your deployment pipeline:

+   1. New pipeline: create a new deployment pipeline.
+   2. Assign your workspace: select the workspaces to assign to each stage in the process.
+   3. Develop and test your content: do your daily work and develop the items and content you need to support your business requirements.
