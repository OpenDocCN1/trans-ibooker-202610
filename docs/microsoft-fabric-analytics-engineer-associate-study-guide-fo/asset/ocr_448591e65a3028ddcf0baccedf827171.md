On the “Deployment history” screen, you can see the following:

+   - What stage the items have been deployed to
+   - The date and time of the deployment
+   - Who made the deployment
+   - An icon you can click to view the list of items that were deployed
+   - An icon you can click to view the notes from the deployment (e.g., “Deploying from Development to Test stage,” per the figure)
+   - The unique ID (name) of the deployment
+   - The deployment status

You can also use the drop-down list at the top right to filter the history by selecting “All” or a specific destination stage.
