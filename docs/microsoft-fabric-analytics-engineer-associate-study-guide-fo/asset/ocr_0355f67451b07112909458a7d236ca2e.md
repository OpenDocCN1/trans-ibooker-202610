Creating relationships in Power BI is easy. You can simply click on a column, such as the Employee_ID column in the Dim_Employee table, and drag it into the corresponding column in the Fct_Sales table. Then, Power BI will automatically open a window where you can define the relationship in more detail. In this window, you can configure the tables and columns involved in the relationship, set the cardinality (such as One to many), and specify the cross-filter direction.
