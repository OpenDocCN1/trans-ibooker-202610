The first step is to choose the two tables you want to connect. When you drag and drop columns from one table into another, Power BI automatically selects these tables in the relationship editor. However, you may accidentally drop the column in the wrong place or realize you actually need to connect a different table. In these cases, you can manually adjust your selection.
