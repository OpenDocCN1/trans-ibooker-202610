It increases the number of relationships and joins in your model, which can make queries slower and the model harder to navigate and filter. That’s why the star schema is almost always preferred. It simplifies the data structure, improves query performance, and makes the model more intuitive for end users.

> **NOTE**
> While the star and snowflake schemas are the most commonly used ones for analytics, other modeling approaches exist and are typically used for other purposes, such as staging and operational systems.
