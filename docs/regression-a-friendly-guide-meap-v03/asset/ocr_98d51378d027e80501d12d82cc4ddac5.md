Recall that Length is the characteristic length of the species in centimeters, and weight is the characteristic weight of the species in grams. Now that we know about simple logistic regression, we’re going to build a model that relates the extinction risks for cartilaginous fish to their weights. To do that, we’ll first convert the factor Category, which has 5 levels and comes straight from the IUCN Red List, into the binary response variable Threatened as follows:

```
threatened = ["Critically Endangered","Endangered","Vulnerable"]

sharks["Threatened"] = sharks["Category"].isin( threatened ).asty
sharks = sharks.drop(columns = "Category")
sharks
```
