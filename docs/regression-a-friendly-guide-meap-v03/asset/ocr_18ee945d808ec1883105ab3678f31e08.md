Following the lingo of binomial experiments, patients with at least one chronic condition are Successes and patients without chronic conditions are Failures. (Weird, I know!) Percentage is, obviously, the percentage of patients of a given age with a chronic condition; we saw at the beginning of the last chapter that the logistic model based on this data fits these age group percentages well. Now, we’re going to change our approach and work with the covariate classes directly: for each age, the number of Successes is an observation of a binomial random variable.
