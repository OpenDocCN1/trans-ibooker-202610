The trend is clearly not linear, but what is this curve exactly and what do we do with it? Excellent questions! The blue S-shaped curve is the logistic function, and logistic regression consists of fitting this curve to binary response data. In this case, the height of the curve at a particular age estimates the probability, based on this data, that a randomly chosen person of that age will have at least one of the given chronic conditions. It’s important to understand that this probability really describes a group of people, not a single individual— it is the percentage of people of a certain age who have a chronic condition.
