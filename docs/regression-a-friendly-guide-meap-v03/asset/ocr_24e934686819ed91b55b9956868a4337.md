There are several things to notice about the plot in Figure 4.3. First, it does not seem reasonable to try to fit a straight line to these points, as we did with simple linear regression; we’ll see in a moment that most of these points fall on a special nonlinear curve, the logistic curve, that gives logistic regression its name. Second, there appears to be an important transition around age 40: people older than 40 are more likely than not to have a chronic condition, while people under 40 are less likely to have a chronic condition. This is just a preliminary estimate of the age at which this transition occurs, however, since grouping the data into age cohorts smoothes out some of the variation
