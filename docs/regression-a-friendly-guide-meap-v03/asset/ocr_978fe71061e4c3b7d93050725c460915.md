# 1.3 Is all that theory really necessary?

Absolutely![1] Think of it this way: you could easily go out and buy a chainsaw, gas it up, pull the handle, and start cutting trees with abandon, but there’s a good chance you’ll maim yourself if you don’t read the manual and get some experienced guidance from someone you trust. Similarly, anyone can start building regression models with Python or R in just a few minutes, but it takes much longer to learn to interpret and assess the results correctly. There’s obviously a much lower risk of personal injury if you misuse regression, but there can be significant consequences— bad business decisions or misguided policy choices, for example— if you don’t know what you’re doing. After hearing various people talk about regression over the years and reading lots of papers and blog posts about regression, there is clearly a need to educate people better on the subject and to disabuse them of some bad habits and misconceptions. Someone should really write a book to do just that!
