rate), so the closer the ROC curve gets to (0,1), the better the family of classifiers is. This is one way to interpret the AUC: the closer the AUC is to 1, the closer the ROC curve is, generally speaking, to the upper left corner. The threshold that produces the point on the ROC curve that is closest to (0,1) is a good candidate for the optimal threshold to use, but that depends on the context of the data and the relative pros and cons of mislabeling cases.
