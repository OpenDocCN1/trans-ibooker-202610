The coefficient of Photoperiod is $b = -5.5250$, with a standard error of $SE(b) = 0.900$. Following the procedures for computing confidence intervals that we went through in Chapter 3, we are 95% confident that the model parameter $\beta$ satisfies

$-5.5250 - (1.96*0.900) \le \beta \le -5.5250 + (1.96*0.900) \Rightarrow -7.29 \le \beta \le -3.76.$

Similarly, we are 95% confident that $\alpha$ satisfies

$74.5837 - (1.96*12.132) \le \alpha \le 74.5837 + (1.96*12.132) \Rightarrow 50.80 \le \alpha \le 98.36,$

since $a = 74.5837$ and $SE(a) = 12.132$. The multiplier 1.96 here is the 97.5% cutoff for the standard normal distribution, meaning that 97.5% of $Z$-scores are less than 1.96; equivalently, 95% of $Z$-scores are between -1.96 and 1.96.
