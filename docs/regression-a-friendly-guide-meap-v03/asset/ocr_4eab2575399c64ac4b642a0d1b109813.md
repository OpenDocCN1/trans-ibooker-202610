As expected, there aren’t many false negatives; by lowering the threshold, this classifier only assigns patients with a very low chance of being sick to the Condition=0 class, so most of those predictions are correct. On the other hand, lots of patients who are likely to be healthy are predicted to be sick, making the false positive rate a high 73.5%. Comparing the results for these three different thresholds (20%, 50%, and 80%), we see that we can’t expect to have both a high sensitivity and a high specificity; increasing one decreases the other, so we have to understand their relative merits. This is where input from domain experts is essential— in this example, we would need trained physicians to advise us on choosing the threshold, based on a combination of their experience and quantitative metrics.
