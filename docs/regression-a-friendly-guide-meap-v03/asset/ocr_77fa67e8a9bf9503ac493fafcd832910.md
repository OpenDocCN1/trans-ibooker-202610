The third version of $R^2$ that we’ll consider is called the *coefficient of discrimination*, which we’ll denote by $R^2_D$. Tjur proposed this statistic in 2009, starting with the perspicacious observation that a logistic model that performs well should assign large probabilities, on average, to the observed successes and small probabilities, on average, to the observed failures.
