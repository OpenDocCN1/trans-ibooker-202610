# Fish weights versus fish lengths

This looks a bit of a mess— most of the points are piled up along the horizontal axis, with a few stragglers trying to escape, and the scale for the weights is several orders of magnitude larger than that of the lengths. This is typical when working with body measurements of animals of different species, and the standard fix is to work with logarithms of the measurements instead of their natural units. If we plot the natural logs of the weights versus the natural logs of the lengths, we get a much nicer relationship:

```
>>> train = train.assign(LogLength = np.log(train.Length), LogWei
>>> sns.scatterplot(data=train, x="LogLength", y="LogWeight")
>>> plt.title("Log(Weight) versus Log(Length)")
```
