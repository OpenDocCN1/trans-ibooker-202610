| input[type="radio"]:checked | 0 | 2 | 1 | 021 |
|:is(input[type="radio"], input[type="checkbox"]):checked | 0 | 2 | 1 | 021 |

Since :is will base its specificity value to be the value of the most specific selector within it, in our particular case, its specificity will be 11 plus another 10 for the :checked, giving us a specificity of 21. Since 21 is greater than 0 we override the styles and our border becomes our accent color.

Our radio inputs and checkbox are now styled both when checked and unchecked, and on hover for both states. Figure 10.23 shows the finished product.
