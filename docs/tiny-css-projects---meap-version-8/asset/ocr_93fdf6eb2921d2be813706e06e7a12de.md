# 5.4.2 Inner container and content

Currently our text is not readable due to the dark text against the image background and also very close to the edge of the outer container.

To better our readability, we give our inner container a background color of rgba(0, 0, 0, .75) which is black with some transparency. We also change the text to whitesmoke and center it. By not using pure black or pure white in our design, we achieve a softer feel for our overall composition.
