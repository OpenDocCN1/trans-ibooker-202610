Again, for clarity, we use −_f to distinguish field subtraction and negation from integer subtraction and negation.

In F₁₉:

−_f 9 = (−9) % 19 = 10

which means that:

9 +_f 10 = 0

And that turns out to be true.

Similarly, we can do field subtraction:

a −_f b = (a − b) % p

where a, b ∈ F_p.

In F₁₉:

11 −_f 9 = (11 − 9) % 19 = 2
6 −_f 13 = (6 − 13) % 19 = 12

and so on.
