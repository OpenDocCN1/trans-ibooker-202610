# ### 5.4 Effectiveness Evaluation

**Metrics.** Although each experiment has different datasets and gold standards, we use the same metrics for all the experiments: *Precision*, *Recall*, and *F-measure*. *Precision* measures what proportion of the performed entity matches are actually correct. That is, *precision* is the fraction of RDF molecules that has been identified as contextually equivalent by COMET (C), which intersects with the Gold Standard (GS). On the other hand, *recall* measures the overall proportion of integrated RDF molecules that were identified correctly. That is, *recall* is measured by the fraction of correctly identified similar molecules with respect to the Gold Standard, i.e., **Precision = |C ∩ GS| / |C|** and **Recall = |C ∩ GS| / |GS|**. *F-measure* is the harmonic mean of *Precision* and *Recall*.
