**3.71** Suitably designed controls, if operating effectively, provide reasonable assurance of achieving the entity's principal system objectives. Suitably designed controls operate as designed by persons who have the necessary authority and competence to perform the controls. Entity management is responsible for designing and implementing controls to provide reasonable assurance that the entity's principal system objectives were achieved based on the applicable trust services criteria, identifying the risks that threaten the achievement of the principal system objectives, modifying the controls as necessary based on new and evolving risks, and evaluating the linkage between the controls and the evolving risks that threaten the achievement of the principal system objectives.

**3.72** Evaluating the suitability of the design of controls involves assessing whether the controls stated in the description adequately address the risks identified by entity management. This assessment is a two-step process:

+   1. Obtaining an understanding of and assessing the robustness of entity management's risk assessment process to identify, design, and implement effective controls
+   2. Assessing whether controls identified, designed, and implemented by entity management are suitably designed

**3.73** When evaluating management's risk assessment process, the practitioner does the following:

+   a. Obtains an understanding of entity management's risk assessment process, which includes the following:

+   i. Consideration of how entity management

+   (1) identifies and assesses the risks that threaten the achievement of the entity's principal system objectives based on the applicable trust services criteria
+   
+   (2) identifies information used by the system to produce, manufacture, or distribute the product and how it determines the threats to that information
+   
+   (3) incorporates information from its monitoring activities, including new threats and exploits, evidence about the operating effectiveness of controls that indicated there was a deficiency in the design of the controls, system events, and circumstances not previously considered
+   
+   (4) identifies its responsibilities for implementing controls that have been communicated by a supplier of goods or services (for example, customer responsibilities or CUEs identified in a supplier's contract)

+   ii. Evaluation of the frequency with which entity management updates the risk assessment and supporting processes and controls
