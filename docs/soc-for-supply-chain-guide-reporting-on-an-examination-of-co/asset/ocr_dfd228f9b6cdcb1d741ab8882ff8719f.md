As discussed in chapter 1, although a control deficiency may be identified during testing controls, and that deficiency may affect whether one or more applicable trust services criteria are met, the practitioner's opinion ultimately focuses on the effect of the control deficiency on the ability of the system of internal control, taken as a whole, to provide reasonable assurance that the principal system objectives are achieved based on the applicable trust services criteria.

# More Than One Control Addresses a Particular Risk

3.79 The entity may have more than one control that addresses a particular risk that threatens the achievement of its principal system objectives. In that situation, if the practitioner determines that one of the identified controls is not suitably designed, the practitioner would ordinarily consider whether the other control has been designed. If the other control has been suitably designed, the practitioner would test the effectiveness of that control. In addition, the practitioner would ask entity management to revise the description to exclude the control that is not suitably designed and include the control or controls that are suitably designed based on the applicable trust services criterion.

# Procedures to Obtain Evidence About the Suitability of Design of Controls

3.80 The practitioner evaluates the suitability of the design of controls by using evidence and other information gathered when

+   a. obtaining an understanding of the entity's system and the controls within that system and
+   b. determining whether the description of the system presents the system that was designed and implemented in accordance with the description criteria (including evidence obtained from performing walk-throughs)

3.81 To supplement such evidence and other information, the practitioner generally performs a combination of the following procedures:

+   a. Inquiry of entity personnel about the design and operation of applicable controls and the types of system events that have occurred or that may occur
+   b. Inspection of documents produced by the entity
+   c. Performing additional walk-throughs of control-activity-related policies and procedures
+   d. Reading applicable and supporting system documentation
