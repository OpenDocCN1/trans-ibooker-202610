As an example, assume a wholesale produce distributor claims it has effective system controls to meet system requirements that support its compliance with certain laws and regulations over food safety. However, the practitioner’s tests indicated that such controls were not effective, and entity management refused to revise the description. When considering whether the misstatement is material, the practitioner may consider the consequences of the failure to comply with such laws and regulations, which could involve both loss of reputation and monetary fines. Alternatively, because of the serious potential consequences of foodborne contamination on human health, the practitioner may consider a misstatement involving compliance with food safety regulations more significant to intended users than a misstatement related to matters other than food safety.
+   - Whether the misstatement is the result of an intentional or unintentional act.

3.65 The following are some examples related to materiality with respect to the description of the entity’s system:

a. Example 1. Example Entity uses a service provider to perform its back-office functions and elects to use the carve-out method. The description includes information about the nature of the services provided by the service provider and describes the third-party risk and control assessment activities and other controls performed at the entity with respect to the processing performed by the service provider. The description includes such information because it is likely to be relevant to intended users and, therefore, such information would be considered material to the description of the entity’s system.

In total, 80% of Example Entity’s shoes are manufactured in the Oregon facility, and 18% are manufactured in the Illinois facility. The Iowa facility manufactures less than 5% per year, and for the last three years has been averaging just 2% of shoe production. Thus, Example Entity management has described the Oregon and Illinois facilities in the description as being within the scope of the examination, but it has determined that the Hawkeyes system, which it uses in the Iowa facility, is not material to the overall manufacturing of running shoes.
