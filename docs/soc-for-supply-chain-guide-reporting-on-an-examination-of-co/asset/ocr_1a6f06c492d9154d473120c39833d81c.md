# Forming the Opinion and Preparing the Practitioner’s Report

# Elements of a Practitioner's Report — *continued*

| AT-C Section 205 Reference | AT-C Section 205 Requirement | Reporting Elements and Additional Guidance for a Practitioner’s Report on a SOC for Supply Chain Examination | Illustrative Practitioner’s Report Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| .63ei | A statement that identifies the responsible party and its responsibility for the subject matter in accordance with (or based on) the criteria or for its assertion | A statement that identifies the responsible party and its responsibilities, which generally include the following: 1. Establishing the entity's system objectives; 2. Identifying the risks that threaten the achievement of the entity's system objectives; 3. Designing, implementing, and operating effective controls within the system to provide reasonable assurance that the entity's principal system objectives are achieved; 4. Selecting the applicable trust services category or categories; 5. Preparing the description; 6. Stating the controls in the description; 7. Providing the assertion about the description and the effectiveness of controls stated therein | *Entity Management's Responsibilities* XYZ Entity management is responsible⁵ for establishing the system objectives, identifying the risks that threaten the achievement of the system objectives, and designing, implementing, and operating effective controls within the system to provide reasonable assurance that XYZ Entity's principal system objectives are achieved. XYZ Entity is also responsible for selecting the applicable trust services category or categories, preparing the description, and stating the controls in the description. XYZ Entity has provided the accompanying assertion titled *[insert the title of the attached entity management assertion]* (assertion) about the description and the effectiveness of controls stated therein. |
| .63eii | A statement that the practitioner's responsibility is to express an opinion on the subject matter or assertion, based on the practitioner's examination | A statement that the practitioner is responsible for expressing an opinion on the description and on the effectiveness of controls stated in the description, based on the practitioner's examination | *Accountant's Responsibilities* Our responsibility is to express an opinion on the description and on the effectiveness of controls stated in the description, based on our examination. |

⁵ As discussed beginning at paragraph 2.140, if entity management elects to use the inclusive method for a supplier, the supplier's controls would be included in the description and addressed by the practitioner's examination. In addition, the supplier would also be considered a responsible party, and additional language would be added to the practitioner's report to refer to the supplier's responsibilities.
