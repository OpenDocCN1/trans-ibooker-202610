# Overview of a SOC for Supply Chain Examination

**1.17** The practitioner performs a SOC for Supply Chain examination in accordance with AT-C section 105, *Concepts Common to All Attestation Engagements*, and AT-C section 205, *Examination Engagements*.¹¹ Those standards establish performance and reporting requirements for the examination. According to those standards, an attestation examination is predicated on the concept that a party other than the practitioner (the responsible party) makes an assertion about whether the subject matter is measured or evaluated in accordance with suitable criteria. An assertion is any declaration or set of declarations about whether the subject matter is in accordance with, or based on, the criteria.

> ¹¹ All AT-C sections can be found in AICPA *Professional Standards*.
