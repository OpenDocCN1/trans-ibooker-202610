+   a. If the practitioner has identified information that is inconsistent with the practitioner's final conclusions, how the practitioner addressed the inconsistency
+   b. If, after the date of the report, the practitioner becomes aware of facts that may have caused the practitioner to revise the report had they been known at the time of the report,
+   i. the circumstances encountered;
+   ii. any new or additional procedures performed, evidence obtained, and conclusions reached and their effect on the report; and
+   iii. when and by whom the resulting changes to the documentation were made and reviewed

# 3.195

As in other attestation engagements, documentation in the SOC for Supply Chain examination would ordinarily also include a record of the following:

+   a. Issues identified with respect to compliance with relevant ethical requirements and how they were resolved
+   b. Conclusions on compliance with independence requirements that apply to the engagement and any relevant discussions with the firm that support these conclusions
+   c. Conclusions reached regarding the acceptance and continuance of client relationships and attestation engagements
+   d. The nature and scope of, and conclusions resulting from, consultations undertaken during the course of the engagement
+   e. If the practitioner uses the work of the internal audit function, other practitioners, or the practitioner's specialists, documentation of conclusions reached by the practitioner regarding the evaluation of the adequacy of the work and the procedures performed on that work

# 3.196

Paragraphs .A117–.A119 of AT-C section 205 provide additional application guidance that might be helpful to a practitioner when deciding what to document in the SOC for Supply Chain examination.
