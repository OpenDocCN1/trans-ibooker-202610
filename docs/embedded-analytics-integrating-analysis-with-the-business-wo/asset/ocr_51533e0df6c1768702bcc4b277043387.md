Naturally, many other chart and graph derivatives and combinations exist, along with more esoteric chart types like radar charts, Isotypes, Sankey diagrams, and chord charts—all of which have specific use cases.

# Tabular Data

What do we mean by “tabular”? As an all-encompassing definition, we can start with “data presented in a table,” which for most of us means a spreadsheet, like an Excel document or a Google Sheet.
