Spreadsheet tools are no longer just for creating tables, and users have access to an enormous range of tools, platforms, and nascent technologies based on the concept of storing data in rows and columns. This doesn’t necessarily mean that tables are needed to form part of an effective embedded analytics solution, but with careful consideration and tool selection, embedding tabular content to provide additional context can provide users with options they expect (like downloading and exporting data). Providing a guided analytic experience is something that any solution architect should explore.
