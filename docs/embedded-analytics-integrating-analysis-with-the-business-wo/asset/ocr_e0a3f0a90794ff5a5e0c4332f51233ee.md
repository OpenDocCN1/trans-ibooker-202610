You're also highly likely to encounter tabular objects in CRM and ERP systems, like what's shown in Figure 5-10: an example of a Salesforce page that summarizes yearly sales performance figures. Note that there's a high level of precision for the aggregated figures in the bottom section. Whether or not this is useful to the viewer is a decision you need to make when using any kind of aggregated values shown in tabular format.
