# Binary and Hexadecimal

We should now take a quick gander at the two of the most relevant numeral systems in computer science and, naturally, in cryptography as well. It’s quite important to know how to interpret a value in any of the numerical systems mentioned in this section. If your math is sort of rusty, do read on and master the information; otherwise, you can skip this section.

All of us are familiar with the *decimal system*, sometimes also called the *base-ten system*. Decimal digits range between 0 and 9. This is a people’s system as computers prefer other types of numerals, as you will learn next.

*Binary* represents a numerical system with only two symbols: 0 and 1. Also known as the *base-two system*, binary is what digital devices use to function on the most basic level.
