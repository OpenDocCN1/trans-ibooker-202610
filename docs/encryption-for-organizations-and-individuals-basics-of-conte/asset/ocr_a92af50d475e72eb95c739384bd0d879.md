Polarization can be performed either on a rectilinear or diagonal basis in BB84 (see Figure 11-2). The sender side chooses which basis/approach to use for each bit and informs the receiver; this stage is called key sifting. Often a nonsecure conventional channel of communication is used with the BB84 for negotiation and sifting purposes.
