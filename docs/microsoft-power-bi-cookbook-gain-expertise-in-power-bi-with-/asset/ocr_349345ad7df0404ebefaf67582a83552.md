11. Add specific individuals or groups to have access to the app and set the permissions. It is recommended that you uncheck the settings under the **Allow everyone who has app access to** heading and instead control permissions at the dataset level. In addition, it is generally recommended to check the checkbox for **Install this app automatically** under the **Installation** heading.
