There is room for maneuver for socioeconomic appropriation of national accounting practices. It is all about representations, the propagation of concepts, the capture of these concepts, power games and therefore politics.

The map to which we refer has the particularity of being dynamic, made up of both social expression and digital networks, that of an information system in perpetual evolution. For Maurice and Reineix (2011), “the dynamic aspect of complexity imposes the idea of a topology that is not rigid, but can evolve, transform itself in time and space. In this drawing, networks appear as simple elementary physical supports whose arrangement can lead to a more complex and evolving hierarchical system. The concepts of emergence, of information transmission with an abstraction dimension not present in networks, have led to the idea of encapsulating these networks in a space that better represents this layer of information as a junction”.

This layer of information is not only shared between the actors, it does not only ensure the junction between them for the dissemination of *circulating information*, nor the only support for the emergence of *organizing events* in the sense of Morin. It is *Communication* in the sense of Luhmann (1978), for whom “it is not humanity, but a system of communication that selectively integrates the physical, chemical, organic and psychological potential of humanity and which, in the perspective of this selectivity, has its own reality and autonomy as a system”.

How to navigate then? The ultimate characteristic of a system being linked to the fact that it is constantly structured and reconstructed, following a differentiation from an environment that is specific to it (Bertalanffy 1968; Luhmann 1984; Le Moigne 1977), we could approach the institutionalization of the information system as an autopoiesis, which would lead us to consider a network of processes for producing *mediating artifacts* that is carried out by adaptive cycles in the topological space of territorial controversy maps.
