There is something more in the word *relationship* than in the word *link*, something more than temporary and compartmentalized utilitarian commitments. The relationship invites us to think of both cohesion and constraint; it invites us to approach the notion of limit no longer as a border but as a field of attractions and fragilities, no longer as a limit *state* but as a *regime* of functional dependence.

If *socioeconomic negentropy* then describes the *state* of cohesion of social, ecological and economic subsystems, even measuring the state of an “alter GDP” system anchoring growth in a pool of complementary indicators would only provide a picture, a snapshot, and therefore would not be sufficient to assess sustainability.

Montévil and Matteo (2015) reinterpret autopoiesis by emphasizing the interdependence between the parts of an organism and the historical nature of this interdependence. Historicity is then a causal regime just as structuring as the processes of renewal of the components of these parts. These ideas refer to the concept of *anti-entropy* structured by Longo and Montévil (2012, 2014).

Considering the closure of a system by the historicity of a functional dependency allows a step aside. It should be recalled that the monetary indicator is part of the *DMCS* approach in a very specific function: that of a tool for monitoring the quality of economic information. It is used to judge the coherence of the meta-information systems associated with it at different *monetarization localities*; to identify whether one of them is captured by the attraction of one of the *financialization singularities* that distort territorial space and time by dispelling both meta-information and the ability to represent it; and to produce a mapping of socioeconomic negentropy – a new layer to be built on the mapping of controversies when it comes to investing in cooperation mechanisms for sustainability.

The monetary indicator is not the “output product” of the *DMCS* assessment. It is one of the inputs. This upheaval is not “Copernican”. The *DMCS*’s output product is information on individual and collective representations of the sustainability of the territory. It tools the re-embedding of the economic into the social and democratic relationship, i.e. the political economy.
