# 3.1.2.2. Deliberate to establish agreements

The cooperative transaction changes both the plural value systems mobilized by the actors, and their individual representations of the objects they consider together. In other words, people’s experiential content and mediating artifacts are co-evolving during this transaction.

The four phases of a cooperative transaction, described above – virtualization, skills acquisition, performance, sanction – can be compared to those of the Holling adaptive cycle mentioned in Chapter 1 (see section 1.2.2.2).
