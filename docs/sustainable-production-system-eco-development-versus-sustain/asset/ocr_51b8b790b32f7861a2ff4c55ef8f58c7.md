The *articulation* embodies the functional ecosystem into a solution that is not only made up of products and services, but also of the materialization of the common institutional horizon resulting from the projection.

In other words, in a functional economy model, an institutional solution accompanies an organizational innovation and makes it possible to link the innovation niche to the regime that it makes favorable to the diffusion of this innovation.

> The Eurbanlab approach (Bosch et al. 2013) is of interest in this regard. It mobilizes a tool for benchmarking urban innovations whose performance is considered according to sustainability criteria that add the *process* and *propagation* of innovation to the environment, economy and social triplet.
> 
> *Process* performance refers in particular to the qualities of leadership, the adequacy of innovation in the context of its emergence, the level of cultures shared by the actors and their involvement in the different phases of innovation.
> 
> *Propagation* performance reflects the diffusion potential of the innovation, a potential linked to the characteristics of this innovation that are likely to change the territorial system from which it emerges.
> 
> In our opinion, this approach makes it possible to include in the evaluation of PSS offers the conditions for their recognition by other economic localities, i.e. the conditions for expanding the functional ecosystem.
