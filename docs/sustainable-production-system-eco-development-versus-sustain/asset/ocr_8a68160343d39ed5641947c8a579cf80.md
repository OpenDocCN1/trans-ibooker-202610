In a neoliberal industrial economy, the situation is different. This economy also has as its horizon the universality of the market and the exchange value as a standard of wealth, but there remains at the level of the individual an autonomous space of representation, that of production, where the use value intervenes.

Value judgments then put local tension on exchange and use in a social group that is no longer necessarily limited to market agents, but includes actors in the industrial economy who extend the scope of objectification of utility to the act of physical production. Individuals bring together at the level of the singular and the particular *knowledge* whose usefulness for development implies reconsidering the foundations of the “labor factor”.

An interface between two economic worlds then remains materialized by the relationship to the physical dimension of the productive apparatus. The particularities of this apparatus impose a distance from exchange by mixing in negotiations around the value concerns related to socioecological and economic non-market costs specific to the mode of production.

This interface structures spaces of representation that allow the existence of diversified socioeconomic communities, thus likely to open the field of a political economy, by crossing the views on the value of productive phenomena.

Let us then assume here that the diversity of communities led to consider the value of productions results in a diversity of views on the phenomenon of work, and that it is not homogeneous but composite – it is a *plural work*, of which we distinguish five components here:

+   - bio-physical-chemical phenomena related to the production and reproduction functions of living organisms are the result of *ecosystem-based work*;
+   - community and political phenomena related to the production and reproduction functions of life in society are the result of *social work*;
+   - psychosociological phenomena linked to the production and reproduction functions of the capacities for the recognition of socioecological phenomena are the result of *economic work*;
