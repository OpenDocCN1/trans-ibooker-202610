Air quality and comfort of use are, for example, inseparable from the emergence of another phenomenon – absenteeism – which by its obvious managerial materiality has a strong impact on investment choices. Extracting from these three phenomena only factorial information intended to be used in the definition of a market price, would make the understanding of their emergence impossible.

Lorentz’s approach, which is nothing more than an internalization of externalities, must be based on a systemic analysis of value, and this systemic approach must never be abandoned.

Productivity at work and the reduction in absenteeism, due among other things to increased comfort and the satisfaction of knowing health quality is taken into account, are to be compared with the total cost of occupying office buildings that have been renovated.
