# 1.2.2.2. Political economy and plural work

The critical thresholds whose attainment would result in the loss of the power of value recognition enjoyed by the political economy are fundamentally related to the weakening of the social functions that allow interactions between community spaces of representation.

The difficulty in articulating singular, particular and universal, mentioned by de Courcelles (2009), is no stranger to this problem.

The representation of wealth at the universal level is directly influenced by the influence exerted from the singular level by the mobilization of plural social value systems during the development and renewal of the political process. At the level of the individual, the interactions generated by the political process resonate with the dynamics of these social systems, leading to a phenomenon of creative differentiation of individuality and shared knowledge, which influences the redefinition of wealth at the universal level.
