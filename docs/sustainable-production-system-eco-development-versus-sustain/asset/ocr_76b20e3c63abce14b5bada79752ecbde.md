The territorial information system could then be considered as the vector of interpenetration by which:

+   - a local functional ecosystem would perceive the autopoiesis of the territorial functional ecosystem as part of its own system and thus consider territorial dynamics as determinants of its own performance;
+   - a territorial functional ecosystem would perceive the co-evolution of local functional ecosystems – each with autopoietic behavior – and then adapt the territorial valuation process accordingly;
+   - a monetary representation system of the valuation process would perceive a non-monetary representation system as part of its own system (microaccounting / multi-dimension analytical balance sheet; core meso accounts / satellite accounts );
+   - a non-monetary representation system would perceive a monetary representation system as part of its own system (need to finance the maintenance/renewal of support functions).

The institutionalization of the territorial information system would thus refer to one of the characteristics of autopoiesis: self-reference, which from a sociological point of view is for Luhmann (1981) “a result of the evolutionary differentiation of systems”.

However, we will refuse to retain from the concept of autopoiesis the first cognitivist formulations that refer to the vision of machinery that would ensure the identical reproduction of its own components and their only in what they are at a given moment. It would be the same mistake to consider a functional economy in which the service would exist without the functional heritage, i.e. forgetting the history of the relationships between components of the system.

This approach would indeed make it difficult to approach the territorial heritage, which we have certainly qualified as informative and cognitive, but also functional and to which we have often associated the proposal of Colletis and Pecqueur (2004) inviting us to integrate, beyond the links and with them, the time dimension, i.e. the relationship and its historicity.
