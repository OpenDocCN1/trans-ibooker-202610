The recognition path proposed by these methods consists of a nonlinear and recursive path consisting of four steps: (i) structuring of the decision problem by associating decision criteria with issues; (ii) articulating and model preferences; (iii) aggregating the alternative evaluations (preferences); and (iv) making recommendations (Guitouni and Martel 1998).

A multi-criteria analysis can be used to support a mathematical calculation associated with the valuation of ecosystem services to the economy, or the determination of a political price aimed, for example, at introducing a tax on polluting activities. But more fundamentally, it is intended to provide representations to inform the decision. Monetary representation, when it intervenes, is thus considered as a tool for the application of the decision – which remains the result of social representations.

The neoclassical economy is an economy that aspires to induce intelligible reality.
