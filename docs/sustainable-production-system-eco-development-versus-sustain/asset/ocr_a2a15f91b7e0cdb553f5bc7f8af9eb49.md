# Maintaining jobs

+   - Careers provide jobs, most of which are low skilled and non-relocatable⁷.
+   - The increase in needs, the exhaustion of stocks and the high use of imports are weakening employment in the Paris region quarries.
+   - Recycling would slow this increase and also contribute to the creation of non-delocalizable employment.

# Ensuring the safety and quality of the structures

+   - Competition between quality/safety of construction and preservation of the aggregate resource.
+   - Strong anchoring of a preference for virgin (“natural”) aggregates even when it does not correspond to a technical, normative or regulatory requirement.
+   - R&D in the various trades (builders, concrete and cement manufacturers, quarries): ensure the same quality of work with fewer virgin materials and support the development of new techniques related to recycling.

# Delivering the resource where it is needed

+   - Aggregate is a heavy but inexpensive resource so the share of transport in its final price is significant: this price doubles every 50 km. Massive transport would improve profitability.
+   - Need to take into account the configuration of the Ile-de-France region and the transport problems (road traffic, difficulties in crossing the capital via the Seine) – the supply must be made from all sides⁸.
