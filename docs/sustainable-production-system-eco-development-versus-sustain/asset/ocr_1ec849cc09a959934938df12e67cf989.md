Results of the second phase: Identification of levers that can be used to compose coherent packages of actions to be adapted to the specific situation of each community.

+   - Pedagogy lever: Relating to the needs of occupants (surveys initiated by local authorities, the results of which could be disseminated to landlords); accessibility, health and safety; measures related to the Grenelle de l’Environnement (green lease, decree on the tertiary sector); eco-actions (awareness of good practice and existing tools); future energy costs; green value; existing subsidies and schemes (direct subsidies, Energy Savings Certificates (ESCs), Energy Performance Contracting (EPC), etc.).
+   - Urban planning lever: Relating to a possible Floor Area Ratio bonus for building renovations (linked to performance or a label); to the authorization of change of use of buildings in the event of renovation; to mobility (improving mobility on the territory and optimizing employee mobility); to actions on urban planning tools and on the Climate Plan.
+   - Incentive lever: Relating to the promotion and support of third party financing initiatives (enhancing and pooling ESCs, facilitating and guaranteeing shared EPCs, etc.); to support the establishment of a single financing window, as well as an SEM (société d’économie mixte – mixed economy society); the creation or strengthening of partnerships between banks and project owners; the implementation of a public investment policy, subsidies, bonuses; the implementation of “lobbying” actions (with the region, Europe, etc.).
+   - Service lever: Relating to the development of territorial approaches to support project owners (Shared Energy Advisor at intermunicipal level and/or presence in the vicinity of the territory of a Local Energy Agency, specialized in energy efficiency); to support the emergence of a collective intelligence (mobilize business associations, define a matrix of skills on the territory, identify and create possible synergies, etc.); to propose a mediator role (encourage the establishment of new SMEs within their territory); to strengthen services within the territory.
