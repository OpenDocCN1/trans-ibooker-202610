It is here that the relationship of *spatial proximities* to the space of recognition of values is recast.

We then quite naturally consider the *acceleration value* as relative to the concretization of the *territory* in the sense of Colletis et al. (2005). Spatial, organizational and institutional proximity are only one potential of this territory which, it should be recalled, must “be revealed in a situation of coordination, ranging from the resolution of a problem encountered by an actor located in time and space to a territorial development project bringing together the time horizons of actors involved in this triple proximity” (*ibid.*).

*Institutional proximity* between actors is both the outcome and the condition for *political deliberations* that drive the release of the informative and cognitive “resources” necessary for the *concerted specialization* of actors. These deliberations are therefore functionally linked to the *cooperative transactions* that accompany the co-implementation of *PSS-type* offers, and guide the *industrial structuring* of the territory – thus renewing both the relationship to *organizational proximity* and *spatial proximity*. They make it possible to establish the relationship between the geography of needs and activity products, i.e. on the one hand, the *mapping of the physical anchoring of the matter-energy flow*, and, on the other hand, the *territorial inscription of controversies maps* relating to the impact and control of this flow.

This *situation of territorial coordination* between political and economic activities, of the re-embedding of the economy in the social and political spheres – therefore of *political economy* in the sense of Polanyi – may or may not be covered by a formal contract. In all cases, it leads to the description of new actors, objects and productive phenomena identified as strategic for sustainable development. The changes in the procedures and methods of conducting activities, to which Bounfour and Epinette associate an innovative value of the new IS, are, in our opinion, more broadly part of an *institutionalization value*. They reflect the ability to practice a new integration of the micro-, meso- and macroaccounting and extra-financial systems, i.e. the territorial capacity for economic *Communication*.
