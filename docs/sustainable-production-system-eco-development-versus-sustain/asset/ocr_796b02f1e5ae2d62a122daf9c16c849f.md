This situation is not inevitable. Other relationships between economics and the practice of information and currency are possible. Some may be frightened by our Marxian approach of *incorporating a collective work of representation* when analyzing the value of productive phenomena, or by an institutionalist vision in which *money is a social relationship*. Let us invite them to carry out a similar process with more *mainstream*, more “orthodox” tools of inspiration.

Organizational management commonly refers to congruence, i.e. the *strategic alignment* of ISs (Liavari 1992; Henderson and Venkatraman 1993; Bergeron et al. 2001, 2004). And the notion of *co-alignment* of IS governance and activity governance (Bergeron et al. 2004) would thus influence a *co-variation* between performance, business models and IS (Venkatraman 1989; Bergeron et al. 2001). For Henderson and Venkatraman (1999), a link should be established between the number, power and types of technologies; systemic skills to support the entity’s strategy; and governance of availability and IS capabilities (Boufou r and Epinette 2006).

Our DMCS approach indicates how to measure a co-variation between the collective performance of the processes of maintaining the sustainability of the territory, the business models that contribute to this maintenance and the ISs. To do this, it is not the number, power and type of technologies that we have referred to when we compare them to governance and IS capabilities. It is to the diversity, the importance and the consistency of the representations mobilized by the actors – both in the exercise of the profession specific to each one and in their relationship to the common horizon – that drives the governance of the territory: the search for sustainability.

The evaluation of the representation capacities offered by the territorial IS is carried out by establishing the *informative and cognitive balance sheet* following the measurement of the *triple heritage identity*, which is a “signature” of the local anchorage of this IS. This approach, which very directly feeds strategic alignment, is also a prerequisite for determining the level of *micro–meso–macro co-amortization* of assets in the *negentropic decoupling balance sheet*.
