The other end of the culvert was stuck into the emergency door of a school bus. The long vehicle was buried in the pile of cars. Couches replaced most of the bus seats, a dining table and chairs were pushed against one wall, and a laptop computer blinked on a table in front of a sofa. Long ago, the kids had snaked an electrical cord to their sanctuary from a nearby hydro pole.

Chickadee took a Wi-Fi adapter from her pocket and plugged it into her computer’s USB port. In a moment, she was searching for the doctor’s name.

“Troy Pixton…Troy Pixton…Troy Pixton…come to me, Troy Pixton.”

The boys giggled.

“Shhh!” Chickadee scolded. But she broke into a grin as her attention shifted back to the screen.

The boys moved farther into the bus.

“Probably not the cricket player…. Wow! A lot of people know him,” she cried. “Probably not the psychiatrist from the States.”
