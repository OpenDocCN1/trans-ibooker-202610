“This theft of our history and our resources and our ceremonies has to stop! Hey, little cousins?” Denice’s sentences always seemed to start out angry but ended with a smile. The Muskrats loved her dearly as she was fiercely protective of her family, especially the younger ones. Denice was passionate about everything she did. She always made everyone feel cared for, but they knew it was dangerous to get her angry.
