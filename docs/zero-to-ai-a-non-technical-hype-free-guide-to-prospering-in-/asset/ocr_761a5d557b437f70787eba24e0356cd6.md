Notice that because both width and depth contribute to the complexity of a task, we can say that the overall complexity of the task is proportional to their product:

$$Complexity = Width \times Depth$$

If we draw a graph with these two metrics on the axes, we get an effective way of visualizing the complexity of NLP tasks. Tasks that form a rectangle with a large area are more complex than tasks with a smaller area, as shown in figure 5.1.
