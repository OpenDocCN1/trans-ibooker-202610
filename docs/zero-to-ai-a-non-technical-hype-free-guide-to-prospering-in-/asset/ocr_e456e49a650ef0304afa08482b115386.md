# How this book is organized

Because you’ll need two fundamental sets of skills to bring AI into your organization, this book is also divided into two parts covering 10 chapters.

Part 1 is about understanding AI. Because modern AI is built on data, each chapter in this section introduces you to different types of data and the AI tools best suited for each:

+   - Chapter 1 is a brief introduction to the history of AI and the innovations that sparked the AI revolution of the 2010s.
+   - Chapter 2 is about the data produced by core business operations, and how AI can be used to build unique products and services on top of it.
+   - Chapter 3 takes a deeper look at AI applications for sales and marketing.
+   - Chapter 4 introduces AI models that can understand, produce, and transform media such as images, video, and audio.
+   - Chapter 5 presents AI algorithms for understanding and producing written text.
+   - Chapter 6 demonstrates models for recommending personalized content to humans.
