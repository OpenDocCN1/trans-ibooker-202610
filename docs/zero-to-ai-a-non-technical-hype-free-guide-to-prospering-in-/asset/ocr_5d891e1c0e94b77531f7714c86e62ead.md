This dataset has more than 14 million images, each labeled with one of around 20,000 classes, including a few hundred breeds of dogs and types of food. Traditional computer vision approaches topped out at around 75% accuracy in the early 2010s. Considering that the average human is 95% accurate, that wasn’t too great. Deep learning crushed this record, surpassing humans in 2015 before the competition was finally closed in 2017 with 97.3% accuracy (see figure 4.4).

Why is deep learning so powerful, and what price are we paying for this newfound power? The rest of this chapter will make that clear.
