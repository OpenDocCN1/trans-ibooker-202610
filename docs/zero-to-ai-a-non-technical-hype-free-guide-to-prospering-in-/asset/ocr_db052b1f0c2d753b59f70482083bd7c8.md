# 5.3.5 Natural conversation

In all our efforts to get closer to the brokerbot, we always relied on human-generated text, and were happy to create algorithms that made it easier for people to access that information. A natural improvement would be a model that can generate language on its own, instead of cobbling together premade sentences.
