Most organizations that are just starting out in AI believe that the key to success lies in advanced algorithms or Big Data. Our consulting experience shows that this philosophy is flawed: sophisticated technology is never a free pass for business success. As AI technology becomes more widely available, the real leaders of the upcoming AI wave won’t be Data Scientists or researchers. Instead, the keys for long-term impact are in the hands of the enlightened group who understands business and at the same time speaks enough technology to develop a consistent and achievable AI vision for their organization.
