# About the Authors

**David Sweenor** is senior director of product marketing at Alteryx, an international speaker, and acclaimed author with several patents. He is a specialist in the business application of AI, ML, data science, IoT, and business intelligence, and is responsible for the go-to-market strategy for Alteryx’s data science and machine learning portfolio.

With over 20 years of hands-on business analytics experience, Sweenor has supported organizations including SAS, IBM, TIBCO, Dell, and Quest in advanced analytic roles.

**Melissa Burroughs** is director of technology solutions marketing at Alteryx, where she crafts messaging and positioning strategy for the Alteryx portfolio in the context of multi-vendor solutions. She is an accomplished technologist, writer, and public speaker, bringing a passion for communication together with diverse experience in science and analytics.

Over 15 years in the software industry Melissa has originated RAD development, professional services, and product marketing for a number of early-stage technology firms. Her prior work also includes accelerator physics research, medical data analytics, and other academic positions.
