experience indicates that both under-differencing and over-differencing can easily result in serial correlations in the residuals. Thus, proper residual analysis can detect mistreatment of strong serial dependence.

Finally, some recent investigations on modeling high-dimensional unit-root time series include Zhang et al. (2019), who investigate identifying cointegration via eigenanalysis, and Gao and Tsay (2020), who study modeling high-dimensional unit-root time series.
