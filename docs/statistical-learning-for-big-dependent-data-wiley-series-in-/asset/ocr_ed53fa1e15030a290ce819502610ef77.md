Second, consider DL. We standardize the predictors of the training subsample so that each predictor has mean zero and variance 1. We then transformed the predictors of the testing subsample based on the means and standard errors of the predictors in the training subsample. Details of the transformation are given in the attached R demonstration. We have tried some DL models and the best model we obtained is as follows: It is a NN with six layers. The first layer is dense with 64 neurons, which has 46,912 parameters. The second layer is a dropout with rate 0.4, and the third layer is also dense with 16 neurons. This is followed by another dropout with rate 0.25. The fifth layer is a dense layer with four neurons, and the final output layer has a single neuron with simple linear activation function. All other dense layers employ the ReLU as the activation function. Overall, the network has 48,025 parameters.
