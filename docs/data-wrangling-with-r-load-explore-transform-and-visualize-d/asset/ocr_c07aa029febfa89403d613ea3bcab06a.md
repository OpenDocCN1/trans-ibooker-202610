The algorithm calculates an anomaly score for each record in the dataset. Negative scores are considered an anomaly and higher scores suggest the sample to be non-anomalous. While it is tempting to remove all samples that are classified as anomalies, sometimes, based on the machine learning problem and the dataset, it may be useful to leave the anomalies in the training dataset. In our example dataset, we can see certain records with low anomaly scores, but no negative values. We will leave these samples as part of the training data.
