While this provides us with the ability to easily apply changes, and therefore implements the idea of a *Bridge*, there is one more step that we can take, to further decouple and to reduce duplication. Let us assume, that we are not just interested in electric cars, but also in cars with combustion engines. It stands to reason, that for every kind of car that we plan to implement, we are interested in introducing the same kind of decoupling from engine details, i.e. the same kind of *Bridge*.
