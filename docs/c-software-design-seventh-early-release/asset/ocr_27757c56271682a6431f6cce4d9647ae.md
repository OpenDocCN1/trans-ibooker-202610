remained. In particular, the shape classes were still forced to deal with the draw() operation, although, for coupling reasons, it is undesirable to deal with the implementation details. Additionally, and most importantly, the Strategy approach proved to be a little impractical for extracting multiple, polymorphic operations. In order to further reduce coupling and to extract polymorphic operations from our shapes, we are now continuing this journey and take the separation of concerns principle to a completely new, potentially unfamiliar level: we are separating the polymorphic behavior as a whole. For that purpose, will apply the External Polymorphism design pattern.
