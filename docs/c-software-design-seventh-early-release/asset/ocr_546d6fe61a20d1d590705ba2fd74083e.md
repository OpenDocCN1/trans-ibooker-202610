8 The rules when a compiler will generate these two copy operations are beyond the scope of this book, but here is a short summary: *every* class has these two operations, meaning they always exist. Either they have been generated by the compiler, or you have explicitly declared, or even defined, them (potentially in the `private` section of the class or via `=delete`), or they are implicitly deleted. Note that deleting these functions does not mean that they are gone, but `=delete` serves as a definition. As these two functions are *always* part of a class, they will *always* participate in overload resolution.
