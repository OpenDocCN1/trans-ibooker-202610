What is software design? And why should you care about it? In this chapter I will set the stage for this book on software design. I will explain software design in general, help you understand why it is vitally important for the success of a project and why it is the one thing that you should get right. But you will also see that software design is complicated. Very complicated. In fact, it is the most complicated part of software development. Therefore I will also explain several software design principles, principles that will help you to stay on the right path.

In “Guideline 1: Understand the Importance of Software Design”, I will focus on the big picture and explain to you that software is expected to change. Consequently, software should be able to cope with change. However, it is much easier said than done, since in reality coupling and dependencies make our life as a developer so much harder.
