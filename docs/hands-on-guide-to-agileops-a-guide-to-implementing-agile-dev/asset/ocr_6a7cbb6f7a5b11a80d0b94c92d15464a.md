# Infrastructure as Code Example

Let’s look at a scenario where an engineer needs to commission a new VM. They access the ITSM tool like ServiceNow and look for the catalog items that allow them to submit a requisition for provisioning a VM. As soon as the request is approved, the related infrastructure pipeline is autotrigged and sets up the VM without needing any manual intervention. Earlier such requests took two to three weeks of time, but now they just take an hour. Many organizations have realized such benefits and aim to first standardize and automate the basic infrastructure needs that do not need manual inputs. The workflow in Figure 8-4 shows how users are empowered to commission and decommission VMs through the ITSM tool. Once their requests are approved by the approvers, the request is directed to orchestration tools like Jenkins that trigger the respective job.
