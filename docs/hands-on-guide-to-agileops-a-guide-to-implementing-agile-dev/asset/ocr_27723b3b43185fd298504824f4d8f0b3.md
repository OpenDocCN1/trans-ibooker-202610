# The Agile Manifesto: Simple Guiding Principles

The Agile Manifesto was written in February 2001 by a group of software and methodology experts. The manifesto is simple to understand. It states the agile values mentioned in Figure 1-4. It emphasizes that the values mentioned on both the sides of Figure 1-4 will exist; however, more focus and effort should be put on the values mentioned on the left side. Like individuals and interactions, processes and tools both will exist, but more focus should be given to establish interactions with individuals than processes and tools.
