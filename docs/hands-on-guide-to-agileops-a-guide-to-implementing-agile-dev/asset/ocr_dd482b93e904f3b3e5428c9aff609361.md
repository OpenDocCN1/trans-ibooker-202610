There are two scaling angles for DAD.

*Tactical agility at scale:* This address scaling for team factors such as size, geography, complexity of project, etc., through the application of processes and standards.

*Strategic agility at scale:* This address scaling for different areas of the organization through the agile and lean strategies.

Thus, the organization can decide which DAD scaling method is required as per applicability.
