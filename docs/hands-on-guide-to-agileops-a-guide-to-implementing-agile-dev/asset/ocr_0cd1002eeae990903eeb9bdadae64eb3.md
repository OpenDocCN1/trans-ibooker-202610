By defining these WIP limits, teams get to focus on what needs attention and prevent tasks from accumulating at any step. It allows teams to know their capacity as well. This mode of working also highlights inefficiencies and bottlenecks. These values should be frequently revisited to see if they need updating.

In the beginning, the ops teams may have separate backlogs for each of the areas like and Windows, Database, Backups, and slowly as teams mature and get used to these practices, the backlogs and teams can be combined (one common backlog), thus leading to cross-functional teams. As teams upscale, they should also do retrospectives themselves and update their ways of working in case things are not functioning as planned.
