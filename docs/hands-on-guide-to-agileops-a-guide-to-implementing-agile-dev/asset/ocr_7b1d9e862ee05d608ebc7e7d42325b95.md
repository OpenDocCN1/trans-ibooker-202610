Based on our assumptions, the state “In development” has a VA value of three days. We will divide it from the total VA value, which is five, and the value turns out to be 60 percent. This indicates that 60 percent of the total time is needed or will spent here in this state of the lifecycle. Similarly, we do it or others as well, and you will see that the reviewing stage has a value of 10 percent, the in-testing state was a value of 20 percent, and finally the deployment stage state has a value of 10 percent. On adding all these values, it should sum up to 100 percent.
