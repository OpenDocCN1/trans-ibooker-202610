Note Teams can succeed in Kanban adoption when they are comfortable with the basic concepts of workflows, lanes, and types of tickets, and can practice the culture of “pulling” work rather than work being pushed to them. A Kanban board is a dynamic visualization of the team workload and helps teams to plan and work in an effective way.
