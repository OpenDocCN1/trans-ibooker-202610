# Questioning Strategy

Understanding how each of the question types fit into an overall plan makes each question you ask a building block for the next.

+   - **Think before you open your mouth, no matter what style of questioning you decide to use.** If you have a lot of questions to ask but your brain hasn't prepared them properly, your strategy will fail; you will not have good flow that your target can follow, and more importantly, you will end up chasing butterflies.
+   - **You can deliberately use a splatter pattern and ask questions that seem all over the place, but ultimately elicit the information you need.** This method is ideal if you are concerned that you will not get straight answers from linear, well-directed questioning that shows your intent.
+   - **Ask the “next question.”** Don't ask, “Are you married?” Ask, “What's your wife's name?” Use common sense, though. You can look like a fool if you ask the wrong next step.
