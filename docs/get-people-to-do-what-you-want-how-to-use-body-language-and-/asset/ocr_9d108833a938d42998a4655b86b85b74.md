+   - *Differentiating through isolation.* It is often difficult to have someone seem special in a hostile pool or one populated by uniquely talented fish. In some cases, a super-typical has used precisely the same talent that an average guy in the room possesses to rise to the level of supertypical.
+   Unless the supertypical is well balanced, she may see the new guy as a threat and intimidate him. His safety net is having the ability to enter a smaller pool where he can comfortably belong. If you make that happen, you create a buffer between the new guy and the paranoid supertypical. Ways to do this can be a contrived meeting change that inadvertently removes the supertypical from the environment, or an invitation to the new guy to participate in something of which the threatened supertypical would never be. Both of these isolate your target to allow her to shine.
