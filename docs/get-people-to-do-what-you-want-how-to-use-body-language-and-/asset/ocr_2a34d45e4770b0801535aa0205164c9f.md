# What Makes Johnny Run?

What did this person decide to load onto his “system”? Once the programs are loaded, where does this person excel? What does he do in a middling way, or where does he fall below the norm? Which of these things causes him pride, and which causes shame?

Understanding all the aspects of the character and all the factors that affect self-image can tell you why the person behaves the way he does, and where he has areas of strength and weaknesses. Once you plot the information about each trait on your mental bell curve, you put them together to compose a 3-D image of where this person is average or typical, where he falls below the norm, and where he stands out above the crowd. This visual representation allows you to define the whole person relative to others in the population with which he identifies.
