# Conclusion

The goal of this chapter has been to highlight the importance of feedback for leaders. Although we typically think of leaders as feedback givers, it is equally important to consider how effective leaders seek and receive feedback themselves. They do so in order to continuously learn and develop. We have emphasized how formal mechanisms or programs, such as coaching and 360-degree feedback, can be used to help leaders both receive and give feedback.
