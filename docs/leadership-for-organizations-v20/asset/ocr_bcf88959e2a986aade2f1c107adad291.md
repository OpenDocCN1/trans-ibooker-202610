Fourth, politically savvy leaders are adept at showing apparent sincerity. The term 'apparent' is significant here. What is really important is that the leader comes off as seeming to be sincere in his or her relationships and transactions. But over time, if the leader is not genuinely honest and forthright, others will see through him or her, and the leader will damage relationships and the ability to influence. That is, the leader will be potentially seen as manipulative. For example, a global bank was struggling and appointed a new leader. He was initially seen by others as charismatic. He was a great orator and talked with passion about the importance of people, both employees and customers. Initially, people were excited and energized by his bold vision for the institution. He seemed sincere in his claims. But over time, it became apparent that he was sincere only about advancing his own career. Within a year, the employees turned against him, feeling deceived. In the end he failed completely to turn the bank around and was replaced.

There are connections between a number of traits or characteristics that were considered in Chapter 2 and political savvy. For example, honesty and integrity (or at least their appearance) are important characteristics if a leader is to be both politically savvy and effective. But over time, if the leader's "apparent" sincerity seems fake or inconsistent, the leader will come off as disingenuous, and thus, lose influence. In a similar manner, the leader might appear to be seeking personalized, rather than socialized, power. Inevitably, people who the leader is seeking to influence will not react favorably to his or her attempts at political savvy. As another example, emotional intelligence and political savvy appear to go hand-in-hand. It is hard to imagine that a leader who lacks emotional intelligence would somehow be able to show political savvy. In sum, the traits that we considered in Chapter 2 go hand-in-hand with the effective, or ineffective, use of political savvy. In Section 4, there is a survey to help you gauge your own political savvy.
