# 2.4 Discussion Questions

+   1. Narcissism on the part of leaders could be considered as a bad thing, since it tends to involve a high degree of self-absorption, need for adulation from others, and extreme (or over) confidence. On the other hand, for people in leadership positions, there are some positive aspects to narcissism. What are these positive aspects? How might it be possible, and even necessary, for a person to paradoxically show both narcissism and humility?

+   2. What are some historical examples of leaders who were "rebels with a cause"? Why do they fit that label?

+   3. Why might having a high need for power actually be a good motive for leaders, depending on how the power is used?

+   4. After taking the survey in Section 5, what component of emotional intelligence do you personally find to be the most challenging for you at times? What might you do to be better at this component?

+   5. If you could go through a neurofeedback process to make you a more effective leader, would you do it? Why or why not? If you have your doubts, what would you want to know to lessen your concerns?
