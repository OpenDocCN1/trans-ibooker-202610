# Conclusion

In this chapter, we have focused on the challenges faced by both majority and minority (or female) leaders who are attempting to lead in increasingly diverse work contexts in North America. Although the nature of these challenges largely differ, they highlight the importance of considering diversity in attempting to understand effective leadership. In addition, we should note that although most of the examples presented here involve female leaders, phenomena such as the glass ceiling and glass cliff are also relevant to various ethnic groups and races. In the next chapter, we continue with considerations of diversity. However, our focus will turn to the international context and global leadership.
