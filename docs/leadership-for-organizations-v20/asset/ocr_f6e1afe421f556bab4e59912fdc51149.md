# Conclusion

Ethical, moral, and responsible forms of leadership are clearly important, and it is on the minds of leaders, followers, and society as a whole. As we have seen in this chapter, issues pertaining to these aspects of leadership are both involved and sometimes not clear. There are a number of actions that leaders can take to demonstrate such leadership. But at the same time, leaders have choices to make that end up shaping their approach to ethics, morality, and responsibility in the workplace. Overall, this topic is likely to become even more important in the coming years.
