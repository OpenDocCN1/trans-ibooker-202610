# Conclusion

Inspiration and vision get at the core of what effective leadership is all about. Inspiring/visionary leaders wield a lot of power to influence others. But the most effective ones use that power to benefit others, rather than themselves. Some people might argue that at its most basic element, leadership is really about change. This chapter illustrates what powerful missions and visions are all about, and sets the stage for thinking about how leaders can help to shape organizational culture and implement change, which are the respective subjects of the next two chapters.
