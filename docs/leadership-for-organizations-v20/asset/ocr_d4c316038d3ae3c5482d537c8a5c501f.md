# Conclusion

If you put this chapter together with the prior one, the message should be clear. Effective leadership processes are not just about the leader and his or her behavior. It is also about followers. Followers need to be effective by thinking independently and critically, being highly engaged in their work and the organization, and by showing loyalty to the leader. They must also manage up by accepting the feedback and direction offered by leaders, working with (rather than against) the leaders' styles and goals, and role modelling the behavior that is desired. Furthermore, there are times that effective leaders need to be able to manage sideways; that is, influence peers.
