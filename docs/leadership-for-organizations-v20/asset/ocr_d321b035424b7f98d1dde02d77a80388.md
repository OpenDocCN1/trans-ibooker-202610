basic idea of a flexible approach to situational leadership is that the leader should alter his or her leadership style to fit the needs of the situation. Several approaches to situational leadership were overviewed here. Together, they help to form a foundation for effective leadership. However, as also emphasized, situational leadership does not solve the entire riddle of what it means to be effective as a leader.
