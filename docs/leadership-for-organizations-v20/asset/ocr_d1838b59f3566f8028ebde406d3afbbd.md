# Interpretation

For each of the three dimensions, a score of 0 or 1 should be considered low; a score of 2 or 3 should be moderate; and a score of 4 should be considered high.

For the total score, a score of 4 or less should be considered low on effective followership; a score from 5–8 should be considered moderate on effective followership; and a score of 9–12 should be considered high on effective followership.
