# Video Case for This Chapter

The video case for this chapter shows a leader dealing with cultural norms that are different from her home culture and personal values, while simultaneously being sensitive to how business is done in another culture.

# Nicole Chang—Building Bonds

Nicole Chang talks about her experiences as a Western-based executive who spent much time in China doing business. She specifically considered how so much of her business transactions were actually pursued after regular business hours in restaurants and “spas,” and how much of this activity occurred in the context of heavy alcohol consumption. In the video, she describes her discomfort not only because she is not a heavy drinker, but also because of her being a woman in an environment that is primarily male. She comes up with some creative ways of dealing with her Chinese counterparts.

# Conclusion

Leading in a global context presents special challenges for leader effectiveness. In this chapter, we first considered what global leadership is all about. Then, using the example of American culture, we identified the importance of first understanding one’s own cultural background before we can accurately understand other cultures. We proceeded to describe how aspects of having a global leader orientation can help provide the basis of leading and influencing people from different cultures. We further cautioned against cultural misinterpretations and the accompanying negative attributions that can form toward people from other cultures.
