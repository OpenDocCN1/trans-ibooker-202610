# Conclusion

In this chapter, we have overviewed a range of traits and characteristics that are associated with effective leadership. They are largely psychological in nature, involving such things as personality, values, beliefs, and so forth. While traits are interesting and informative, we also have suggested that an identification of behaviors or behavioral styles can be helpful in our quest to understand leadership. Most recently, we have begun to dig deeper to identify structures in the brain as the source of leader traits, characteristics, and behaviors.
