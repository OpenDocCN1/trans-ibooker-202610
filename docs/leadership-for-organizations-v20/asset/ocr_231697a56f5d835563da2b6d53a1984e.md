# Conclusion

In this chapter, we have considered the importance of relationship building and maintenance for leaders. Effective leaders need to demonstrate consistently strong relationships with followers through leader-member exchange processes. But moreover, they also need to demonstrate favorable relationships with people who are not direct reports. They do so through their political savvy.

One thing that should be clear in this chapter is that as mentioned above, “it takes two to tango.” The role of followers in leadership processes is often forgotten or underestimated. However, in Chapter 4, the role of followers will come to the forefront.

+   2. How will a leader likely treat in-group members differently from out-group members in terms of the type of leader behavior described in Chapter 2?
