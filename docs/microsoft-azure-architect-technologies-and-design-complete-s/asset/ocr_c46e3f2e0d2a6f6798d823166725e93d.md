Regardless of a perceived inefficiency, NoSQL structures scale very well when the amount of data is large to gigantic in size—it scales so well that companies such as Facebook, LinkedIn, and Google all use NoSQL database structures to store and access their data. As you likely know, those companies earn money by analyzing and learning from the data collected from their services. Because the storage, analysis, and easy access to the data are fundamental to their business operations, they inherently favor the most effective and efficient data storage technologies.
