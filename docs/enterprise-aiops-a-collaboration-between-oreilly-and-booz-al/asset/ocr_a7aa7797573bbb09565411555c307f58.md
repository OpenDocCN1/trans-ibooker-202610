Recall that the AI Adoption Blueprint defined AIOps integration as the responsible integration of DataOps, DevSecOps, and MLOps to design, develop, and sustain an AI capability. Pipeline integration between the AIOps components is crucial to operationalizing and sustaining your enterprise AI system. Both DevSecOps and DataOps are complex disciplines that exist independently of AIOps but require unique consideration when they are applied to AI model development. When implementing MLOps, many organizations focus solely on model development and do not invest the time required to mature other processes around ML model development, a path that often leads to AI systems producing suboptimal results and failing to address business needs.
