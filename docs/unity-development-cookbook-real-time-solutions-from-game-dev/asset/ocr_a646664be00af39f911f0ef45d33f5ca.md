# Discussion

In Unity, rigidbodies usually compute their center of mass from their
colliders, but you can override it.

In real life, a car's center of mass is low because of heavy objects like the
transmission and engine being under the floor. The way we've implemented
it here (and how it's typically implemented in games) is less realistic but
makes for better gameplay.
