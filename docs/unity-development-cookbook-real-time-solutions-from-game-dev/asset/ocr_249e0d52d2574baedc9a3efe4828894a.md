# Solution

To create a dolly camera:

1. Open the Cinemachine menu, and choose Dolly Camera with Track.

This will create two objects: a camera and a track for it to follow. The camera will always be positioned somewhere on the track, though it can rotate freely to look at its Look At target. It will reposition itself along the track to be close to its Follow target.

2. Configure the camera’s Look At and Follow properties to refer to the object you want to track (Figure 8-19).

3. Select the Dolly Track, and configure the points that you want the track to go from and to. You can also create points between the start and finish to create a curving track (Figure 8-20).
