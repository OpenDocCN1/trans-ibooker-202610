Throughout my career I have had a respectfully indifferent relationship with .NET. I have nothing against it, I just have almost never needed it and have spent little time working on anything Windows-specific, which, for many years it was.

Back in the early 2000s, I did help initiate a .NET-based project with some coworkers to visualize scenarios simulating attacks on buildings. The idea was that by using real physics models of explosions we could marry visualization with possible outcomes based upon various protective measures. Security planners could consider different locations for physical barriers and run tests for their facilities involving explosives-laden vehicles. If they could only get so close, what kind of an impact on the building or people standing just inside the entrance might it have²?
