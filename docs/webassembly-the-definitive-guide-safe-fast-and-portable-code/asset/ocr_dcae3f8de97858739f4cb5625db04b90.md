What you end up with is a technology stack tracking the evolution of our industry ready to meet the needs of various runtime configurations and scenarios. Let’s quickly walk through the layers involved.

At the base of the stack is WebAssembly and WASI. From here we have a portable and secure architecture that will run in a wide number of runtimes and environments of varying capabilities and resources. Unlike previous sandboxed environments, this gives us the freedom of language choice and the benefit of high performance results. This base level is shown in Figure 15-7.
