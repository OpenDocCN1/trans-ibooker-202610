On the e-commerce website, users can browse products and add them to the shopping cart. When a user adds a product to their cart, the process shown in figure 12.7 is triggered. A number of things happen:

+   1. The item is added to the shopping cart.
+   2. A new total is calculated for the contents of the cart.
+   3. The recommendation and shopper tracking microservices are notified of the change to the shopping cart through the event feed on the shopping cart microservice.
