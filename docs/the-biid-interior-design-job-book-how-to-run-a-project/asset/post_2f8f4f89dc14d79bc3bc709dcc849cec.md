### F Production Information stage

**Stage description**

Stage F, Production Information, covers the completion of the contract documents (see ‘e Notes on contract documents’, page 144). which will be issued to tenderers for pricing and construction of the works.

**RIBA Plan of Work**

The RIBA Outline Plan of Work 2007 describes the key tasks for this stage as, ‘Preparation of detailed information for construction. Application for statutory approvals. Preparation of further information for construction required under the building contract. Review of information provided by specialists.’

**ID/10**

Stage F is an ‘Installation Management Stage’ on page a of the Schedule of Interior Design Services in ID/10.

**Tasks: Interior designer/ lead consultant**

When acting as the interior designer in a design team or when acting as lead consultant, the designer will carry out all of the following tasks (deliverables in bold):

- Develop the technical design drawings into Production Information drawings showing the construction of all elements integrating structure and services including details of all joinery, sanitary and electrical fittings sufficient to allow tenders to be prepared.
- Provide information to, discuss proposals with, incorporate and co-ordinate input of, other consultants into the Production Information.
- Provide information to the quantity surveyor for the preparation of a bill of quantities or works schedule for tendering.
- Present the Production Information (incorporating the above deliverables) to, and review options with, the client.

**Contractor**

If the designer is acting as contractor under Annex a of ID/10, then the work in this stage, f, together with stages g, Tender Documentation, and h, Tender Action, will be carried out and presented to the client at the end of stage h.

On smaller projects, stages f, g, Tender Documentation, and h, Tender Action, are often carried out in the same exercise.
