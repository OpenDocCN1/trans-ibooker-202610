# 2.4.3 the competence, performance, work, services, products or solvency of Other Persons appointed by the Client

Where the designer is practising in a professional capacity, he/she is not responsible for the performance of other consultants, contractors, etc. unless they are employed directly by the designer. Therefore it is important to ensure that these are employed by the client.

# 2.4.4 the accuracy of any survey made by the Designer unless the survey is specially commissioned by the Client as part of the Services.

Designers are not liable for surveys (including measured drawings) which they prepare for their own use in designing the project. However, if the work to be surveyed is extensive then the designer would be sensible to advise the client to employ professional building surveyors (whom the designer can recommend) under clause 3.2 below. It will be cheaper for the client than paying the designer to do it, and the quality of the survey is likely to be higher.
