
### Cost of Decorations

After 6 months from the date of acceptance of the tender, the rates quoted in the priced specification or tendered schedule of Decorations shall be revised in accordance with the Consumer Prices Index.

### FF&E storage

If the Client instructs that all or any of the goods and/or materials comprising the FF&E are to be stored off the Site prior to installation, the Client shall reimburse the Designer or other supplier for any additional storage or other costs arising.

### Installation

Where the Designer as principal or a supplier or contractor is to install fixtures and fittings and/or Decorations (the ‘Work’):

The Client shall allow the Designer or such supplier(s) or contractor(s):
- (a) to take possession of those parts of the premises where the Work is to be carried out;
- (b) reasonable space to store materials and tools as necessary;
- (c) to use toilet and washing facilities, heating, water and electricity;
- (d) to carry out work from Monday to Friday inclusive between 08.00 and 18.00 hours.

The Interior Designer shall ensure that the Work:
- (a) is executed using skilled tradesmen appropriate to each task and by the specified completion date or any other agreed date; and
- (b) complies with any applicable statutory provision and, where applicable, the contract(s) include for giving all notices and paying any charges arising.

The relevant contract(s) shall provide that the Designer or a supplier or contractor:
- (a) shall be entitled to a reasonable extension of time for delay and costs arising for reasons beyond their reasonable control;
- (b) be liable to the Client after completion of the Work for six weeks for any faults in the Work excluding fair wear and tear

Where the Client is in occupation of the premises, the contract(s) shall provide that such supplier(s) or contractor(s) shall:
- (a) leave the property in a clean and tidy condition and clear away all rubbish arising;
- (b) be responsible for the reasonable security of the property if the Client is absent during the working day;
- (c) warn the Client of any dangers arising from the Work; and the Client shall not allow other people, particularly children, to be exposed to any dangers from the Work.

### Defects

The Client shall give notice in writing to the Designer
- (a) within 7 days of the date of delivery of any apparent defects in any item where the Client is responsible for receiving FF&E and the removal of packaging material;
- (b) within the relevant liability period on becoming aware of an apparent fault in any item of FF&E after it is taken into use, unless the fault is caused by accident or fair wear and tear.

### Guarantees

The Designer shall assign to the Client all available guarantees relating to the FF&E.

## Procurement of FF&E as agent

This section applies when the designer is procuring FF&E. The appropriate services in 'Part 2 Installation Management; FF&E installation' of the Services Schedule should be ticked. In this role, the designer can make an agreed handling charge but must not accept any undisclosed fee from others (e.g. the supplier) for these services (see Applicable case law: Eisenberg v Bachar, page 282). In this arrangement, the supplier is responsible to the client for the adequacy of the FF&E (but the designer must check that the supplier’s terms are reasonable).

### Accounts and payment

B3.2 The sum, which includes the Designer’s fees and expenses, payable to the Designer for the supply, delivery and/or installation of the FF&E is the amount stated in the tender, the contract sum, or any agreed variation to it.

The Designer shall notify the Client on becoming aware of any changes or potential changes to contract sum.

The Designer shall submit an account or accounts for payment in accordance with clause 5.15, including the amount or amounts due prior to delivery of specified items.

### Ownership

B3.3 FF&E supplied and/or installed by the Designer as principal under this clause B3 shall remain the property of the Designer until payment in full has been received by the Designer for all goods and materials supplied.

Until such payment is made the Client shall hold the FF&E as bailee on behalf of and in a fiduciary capacity for the Designer and the Client shall store the same in such a manner that they are clearly identifiable as the property of the Designer.

In the event of the Client failing to pay in full for the FF&E, or any part of it in accordance with these conditions, the Designer reserves the right to enter upon any land or premises owned or occupied by the Client to retake possession of goods and/or materials to the value of the unpaid balance at any time, without liability for any damage caused to the Client’s premises or possessions by obtaining access to and/or unfixing and/or removing of such goods.

### Liability

B3.4 FF&E supplied and/or installed by the Designer under this clause B3:
- (a) shall remain at the risk of the Designer until delivery to the Client is complete (including off-loading and stacking) at which point risk shall pass to the Client; except that
- (b) the Designer shall remain responsible for a reasonable period for the relevant item of FF&E for any faults, excluding fair wear and tear, after it is taken into use or its installation.

Any action or proceedings arising out of or in connection with the said supply and/or installation of FF&E whether in contract, tort and statutory duty or otherwise is commenced before the expiry of the liability period.

### Insurance

B3.5
- (a) The party named in Part 4 of the Services schedule shall insure in the joint names of the Client and the Designer the risks of loss or damage to the existing structures, to any contents including the FF&E and all unfixed materials by fire, explosion, storm, tempest, flood, bursting or overflowing of water tanks, apparatus or pipes, earthquake, aircraft and other aerial devices or articles dropped from them, riot and civil commotion until completion of the installation of the FF&E.
- (b) The Designer shall maintain public liability insurance for death or injury to people or damage to property until completion of the installation of the FF&E.
- (c) On the request of one party the other produces such evidence as may be reasonably required that the actions under paragraphs (a) and (b) above have been carried out.
