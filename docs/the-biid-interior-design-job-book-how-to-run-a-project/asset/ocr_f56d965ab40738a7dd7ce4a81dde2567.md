Dear Mike and Phyllis

Proposed Alterations to 42 Supernova Heights

Following Durr Construction Ltd’s execution of two copies of the construction contract documents, I enclose two copies of:

+   building contract MW 05 completed for execution drawings nos 123.02/1-21 specification sanitary fittings schedule finishes schedule electrical fittings schedule Durr’s priced schedule of works.

Please execute the agreement under hand by signing and having witnessed in the appropriate place on the attestation page in both copies of MW 05 (initialling where indicated) and initial in both copies of all of the other documents. Then return all of the above to us so that we can return one set to Durr and keep your copy on our file.

You will remember that I wrote to you earlier explaining that you should insure the works under MW 05 clause 5.4B and I trust that this insurance is now in place. You should send copies of the policy to Durr for their information.

Now that Durr is about to start on site, I should set out several procedures that should be observed for the smooth running of the project:

We have designated in the contract documents the building works area and additional space to store materials and tools. You should obtain Durr’s permission before going into this area. I know this may sound odd as it is your own home but it is a term of the building contract that they have taken possession of that part of the site and need to be able to work unimpeded.

You must remember that the site is temporarily an industrial workplace and as such can be dangerous. You should therefore be safety conscious whilst on site, wear protective shoes and hard hats, not visit the site after working hours and not take children on the site.

You should allow the contractor’s tradesmen a supply of water and electricity together with the use of lavatory and washing facilities.
