# Procurement of FF&E as agent

This section applies when the designer is procuring FF&E. The appropriate services in 'Part 2 Installation Management; FF&E installation' of the Services Schedule should be ticked. In this role, the designer can make an agreed handling charge but must not accept any undisclosed fee from others (e.g. the supplier) for these services (see Applicable case law: Eisenberg v Bachar, page 282). In this arrangement, the supplier is responsible to the client for the adequacy of the FF&E (but the designer must check that the supplier’s terms are reasonable).

# Procured items

Where in accordance with Part 2 of the Services schedule the Designer is to procure delivery and/or installation, but not the supply of FF&E, the provisions of sections B1 and B2 of this Annex shall apply when the Client instructs acceptance of the tender(s) received for such supply and/or installation.

# Accounts and payment

Prior to delivery of specified items the Designer: (a) shall certify the amount or amounts due to the supplier and pass the account or accounts to the Client for payment in accordance with clause 5.18.1 or

This subclause applies when the designer obtains a pro forma invoice from the supplier, checks it and passes it to the client for the client to pay direct to the supplier.

(b) shall pay the amount or amounts due in accordance with clause 5.18.2.

This subclause applies when the designer has established a project bank account with funds provided by the client. In these circumstances, the Designer should confirm with his/her professional indemnity insurers that the funds in such an account are insured both for negligence and for dishonesty of the designer’s employees.

An account for the Designer’s services shall be made in accordance with clause 15.15.1.

# Client's responsibility

All FF&E approved by or for approval by the Client and delivered to the Client premises shall be the sole risk of the Client

# Statutory rights

The rights relating to the supply of FF&E under this annex B2 are in addition to any statutory rights that may apply.

# Supply of FF&E as principal

This section applies when the designer is supplying FF&E. The appropriate services in 'Part 4 FF&E Supplied by Designer' of the Services Schedule should be ticked. In this role, the designer is not required to disclose his/her margins for these services but is responsible to the client for the adequacy of the FF&E as any other retailer.
