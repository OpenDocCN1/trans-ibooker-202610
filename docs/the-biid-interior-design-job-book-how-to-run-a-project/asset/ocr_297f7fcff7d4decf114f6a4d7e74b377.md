# Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights for Mr M Ployer

We refer to our recent meeting on site where we discussed the possibility of your photographing this project. I should like to confirm the terms we agreed, under which you might carry out this work:

+   1
+   Your fee would be £1,000.00 plus a maximum of £200.00 expenses and you would require two days on site. Our clients have stated that 20/21 April or 3/4 May would be suitable. We have already discussed with you what we would like to be emphasised in your images. I attach a list of the views we agreed, together with a floor plan on which they are marked. I or someone from my office would attend to direct the shots.

+   2
+   You would provide us within 14 days with ten images as we discussed, delivered to us on a CD or DVD as RAW, TIFF and JPEG files to a minimum resolution of 24 megapixels/image and post-processed to resolve converging verticals, correct for exposure, provide high dynamic range (HDR), remove any lens distortion, etc.

+   3
+   You would own the copyright and receive all fees from the use of the images. You would require publishers and others who who use the images to credit us: ‘interior design: Signer Associates’ adjacent to the image (not in a separate list of photo credits). We would have the right to approve the media in which these images are published. You would then advise us when the image(s) have been published and where.

+   4
+   You would grant us a licence to use the images for marketing the practice on our website, in our own practice literature etc. without any further payment to yourself. We will arrange to have you credited as photographer if we do so.

I will contact you after discussing this with our client.

Yours sincerely
