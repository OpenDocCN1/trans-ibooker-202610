# P3 Explanation of ID/10 terms (continued)

When work already carried out is varied, the designer would be well advised to issue a client's instruction form at the time the variation occurs (see 'D Specimen client's instruction', page 140) confirming the reason for the variation and, if the variation has been verbally instructed, his/her understanding of the instruction. This also reminds the client that additional fees may be due. This form will be very useful if disputes arise later.

+   5.8.1 the Designer is required to vary any item of design work commenced or completed pursuant to this Agreement or to provide a new design after the Client has authorised the Designer to develop an approved design;

+   5.8.2 the nature of the Project requires that substantial parts of the design cannot be completed or must be specified provisionally or approximately before construction commences;

+   5.8.3 performance of the Services under any part of the Services schedule is delayed, disrupted or prolonged;

+   5.8.4 the cost of any work, installation or equipment, for which the Designer performs design Services, is omitted from or not included in the Project Cost;

+   5.8.5 the Designer performs services in connection with any work, installation or equipment, which are not executed under the direction of or by the Designer.

This clause 5.8 shall not apply where any adjustment under clause 5.7.1 applies to the same events.

The Designer shall inform the Client on becoming aware that this clause 5.8 will apply. This clause 5.8 shall not apply where any change or extra work or expense arises from a breach of this Agreement by the Designer.

It is often important to the client that he/she is immediately advised (in writing) that additional fees may become due in order to revise his/her financing.

+   5.9.1 a lump sum or sums in accordance with clause 5.4; and/or

# Tender not accepted

5.10 Where the Designer is instructed by the Client to invite a tender or tenders for work or services in connection with the Project but no tender is made or accepted, the Designer shall be entitled to fees due based on the Project Cost or that part of it relating to the said work or services current at the date of invitation to tender.
