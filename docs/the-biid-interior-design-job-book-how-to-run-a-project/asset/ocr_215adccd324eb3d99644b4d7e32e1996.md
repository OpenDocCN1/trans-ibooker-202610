Dear Sirs

Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights for Mr M Ployer

We refer to our recent telephone conversation in which we advised you of a flaw in the carpeting that you supplied for the drawing room in this project.

We note your opinion that the flaw is minor but in our professional judgement it is unacceptable, particularly in a product of the advertised high quality of your ‘Infinite Loop’.

We do not believe that merely a new piece could be inserted as the likelihood that a new dye lot would match the remainder in the room is slight.

We therefore request that you remove and dispose of all the carpeting in this room, arrange for enough carpet to recarpet the entire room to be delivered and fitted and that it match as closely as possible the other carpeting of the same colour that you supplied for other rooms on the ground floor.

Thank you for your help.

Yours faithfully
