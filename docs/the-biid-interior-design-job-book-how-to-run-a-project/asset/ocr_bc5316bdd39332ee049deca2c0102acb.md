Please note that, under the terms of the building contract, we will not be able to certify any work not included in the contract or the subject of a designer's instruction. Indeed, should such work be built into the project, we may deduct from the certified sum the cost of removing it. Therefore, should you receive any purported instruction from any party other than us, you should not carry it out before obtaining our written instruction to do so.

Whilst we will visit the site to see that the work is proceeding generally in accordance with the building contract, you will be responsible for quality control. We shall carry out site visits as the progress of the works requires it; if you wish us to visit the site at any point during construction, please contact us giving as much notice as possible.

We look forward to your starting on the date for possession, 8 October 2007.
