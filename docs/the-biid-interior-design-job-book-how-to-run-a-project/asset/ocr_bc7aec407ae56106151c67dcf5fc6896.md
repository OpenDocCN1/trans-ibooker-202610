The client has the right to instruct the designer provided the client is not requiring the designer to, say, contravene the CDM Regulations or to carry out the design work in an impractical or unprofessional way.

Where the Designer, as Lead Consultant or Contract Administrator, has responsibility to direct and/or co-ordinate the work or services of or give instructions to Other Persons, such instructions shall be issued only through the Designer.

If the Client or anyone acting on behalf of the Client, issues instructions to any Other Persons the Designer shall not be responsible for the consequences of such instructions.

It is not uncommon for a client to well-meaningly directly instruct the contractor on site, perhaps without regard to other aspects of the design or even in contravention of building or planning regulations. Designers should draw this clause to the attention of the client before construction starts and point out that the remedy of any problems caused may attract additional fees (see 'J Model letter to client before construction', page 200 and 'J Model letter to contractor before construction', page 198).

# Applications for consent

The Client shall instruct the making of applications for consents under planning legislation, building acts, regulations or other statutory requirements and by others having an interest in the Project. The Client shall pay any statutory charges and any fees, expenses and disbursements in respect of such applications.

When the designer is asked to apply for planning permission, building regulations approval and/or other statutory requirements, he/she should exercise the 'reasonable skill, care and diligence' required in clause 2.1. The designer has the right of reasonable objection to carry out a task beyond the designer's expertise and/or resources and should in these cases advise the client to take specialist advice.

# Appointment of Other Persons

Where work or services, other than those to be performed by the Designer, are required, the Client shall appoint and pay Other Persons under separate agreements to perform such work or services, shall hold such Other Persons responsible for the competence and performance of their work or services, and shall require them to Collaborate with the Designer.

The Client shall confirm in writing to the Designer the services to be performed by Other Persons, their disciplines and the expected duration of their employment.

When it becomes evident that specialist advice is required it is important that consultants are directly appointed by the client. If they are appointed by the designer, he/she becomes responsible for the consultant's work and should obtain evidence of the consultant's PII policy and a written commitment that the policy will be maintained for as long as the time limit for action shown in the appointment letter. The designer should notify his/her own PII insurer of the arrangement and should consider whether to charge an additional fee for this risk.
