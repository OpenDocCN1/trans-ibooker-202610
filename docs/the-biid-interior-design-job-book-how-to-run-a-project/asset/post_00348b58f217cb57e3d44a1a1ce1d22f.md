## L Post Practical Completion stage

### Stage description

Stage L, Post Practical Completion, covers the administration of the building contract after practical completion and carrying out final inspections. For an additional fee, the designer can assist the client in occupying the building and reviewing its performance in use.

It should again be noted that it is the contract administrator's responsibility to identify and remedy defects and so the designer should monitor the works to identify any other defects and ensure that all are remedied before issuing a final payment certificate.

### RIBA Plan of Work

The RIBA Outline Plan of Work 2007 describes the key tasks of this stage as, 'L1: (After Practical Completion) Administration of the building contract after Practical Completion and making final inspections. L2: Assisting building user during initial occupation period. L3: Review of project performance in use.'

### ID/10

Stage L is an 'Installation Management' stage on page A of the Schedule of Interior Design Services in ID/10 unless the designer is acting as constructor in which case it is a 'Building Works' stage.

Where the designer is supplying FF&E, this task is shown in the 'FF&E supplied by designer' stage on page D of the Schedule.

### Tasks: Interior designer/lead consultant

When acting as the interior designer in a design team or when acting as lead consultant, the designer will carry out only the following five tasks (deliverables in bold):

- Prepare a schedule of defects.
- Visit the site to monitor the remedying of defects.
- Provide information to the quantity surveyor for the preparation of the final account.
- Issue a certificate of completion of making good defects.
- Issue a final certificate.

### Contractor

When acting as contractor under Annex A of ID/10, the designer will carry out only the following three tasks:

- Facilitate the installation and commissioning of FF&E.
- Remedy any defects.
- Issue a final invoice to the client and receive payment.

### FF&E procuring

When procuring FF&E under Annex B, sections B1 and B2, of ID/10, the designer will carry out the following two tasks:

- Monitor delivery/installation of specialist items and/or FF&E.
- Provide the client with maintenance advice regarding FF&E.

### FF&E supplying

When supplying FF&E under Annex B, sections B1 and B3, of ID/10, the designer will carry out the following tasks:

- Supply/install specialist items and/or FF&E.
- Provide the client with maintenance advice regarding FF&E.

On smaller projects, stages J, Mobilisation, K, Construction to Practical Completion, and L are often carried out in the same exercise.
