# 2.7 Designer's Representative

The Designer’s Representative shall have full authority to act on
behalf of the Designer for all purposes in connection with
performance of the Services but not to vary the terms of the
Agreement.

Clients often like to know who in the designer’s organisation will
be in charge of their project. If this person changes, the designer
should remember to notify the client.

# 2.8 Estimates

When the Designer is undertaking the Building Works, Decorations
and or supplying F&E as Principal, any cost or time estimates
prepared by the Designer shall not be construed as offers unless
submitted as tenders in accordance with Parts 3 and 4 of the Services
schedule.

This clause is to distinguish between cost and time estimates of
the design which the designer does not warrant and tenders which
the designer might submit if he/she is acting as contractor and/or
retailer and which are binding if accepted.

# 2.9 Photography and publicity

The Designer shall have the right to take and publish photographs of
the Project, and the Client shall give reasonable access to the Project
for this purpose for 2 years after practical completion of the Project.
The Designer shall obtain the consent of the Client, which consent
shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed, before publication of
any other information about the Project, unless reasonably
necessary for performance of the Services.

Many clients are understandably concerned to protect their privacy
and perhaps not advertise expensive possessions. However, this
clause allows the designer to have photographs of the completed
project published in his/her portfolio or website and in
publications. The designer should obtain the client’s consent
before publishing the client’s name or the project address. For
large projects the two-year period may have to be extended.

# 2.10 Confidentiality

The Designer shall not disclose to any other person Confidential
Information unless:

+   - 2.10.1 disclosure is necessary for the proper performance of the Services, or in order to take professional advice in relation to this Agreement or the Services, or in order to obtain/maintain insurance cover as required by this Agreement;
+   - 2.10.2 is in the public domain other than due to wrongful use or disclosure; or
+   - 2.10.3 disclosure is required by law or because of disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

Designers should be wary of potential clients attempting to
impose stricter confidentiality terms as these may not allow the
designer to perform the services effectively.
