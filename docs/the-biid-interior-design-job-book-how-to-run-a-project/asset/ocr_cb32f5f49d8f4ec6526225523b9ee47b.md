Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

Following your approval of our stage E proposals, we shall now commence stage F, Production Information, in which we will prepare detailed information for construction.

It is important at this stage that we consider how you wish to procure the construction of this project, i.e. on what basis do you wish to select a contractor and on what terms?

First there is the matter of the form of contract between you and the main contractor. For this size of project it is recommended that we use one of the industry standard forms. From my experience, I would suggest that the Joint Contracts Tribunal Minor Works form (MW 05) is the most appropriate for this project. It is not perfect but the cost of drafting a bespoke contract, together with the difficulty of finding a contractor prepared to tender on terms with which they are not familiar, make this in our view the best choice.

I explained the range of construction procurement strategies available, in particular obtaining competitive tenders or alternatively negotiating an acceptable tender price and timetable with a selected contractor. Please advise me which procurement route you prefer.
