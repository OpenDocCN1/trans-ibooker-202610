[It is assumed for the purpose of the example project in this book that the Principal Contractor is the Contractor.]

+   Base Date 7 September 2007 [A finite date, usually ten days prior to the date on which tenders are to be returned, to determine what fluctuations are payable.]

+   CIS Employer at the Base Date is/is not a contractor/is not a contractor for the purposes of the cis

+   CDM Regulations the project is/is not notifiable

+   Arbitration Article 7 and Schedule 1 (Arbitration) apply/do not apply

[It is assumed for the purpose of the example project in this book that arbitration is preferred to litigation for reasons of privacy.]

+   CDM Planning Period shall mean the period: wA

+   Date for Commencement of the Works: 8 October 2007

+   Date for Completion: 5 May 2008

+   Liquidated damages £ 1,000.00 per week [This sum must be a realistic figure in view of the actual loss that would be suffered if the project completion were late.]

[It is assumed for the purpose of the example project in this book that (a) the particulars for clauses 2.10, 4.3, 4.5, 4.8.1, 5.4a.1 and 5.4b.1.2 are completed as suggested in the form and (b) that Clause 4.11 is deleted in the Particulars unless it is intended that the contract price is to fluctuate in response to tax changes, etc.]

+   Contractor's insurance £ 5,000,000.00 [Covers injury to persons and/or property and should not be confused with insurance of the works under 5.4. Minimum £2m.]

[Insurance of the Works, etc.: clause 5.4A, is selected when insurance is to be taken out by the contractor in respect of new buildings; clause 5.4B, taken out by the employer, is for new work to existing buildings and 5.4C is taken out by the employer for an existing building and by the contractor (under clause 5.4A) for the new works.
