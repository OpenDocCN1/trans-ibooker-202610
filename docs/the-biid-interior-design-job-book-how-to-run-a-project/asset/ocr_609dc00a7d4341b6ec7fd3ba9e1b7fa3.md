Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

Following our recent discussion, the tender documents which you wish us to issue will be on the basis of the JCT Minor Works Building Contract 2005, Revision 2 (MW 05). You asked us to show in the tender documents the following, which will be incorporated into the building contract after a tender has been accepted:

+   1. The method of final dispute resolution will be arbitration (as in the event of a dispute with the contractor, you would prefer any proceedings to be private).
+   2. The contract will be executed under hand.
+   3. As the project is work to an existing building, clause 5.4B will apply regarding works insurance. You should immediately notify your house and contents insurers regarding the work to be carried out and ensure that revised cover commences before the site is handed over to the contractor.

You also asked us to show:

+   4. the period for tendering to be four weeks.
+   5. the date for receipt of tenders to be 19 September 2007
+   6. the tender is to remain open for 31 days
+   7. the date for the start of the works to be 26 September 2007. You decided to ask the tenderers to indicate the date for completion in their tenders
+   8. the amount of liquidated damages is to be £1,000 per week. You have calculated this to be a fair estimate of the amount of loss you would suffer if the project were late.

Please let me know if you wish me to further explain any of these provisions to you.

Yours sincerely
