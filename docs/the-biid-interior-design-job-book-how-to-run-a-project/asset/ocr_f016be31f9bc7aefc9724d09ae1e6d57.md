Dear Sirs

Professional Indemnity Insurance Policy no 1234 5678: Potential Claim Regarding Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights, Anytown for Mr M Ployer

We have just received a letter (copy enclosed) from our client suggesting negligence in the carrying out of our duties in respect of the above project.

We are surprised at his claims, which he has not previously hinted at. Indeed, until recently our relationship with him has been very satisfactory.

We have carefully considered his letter and reviewed our project file and believe the claims to be without justification. We have also consulted the specialist construction law department of solicitors, Phew & Sons, who advise that they can see no reason, from what we have advised them, why Mr Ployer should have any basis for a successful claim.

Although it may have no bearing on this issue, we have noted that he has recently become associated with a Ms Phyllis Tyne who has seemed antagonistic to some of our design ideas. Pressure of work has also meant that we have not claimed our fees for several months and so there is a fairly considerable amount, £20,080.27, due now at the end of construction.

Phew & Sons have advised that, as our appointment was under ID/10 and because we explained sensitive clauses at the time to Mr Ployer (see confirming letter to Mr Ployer dated 20 January 2007 attached), we should have a good chance of collecting the sums due. They have drafted the attached letters, the first of which you will see refutes Mr Ployer's claims in detail. Subject to your approval, we will send the letters to Mr Ployer.

Yours faithfully
