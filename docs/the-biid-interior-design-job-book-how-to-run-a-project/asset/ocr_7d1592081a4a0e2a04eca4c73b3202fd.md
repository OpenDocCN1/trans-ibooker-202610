# FFE Notes on letter and FF&E sales order (as principal)

# Sales orders

The sales order form is for use where the designer is procuring FF&E as principal (acting in effect as a retailer) under sections B1 and B3 of ID/10 and is the contract of supply by the designer to the employer. Two copies of the sales order should be sent to the employer with a copy for the designer’s file. As a retailer, the designer must comply with the Sale of Goods Act.

To ensure that the client wishes the items of FF&E to be purchased, a sales order describing the items precisely should be sent to the client prior to placing an order with the manufacturer and a copy signed by the client and returned to the designer.

# Deposits

Designers supplying bespoke items of FF&E and fabric as principals often require the full price to be paid to them by the client before placing an order with the manufacturer. Alternatively, with ready-made items, the designer may ask for a substantial deposit, say, one half of the price, to be paid before ordering, and the balance before delivery and installation.

Sometimes, despite the designer’s best efforts to explain how a piece will appear, clients can have second thoughts when they see it upon delivery and refuse to pay. Therefore, it is important that the designer is not out of pocket at any point in the procurement process.

# Samples

When fabric or carpet is part of the order, the designer should attach samples of the fabric(s) or carpet(s) to all copies of the order. For large orders, supplementary pages can be added.
