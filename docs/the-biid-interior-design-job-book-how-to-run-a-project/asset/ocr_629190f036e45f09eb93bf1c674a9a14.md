# ## Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

In our recent meeting in which you approved the tender documents, we discussed procurement strategy. I noted that you could select a contractor based on their reputation for quality and ask Sir Veigh Orr to negotiate a price with them or that you could obtain competitive tenders to be assured that you had received the keenest price.

You prefer to tender the job and so we propose to invite the following builders (listed in alphabetical order) to submit main contract tenders:

+   - Durr Construction Ltd Anytown
+   - Rix & Mortimer Ltd Anothertown
+   - Trowell & Stone Ltd Anearbytown
+   - Interior Fitup Plc Sumothertown

These contractors have successfully carried out projects where we were designers or, in the case of Interior Fitup, comes recommended to us by a colleague designer whose opinion we respect. Having said that, we cannot of course warrant that whichever contractor is chosen will perform satisfactorily on this project.

You have mentioned that a friend employed Joyston Wall Ltd of Anytown and he was satisfied with their performance. Whilst we imagine that this firm might be suitable, neither we nor our colleagues have first-hand experience of their capabilities or past performance and so cannot comment. We will include them on the list if you wish.
