# Schedule of Fees and Expenses

**Note** Unless otherwise stated, fees and expenses will be payable in monthly instalments in accordance with clause 5 of ID/10. The following instalments are to be paid upon the completion of major work stages (e.g. Design, Pre-construction, etc.). When fees are charged on a percentage or lump sum basis, unless otherwise agreed, work additional to that shown in the Schedule of Fees and Expenses will be charged on a time basis. Fees, expenses and disbursements shown below are exclusive of VAT which will be added at the current rate.

This Schedule is to be read in conjunction with ID/10 and the designer's letter to the client dated 20/07

**Fees** Percentage fee(s) in accordance with clause 5.3: 20% (twenty per cent) of the Construction Cost for work stages C-L; 15% of the net cost of procured FF+E.

and/or lump sum fee(s) in accordance with clause 5.4: 'success fee' of £1,000: immediately the planning committee resolves to grant a planning consent subject to conditions; £1,000: scheduling client's requirements. and/or: time based fee(s) in accordance with clause 5.5: for work stages A, B; for preparing a condition schedule

The number of site visits allowed for in the percentage fee is 10. Additional site visits will be charged on a time basis at the rates shown below.
