# H Notes on letter to client re site inspector

(None of the following applies when the designer, acting as contractor, is carrying out the building works under Annex A of ID/10.)

# Quality control

One misapprehension amongst architects, interior designers and sometimes clients is that the designer is responsible for quality control during construction. This is not the case unless specifically provided for in the designer’s appointment. The designer should not contract with the client to ‘supervise’ construction, rather to ‘monitor’, ‘inspect’ or (as in ID/10) to ‘administer the terms of the building contract’.

The main contractor is responsible for complying with the terms of the building contract, including the drawings and specification. Therefore it is important to be as specific as possible in these documents about the level of quality required.

However, some processes, e.g. special decorative finishes, may require the designer to instruct the contractor on site precisely what his/her intentions are and to monitor the results. In these cases the designer should show a note in the specifications and/or the drawings that the contractor should not commence this work without giving the designer adequate notice to be in attendance and to approve initial samples of the work.
