## ANNEX A Designer undertaking Building Works

Annex A applies if the designer is acting as a contractor. The appropriate services in 'Part 3 Building Works' of the Services Schedule should be ticked.

### A1 Tender and acceptance

Where in accordance with Part 3 of the Services schedule the Designer is to carry out the Building Works these supplementary conditions in this Annex A shall apply when the Client accepts in writing the tender of the Designer for carrying out the Works.

### A2 Accounts and payment

The sum, which includes the Designer's fees and expenses, payable to the Designer for execution of the Building Works is the amount stated in the tender, the contract sum, or any agreed variation to it. The Designer shall submit an account or accounts for payment in accordance with clause 5.15.2.

The final instalment of not more than 5% of the said sum shall become due after the Designer notifies the Client that all the Building Works are complete and has made good without charge any faults notified by the Client within 6 weeks of such notification.

### Cost of Building Works

A3 After the expiry of 6 months from the date of acceptance of the tender, the rates quoted in the priced specification or tendered Schedules of Building Works and/or Decorations shall be revised in accordance with the Consumer Prices Index.

### A4 Execution and completion of Building Works

The Designer shall execute or procure the execution and completion of the Building Works with due diligence and in a workmanlike manner using skilled trades people appropriate to each task in accordance with the drawings and specifications and by the completion date identified in the tender or any other date agreed in writing by the parties.

### A5.1 Possession of the Site

The Client shall allow the Designer:
- (a) to take possession of those parts of the premises where the Building Works are being carried out;
- (b) reasonable space to store materials and tools as necessary;
- (c) to use toilet and washing facilities, heating, water and electricity;
- (d) to carry out work from Monday to Friday inclusive between 08.00 and 18.00 hours.
- A5.2 The Designer shall leave the property in a clean and tidy condition and clear away rubbish arising from the Works at the end of each day.
- A5.3 The Designer shall be responsible for the reasonable security of the property if the Client is absent at any time during the course of the Building Works.
- A5.4 The Designer shall warn the Client of any dangers arising from the Building Works.
- A5.5 The Client shall not allow other people, particularly children, to be exposed to any dangers from the Building Works.

(See ‘J Model letter to client before construction’, page 200.)

### Progress

- A6.1 The Designer shall notify the Client on becoming aware of any changes or potential changes to the progress or the contract sum for the Building Works.
- A6.2 The Designer shall not be responsible in respect of any defects in or delays to the completion of the Building Works caused by persons other than the Designer’s employees, sub-contractors or suppliers of the Designer; but
- A6.3 The Designer shall be entitled to a reasonable extension of time and to payment for any increased costs incurred.

### Statutory obligations

- A7 The Designer shall comply with any statutory provision applicable to the Building Works, and, where required, shall give all notices and pay any charges arising.

### Liability

A8 The Designer undertaking Building Works, including any Decorations under this Annex A, shall remain responsible for 6 years from practical completion for any faults, excluding fair wear and tear, in the execution of or of the goods and materials used in the Building Works.

Any action or proceedings arising out of or in connection with the said Building Works and/or Decorations whether in contract, tort and statutory duty or otherwise shall be commenced before the expiry of the liability period.

### Insurance

- A9.1 The party named in Part 3 of the Services schedule shall insure in the joint names of the Client and the Designer the risks of loss or damage to the existing structures, to any contents and to the Building Works and all unfixed materials by fire, explosion, storm, tempest, flood, bursting or overflowing of water tanks, apparatus or pipes, earthquake, aircraft and other aerial devices or articles dropped from them, riot and civil commotion until completion of the Building Works.
- A9.2 The Designer shall maintain public liability insurance for death or injury to people or damage to property until completion of the Building Works.

On the request of one party the other shall produce such evidence as may be reasonably required that the actions under paragraphs A9.1 and A9.2 above have been carried out.

The designer should, before the commencement of building works, remind the client of his/her responsibility to insure the works (see 'Notes on letters before construction', page 196).

## Designer supplying FF&E as agent or supplier

Annex B applies if the designer is procuring or supplying (or both) FF&E. The appropriate services in 'Part 2 – Installation management' and/or 'Part 4 – FF&E supplied by Designer' of the Services Schedule should be ticked.

### FF&E - GENERAL

This section always applies if the designer is either supplying or procuring FF&E.

### Procured items

- Where the Designer as agent or principal is to procure supply, delivery and/or installation, the Designer shall take all reasonable steps to ascertain that such FF&E items are:
- (a) obtained from manufacturers or other suppliers under conditions of sale which contain no unreasonable exclusions or limitations of liability;
- (b) of reasonable quality and fit for their purpose and delivered to the Site in good order by any agreed delivery date;
- (c) procured in accordance with the provisions of this Annex; and
- (d) if faulty, remedied or, if necessary replaced, in accordance with the conditions of sale if notified within a reasonable period depending on the durability of the product.

### Cancellation of orders

- The Client acknowledges that:
- (a) in the event that the Client instructs cancellation of any order given to an FF&E supplier by the Designer as agent or principal, the Client reimburses the Designer for the costs arising; and
- (b) custom-made items of FF&E are paid for in full at the date of ordering; and
- (c) cancellation of a stock item may be subject to a cancellation charge.

Custom-made items cannot be cancelled once fabrication has commenced, and full payment for all such items shall be made by the client to the designer prior to the placing of an order for such items to be fabricated.

### Prices of supplied items

Supplied items shall be charged at the prices quoted in the tendered schedule of FF&E.

- The Designer shall notify the Client of the variation to the contract sum if:
- (a) the Designer is unable to obtain any specified items of FF&E outside the reasonable control of the Designer, and the Designer is instructed by the Client to substitute alternative items; or
- (b) the price of any item is varied by the manufacturer/supplier before delivery.
