Dear Mike and Phyllis

## Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

We have written to you separately today in reply to your letter dated 19 June. Whilst we stand by all that is said in the open letter, we would prefer to reach a swift and commercial resolution of the practice’s appointment by you. If such a resolution cannot be achieved, then we shall have no alternative but to seek advice from our solicitors in respect of recovering all the currently overdue fees including those issued today.

As far as your suggestion of a claim against the practice is concerned, you will appreciate from my open letter of today that we consider such a claim to be entirely without foundation. In any event, you will of course appreciate that by virtue of clause 5.20 of the Appointment you may not withhold payment of any part of the practice’s fee accounts in lieu of any purported claim. Further, you have failed to provide any payment notice in accordance with clause 5.19 and therefore the fee accounts are due in full. Any purported claim against the practice would have to be the subject of separate proceedings.

Having said this, we have no wish to become embroiled in legal action either against you or in defence of any purported claim by you. We therefore send this letter pursuant to Part 36 of the Civil Procedure Rules (CPR).

Signer Associates Limited’s Part 36 offer is to accept from you payment of the sum of £20,000.00 including VAT, in full and final settlement of all parties’ (including principals’) claims against each other howsoever arising, including but not limited to Signer Associates Limited’s contract of appointment by you dated 27 January 2007:

- 1. All claims for interest to be waived.

- 2. Each party to bear their own legal costs, if any.

- 3. Signer Associates Limited’s appointment by you dated 27 January 2007 to be at an end with no obligations thereunder remaining upon any party to that contract of appointment save and except for the copyright provisions at section 6 of the same.

- 4. For your information the sum of £20,000.00 (including VAT) represents approximately a 9% discount on the sums due. We are therefore proposing that a total of £1,961.52 is waived in relation to fees.

We consider this to be a generous offer made only for the reasons stated earlier. You should appreciate that the sums invoiced with our open letter of today are included in the proposed settlement.

This offer is open for a period of 21 days from your receipt of this letter. Please confirm the date of receipt by you upon receipt. After the 21-day period you may accept this offer only if we agree liability for any legal cost incurred after the 21-day period, or if the court gives permission. Where Signer Associates Limited does better in subsequent court action at trial than this Part 36 offer, then the court may order under CPR 36.21 that Signer Associates Limited is entitled to both enhanced levels of interest and costs.

Whilst we appreciate that court action has not yet ensued between us, this letter is written in accordance with CPR Part 36.10 which allows the court to take into account a Part 36 payment made prior to the issue of court proceedings.

It seems to us that the above Part 36 offer provides a sensible means of drawing Signer Associates Limited’s involvement in this project to a close and will enable us all to move on without expensive and time-consuming legal action.

We look forward to hearing from you within the period stated in this letter.
