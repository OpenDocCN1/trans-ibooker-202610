# P1 Fundamentals stage

This preliminary stage covers general procedures that should be used on all projects.

Designers should remember that they are often spending large sums of their clients' money and must always be able to show that they have not been careless. Clients can sometimes misguidedly or even malevolently claim that a designer has been negligent. The best protection, in addition to being competent, is to carefully document every action taken on a job and the reasons for doing so.

Good practice therefore demands that certain procedures should be followed throughout the course of a job. These include making careful paper records of all emails, telephone conversations and meetings, keeping careful time and expense records and registering all documents issued to others.

Job filing should be kept current, at the very least every week, as unsuspecting practices can be 'ambushed' by an adjudication which will be determined in only 28 days. Mounting an effective defence can be achieved only if the practice is always prepared.

Files for small jobs can simply be kept chronologically but it is often useful for the files for large projects to be broken down into sections, for example:

+   0 General
+   1 Client
+   2 Design team
+   3 Consents
+   4 Services/utilities
+   5 Contractor
+   6 Subcontractors/suppliers
+   7 Construction administration
+   ... Other as necessary
