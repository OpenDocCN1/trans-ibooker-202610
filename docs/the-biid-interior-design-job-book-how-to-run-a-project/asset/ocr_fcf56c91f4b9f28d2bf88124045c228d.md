# P3 Explanation of ID/10 terms

The following is a list of the terms of ID/10 (in blue italics) together with explanations (in black) of those that are not self-evident:

# 1 GENERAL

# Interpretation

The headings and notes to the Conditions are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words denoting natural persons include corporations and firms and vice versa.

# Applicable law

This Agreement is subject to the law of England unless otherwise agreed and specified in the Project Data and subject to clause 9.3 the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the specified jurisdiction.

This clause is very useful to avoid disputes with foreign clients or projects being heard in foreign courts.

# Communications

Any notice or other document required under this Agreement shall be in writing and given or served by any effective means to the address of the recipient specified in this Agreement or such other address, including a postal address or fax number notified to the other party.

Communications between the Client and the Designer that are not such notices or documents may be sent to any other address, including an e-mail address, notified by the other party as an appropriate address for specific communications. Communications take effect on receipt, but if not in writing are of no effect unless and until confirmed in writing by the sender or the other party.

Communications sent by special delivery or recorded delivery shall be deemed (subject to proof to the contrary) to have arrived at the appropriate address on the second working day after posting.

All but trivial communications between the designer and any other parties involved in a project should be in writing, or confirmed in writing. Emails are beginning to be accepted by the courts as acceptable evidence but the designer should remember to make and file a paper copy when they are sent or received.

# Public holidays

Where under this Agreement an action is required within a specified period of days from a specified date, that period commences immediately after that date. The period shall include Saturdays and Sundays but shall exclude any day that is a public holiday.

# Duration

The provisions of this Agreement are without prejudice to the respective rights and obligations of the parties and continue in force as long as necessary to give effect to such rights and obligations.
