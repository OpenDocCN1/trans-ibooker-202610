# D Model letter to planning/listed buildings officer

Signer Associates Interior Designers

Mr Charles Wails MRTPi Listed Buildings Officer Anyshire County Council 111 Castle Hill Anytown Anyshire AN1 1AA

123.02 2 April 2007

Dear Mr Wails

Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights for Mr M Ployer

Thank you for meeting us on site on 1 April to discuss our preliminary proposals for this site, a copy of which we handed you.

We understand that you were happy with these save that you wished us to retain several existing internal doors rather than replacing them with new ones. We also understand that you believe that there are no controversial planning matters raised by our proposals but that you will discuss our scheme with the area planning officer and revert if there are any problems.

We will discuss this with our client and look forward to submitting planning and listed building applications in the near future. Subject to the matter of the doors, we understand that you will be able to recommend it to the Planning Committee for approval.

We gather from you that you are confident that our application can be decided within the eight-week statutory period.

Yours faithfully
