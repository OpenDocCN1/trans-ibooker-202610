# Incomplete services

5.12 Where for any reason the Designer provides only part of the Services specified in the Services Schedule, fees shall be calculated as described in the schedule of Fees and Expenses for completed work stages and for the balance of work carried out the fees shall be on the basis of the Designer’s estimate of the percentage of completion.

The appointment letter should refer to an enclosed Schedule of Fees and Expenses (see ‘p3 Specimen Schedule of Fees and Expenses’, page 44) which sets out payment at the completion of each work stage.

# Expenses and disbursements

5.13 The Client shall reimburse the Designer for expenses in the manner specified in the schedule of Fees and Expenses.

Expenses in connection with Services under Parts 3 and/or 4 of the Services Schedule shall be deemed to be included in the tender or tenders for such Services.

It is useful to include a percentage uplift (or handling charge) to expenses to remunerate the designer for the administrative time in billing and collecting expenses, for the interest on payments made to suppliers before equivalent sums are collected from the client and for the risk that the client might not pay.

# Maintain records

5.14 The Designer shall maintain records of time spent on Services performed on a time basis for the purpose of verifying charges under clause 5.6 and shall in addition maintain records of any expenses and disbursements to be reimbursed at net cost. The Designer shall make such records available to the Client on reasonable request.

It is important to carefully record time spent on projects, not only the amount of time spent but also what tasks were carried out in that time (see ‘p1 Specimen time sheet’, page 15 and ‘Applicable case law: Munkenbeck v Harold, page 282). Expenses should also be carefully recorded, supported by suppliers’ invoices.

# Part B: Payment

# Payments to the Designer

5.15 The Designer shall issue separate accounts for amounts due, calculated on the basis of the Designer’s reasonable estimate of the percentage of completion of the Services specified in the Services schedule for:

+   - for fees due under parts 1 and/or 2 of the schedule together with any expenses; and/or

+   - for other amounts due under part 3 of the schedule for the Building Works; and/or

+   - for other amounts due under part 4 of the schedule for FF&E.
