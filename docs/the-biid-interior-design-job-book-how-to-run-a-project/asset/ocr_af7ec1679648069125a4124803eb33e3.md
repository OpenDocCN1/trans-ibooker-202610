# Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

We are about to complete the preparation of the tender documents and there is one matter that I wish to bring to your attention at this time.

![](bbox=[0.82, 0.42, 0.98, 0.51])

As you will remember, our agreement with you provides for us, amongst other tasks at this stage, to administer the terms of the building contract and to visit the site to see that the work is proceeding generally in accordance with the building contract. We have allowed for 10 site visits although we will visit the site as necessary depending on the progress of the building work. I would estimate the frequency of these visits normally to be approximately at 14 to 31 day intervals.

Although we will monitor the quality of the building work during our site visits, it is the contractor’s responsibility to ensure that the quality of the work complies with the drawings and specification. Therefore it is conceivable that faulty work could be incorporated into the project and covered up between site visits without us being able to observe it.

If you wish to have more frequent inspections (and our view is that for a project of this scope and complexity, inspections should be carried out every [?] days), we can arrange for you to employ a [part-/full-] time site inspector (clerk of works) to inspect the work more frequently under our direction. We would help you to find a suitable inspector and arrange his/her employment terms. [We would wish to charge an additional hourly fee for managing the site inspector.]

Prior to issuing the final payment certificate to the contractor, we will inspect the completed works and, if they are satisfactory, we will issue a certificate of completion of making good defects.
