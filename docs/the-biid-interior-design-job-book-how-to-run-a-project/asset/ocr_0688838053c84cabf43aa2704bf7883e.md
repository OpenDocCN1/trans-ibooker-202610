# P1 Notes on print register

**Print register**

The printing of drawings often makes up the major portion of recoverable expenses charged to a client and so it is useful to collect up all printing on projects on a print register, whether the prints are made in-house or by a service bureau, for easy reference when billing the client.

Most designers charge in-house printing at rates comparable with current service bureau rates. For large practices this can sometimes justify the lease or purchase of dedicated printers.
