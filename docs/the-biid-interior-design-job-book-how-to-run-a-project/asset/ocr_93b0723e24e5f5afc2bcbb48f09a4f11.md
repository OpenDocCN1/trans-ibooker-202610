Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

I refer to our meeting on site on 18 June with the consultants and contractor. Following an inspection of the works, I determined that practical completion has been achieved and so I enclose the employer’s copies of the certificate of practical completion and the penultimate certificate which I have just issued to the contractor.

I gather that you were generally satisfied. You were handed the keys and are now able to take possession.

The rectification period commences from the date on the certificate. Please note that defects that appear during this period will be the responsibility of Durr to rectify. I would be grateful if you would advise us of any apparent defects that you might notice and I will draw any relevant ones to Durr’s attention. We will be carrying out a further inspection after Durr has completed rectification.
