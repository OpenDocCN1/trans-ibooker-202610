# Designer supplying FF&E as agent or supplier

Annex B applies if the designer is procuring or supplying (or both) FF&E. The appropriate services in 'Part 2 – Installation management' and/or 'Part 4 – FF&E supplied by Designer' of the Services Schedule should be ticked.

# FF&E - GENERAL

This section always applies if the designer is either supplying or procuring FF&E.

# Procured items

+   Where the Designer as agent or principal is to procure supply, delivery and/or installation, the Designer shall take all reasonable steps to ascertain that such FF&E items are:
+   (a) obtained from manufacturers or other suppliers under conditions of sale which contain no unreasonable exclusions or limitations of liability;
+   (b) of reasonable quality and fit for their purpose and delivered to the Site in good order by any agreed delivery date;
+   (c) procured in accordance with the provisions of this Annex; and
+   (d) if faulty, remedied or, if necessary replaced, in accordance with the conditions of sale if notified within a reasonable period depending on the durability of the product.

# Cancellation of orders

+   The Client acknowledges that:
+   (a) in the event that the Client instructs cancellation of any order given to an FF&E supplier by the Designer as agent or principal, the Client reimburses the Designer for the costs arising; and
+   (b) custom-made items of FF&E are paid for in full at the date of ordering; and
+   (c) cancellation of a stock item may be subject to a cancellation charge.

Custom-made items cannot be cancelled once fabrication has commenced, and full payment for all such items shall be made by the client to the designer prior to the placing of an order for such items to be fabricated.

# Prices of supplied items

Supplied items shall be charged at the prices quoted in the tendered schedule of FF&E.

+   The Designer shall notify the Client of the variation to the contract sum if:
+   (a) the Designer is unable to obtain any specified items of FF&E outside the reasonable control of the Designer, and the Designer is instructed by the Client to substitute alternative items; or
+   (b) the price of any item is varied by the manufacturer/supplier before delivery.
