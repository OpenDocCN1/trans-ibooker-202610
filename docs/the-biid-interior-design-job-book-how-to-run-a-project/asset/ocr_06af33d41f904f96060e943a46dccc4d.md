# # A Specimen proposed scope of services





Ployer Enterprises is one of the world’s leading developers and manufacturers of gadgets, widgets and gizmos. Its management wishes to have a strategy prepared to identify suitable sites/buildings with room for future expansion to move office staff into within the next two years.

This brief report outlines how this strategy could be prepared, agreed and then implemented.



The first step will be to assemble key information about buildings/sites being considered including:

+   1. measured drawings and a photographic survey of suitable buildings/sites

+   2. any original building contract documents that may exist with key drawings being digitised

+   3. any building or services surveys that have been carried out since the buildings’ completion.



In addition to Signer Associates, providing interior design services, advice on the following should be sought:

+   1. cost and time estimating

+   2. architectural design of any major proposed building alterations or new extensions

+   3. health and safety advice (appointing a CDM Co-ordinator)

and advice may need to be sought regarding:

+   4. an analysis of existing building mechanical and electrical systems

+   5. sustainability.



Having assembled the design team, the next step will be to define Ployer’s requirements by doing the following:

+   1. identify and evaluate possible sites and/or buildings

+   2. compile a list of spaces to accommodate the required functions and prepare a room schedule

+   3. interview key staff to determine precise requirements for each space and prepare room requirements schedules

+   4. analyse desired adjacencies and prepare an adjacency relationships matrix
