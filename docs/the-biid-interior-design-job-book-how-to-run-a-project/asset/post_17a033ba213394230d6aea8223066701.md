You should allow the contractor’s tradesmen a supply of water and electricity together with the use of lavatory and washing facilities.

## K Construction to Practical Completion stage

### Stage description
Stage k, Construction to Practical Completion, covers the administration of the building contract and the issuing of any further information, as may be required, to the contractor.

### RIBA Plan of Work
The RIBA Outline Plan of Work 2007 describes the key tasks for this stage as, ‘Administration of the building contract to practical completion. Provision to the contractor of further information as and when reasonably required. Review of information provided by contractors and specialists.’

### ID/10
Stage k is an ‘Installation Management’ stage on page A of the Schedule of Interior Design Services in ID/10 unless the designer is acting as contractor in which case it is a ‘Building Works’ stage.

- **Tasks: Interior designer** When acting as the interior designer in a design team, the designer will carry out only the following task (deliverables in bold):
  - Advise on firms to be invited to submit tenders for specialist items and/or FF&E; invite, appraise and report on tenders to client.

- **Lead consultant** When acting as lead consultant, the designer will carry out the task above as well as the following five tasks:
  - Administer the building contract.
  - Conduct meetings with the contractor to review progress.
  - Visit the site at intervals appropriate to the stage of construction to monitor the progress and overall quality of the building works and receive the site inspector’s reports.
  - Provide information to the quantity surveyor for the preparation of valuations of work carried out and financial reports to the client.
  - Issue payment certificates and a certificate of practical completion.

- **Contractor** When acting as contractor under Annex A of ID/10, the designer will carry out only the following two tasks:
  - Carry out and complete the building works and hand over to client.
  - Issue invoices to the client periodically and receive payment.

- **FF&E procuring** When procuring FF&E under Annex B, sections B1 and B2, of ID/10, the designer will carry out the following two tasks:
  - Agree with the client the basis of paying for items of FF&E (e.g. against pro forma invoices or from a client account).
  - Issue purchase orders in the name of the client (with copies to client) for items of FF&E.

- **FF&E supplying** When supplying FF&E under Annex B, sections B1 and B3, of ID/10, the designer will carry out the following task (shown in the ‘FF&E Supply’ stage, pages 234–241):
  - Issue purchase orders to suppliers for items of FF&E and sales orders to the client for the supply of such items.

On smaller projects, stages j, Mobilisation, k and l, Post Practical Completion, are often carried out in the same exercise.
