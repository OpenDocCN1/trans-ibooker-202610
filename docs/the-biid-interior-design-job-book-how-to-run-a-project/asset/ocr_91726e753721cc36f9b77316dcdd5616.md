**Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights Anytown for Mr M Ployer**

Our client is contemplating alterations and an extension to this terraced property which we believe will have implications with regard to party walls shared by neighbours both to the left and to the right.

We would be grateful therefore if you would supply us with a quotation for providing our client with party wall services sufficient to satisfy all the applicable legal requirements.

We enclose a copy of the client’s brief to provide you with an indication of the work being contemplated.

Arrangements should be made direct with our client to view the property and obtain details of the adjoining owners at m.ployer@ployer_enterprises.co.uk.

The quotation should be received by us before 12 noon on 4 February 2007. The survey should be delivered to ourselves within 21 days of an acceptance of your quotation.

Please note that the employer is not required to accept the lowest or any other tender and that the tender documents have been prepared for the benefit of just him and cannot be relied upon by third parties.
