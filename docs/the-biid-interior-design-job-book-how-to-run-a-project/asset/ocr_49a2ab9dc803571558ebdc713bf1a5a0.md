# **Description**

This section covers the enforcement of fees and other sums due to the designer when a client is reluctant to pay.

# **General**

Unfortunately, a few unscrupulous clients are sometimes tempted to avoid sums due, often alleging negligence on the part of the designer as a reason for not paying, particularly at the end of a job when they perceive that the designer's services will be no longer needed. This typically manifests itself in complaints by the client that the designer did not follow the client's instructions or did not perform competently.

When this happens, then the designer should look carefully at the available options as set out in '£C Payment collection', page 258.
