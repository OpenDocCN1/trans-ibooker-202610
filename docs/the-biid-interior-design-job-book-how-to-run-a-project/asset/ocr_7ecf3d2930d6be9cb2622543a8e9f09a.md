Dear Mike

Proposed Alterations and Extension to 42 Supernova Heights

Now that you have approved the Technical Design stage, we are able to apply for building regulations approval.

We can arrange for such an application to be made to the local authority who would require that a fee be paid before such an application be considered. We have calculated the Council's fee for this to be £2,120. If you wish us to do this, please send me a cheque in that sum made out to Anyshire County Council.

Alternatively we can arrange for the application to be dealt with by a Corporate Approved Inspector. These are private-sector firms approved by the Construction Industry Council to process such applications. In my experience, these firms often provide a better, faster service for fees which are less than those charged by local authorities. If you wish, I can obtain fee quotations from three such inspectors for your consideration.

Yours sincerely
