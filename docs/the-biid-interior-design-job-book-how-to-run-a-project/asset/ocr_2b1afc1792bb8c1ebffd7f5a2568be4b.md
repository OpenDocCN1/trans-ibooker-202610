If you see anything that concerns you, or you wish to make a change, you should not instruct Durr but discuss the matter with us. This allows us to consider the advisability of such a change, confirm that it would conform to the building regulations and planning consent, and obtain an estimate of its effect on budget and programme. We have explained to Durr that, under the terms of the building contract, we would not be able to certify sums for any work not the subject of the contract or a designer’s instruction.

The terms of the building contract require you to pay within 14 days to Durr the sums shown in the payment certificates that we will be issuing each month. Failure to do so would be a breach of the contract and could cause very substantial problems.

The law requires that you, within five days of the date of issue of our payment certificate, give written notice to the contractor of the amount you propose to pay. If you wish to deduct an amount from that certified, you must give a second notice within five days together with the reason for doing so. The latter must not be done without good reason and so you should consult me before doing so.

I shall of course be happy to answer any questions that you might have as construction progresses.

Yours sincerely

Dee
