# # E Technical Design stage

Stage E, Technical Design, covers the evolution of the detailed design into a technical design, integrating the consultants’ detailed designs into the scheme, preparing typical construction details and determining building materials and standards of workmanship.

The RIBA Outline Plan of Work 2007 describes the key tasks for this stage as, ‘Preparation of technical design(s) and specifications, sufficient to co-ordinate components and elements of the project and information for statutory standards and construction safety.’

Stage E is an ‘Installation Management Stage’ on page A of the Schedule of Interior Design Services in ID/10.

+   When acting as the interior designer in a design team, the designer will carry out only the following three tasks (deliverables in bold):
+   
+   * Show the specific location of FF&E on the technical design drawings.
+   * Prepare final schedules of internal finishes, sanitary and electrical fittings, doors/ironmongery, FF&E, etc.
+   * Show specific location of service points on the technical design drawings.

+   When acting as lead consultant, the designer will carry out all of the above tasks as well as all of the following:
+   
+   * Develop the developed design drawings into technical design drawings sufficient to co-ordinate components and elements of the project.
+   * Develop the outline specification into a final specification of materials and workmanship.
+   * Develop, if necessary, the draft work schedule into a final work schedule.
+   * Provide information to, discuss proposals with, and incorporate detailed inputs of, other consultants into the technical design.
+   * Provide information to the quantity surveyor for the preparation of a construction cost and time estimate including FF&E.
+   * Review scheme with CDM co-ordinator.
+   * Present the technical design (incorporating the above deliverables) to, and review options with, the client.

On smaller projects, stages C, Concept, D, Design Development, and E are often carried out in the same exercise.
