# British Institute of Interior Design

The British Institute of Interior Design (BIID) is the oldest professional body in Britain that represents only interior designers. Founded in 1965 as the Interior Designers and Decorators Association (IDDA), in 2005 it merged with the International Interior Designers Association to become the British Interior Design Association (BIDA). In 2009, Companies House, acting on behalf of the Secretary of State for Science and Innovation, granted the Association the right to incorporate the word 'Institute' into its name. Companies House allows this privilege only to 'professional bodies of the highest standing'.

In 2005, the BIDA collaborated with the Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA) to produce ID/05, a form of consultancy contract between designer and client, which has become the industry standard. This has now been updated to ID/10.

The BIID was invited in 2009 to become a member of the Construction Industry Council (CIC), the association of the consulting bodies in the construction industry. This allows the voice of the interior design profession to be heard at the highest levels of industry and government.
