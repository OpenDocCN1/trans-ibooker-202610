# H Tender Action stage

**Stage description** Stage H, Tender Action, covers the process of obtaining tenders for the work, including tenders of any specialist subcontract or supply items, appraising the tenders and submitting them to the client together with recommendations as how to proceed.

**RIBA Plan of Work** The _RIBA Outline Plan of Work 2007_ describes the key tasks for this stage as, ‘Identification and evaluation of potential contractors and/or specialists for the project. Obtaining and appraising tenders; submission of recommendations to the client.’

**ID/10** Stage H is an ‘Installation Management’ stage on page A of the Schedule of Interior Design Services in ID/10 unless the designer is acting as contractor, in which case it is a ‘Building Works’ stage. Where the designer is supplying FF&E, this task is shown in the ‘FF&E supplied by designer’ stage.

+   **Tasks: Interior designer** When acting as the _interior designer_ in a design team, the designer will carry out only the following task (deliverables in **bold**):
+   * Invite and obtain **interior specialist subcontract/supply tenders** and report to the client.

+   **Lead consultant** When acting as _lead consultant_, the designer will carry out the task above as well as the following three tasks:
+   * Invite and obtain **tenders for the main contract works**.
+   * Assist the quantity surveyor to appraise the tenders received, prepare a **tender cost and time report** including FF&E and negotiate with selected tenderer(s).
+   * Present the **tender report** (incorporating the above deliverables) to, and review with, the client.

+   **Contractor** When acting as _contractor_ under Annex A of ID/10, the designer will carry out only the very first task above (under ‘Interior designer’) together with the following four tasks:
+   * Incorporate the drawings, specification and schedules carried out in previous stages into a **contract documents package**.
+   * Prepare a time and cost tender.
+   * Prepare a **form of building contract**.
+   * Present the three deliverables listed above to, and review with, the client; agree and execute the **building contract**.

+   **FF&E procuring** When _procuring_ FF&E under Annex B, sections B1 and B2, of ID/10, the designer will also carry out the following task:
+   * Invite and obtain **quotations for items of FF&E**; present to, and review with, the client.

+   **FF&E supplying** When _supplying_ FF&E under Annex B, sections B1 and B3, of ID/10, the designer will also carry out the following task:
+   * Present the **priced schedule of FF&E** to, review with, and agree with, the client; arrange for payment.

On smaller projects, stages F, Production Information, G, Tender Documentation, and H are often carried out in the same exercise.
