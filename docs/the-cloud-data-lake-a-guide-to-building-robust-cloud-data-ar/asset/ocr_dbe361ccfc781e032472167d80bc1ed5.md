# Setting for Klodars Corporation

Klodars Corporation is an organization selling rain gear and umbrellas to other retail outlets as well as directly to customers through its website. Its website is powered by an operational data-store. Klodars is motivated to invest in a cloud data lake architecture to better support the expansion and growth of its business. The data platform team will define, build, launch, and operate the cloud data lake to serve the needs of the company. The customers of this data platform are various divisions within Klodars that leverage data to drive product or business insights.
