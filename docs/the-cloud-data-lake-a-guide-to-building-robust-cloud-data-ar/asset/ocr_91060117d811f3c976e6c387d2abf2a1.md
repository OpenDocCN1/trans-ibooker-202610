If you are running Spark on IaaS VMs, you will manage the flavor and number of VMs yourself. The question that you would ask at this point is how to determine the amount of resources you need. While you can use existing best practice articles as a starting point, in my opinion, a PoC, running a scaled-down version of your job and studying the characteristics of the resource consumption, is the most accurate way to understand what your job demands.
