When Klodars Corporation wants to expand to winter gear, it can effectively share its insights with the ski corporations it partners with to promote a better partnership, extending the data mesh architecture. Klodars Corporation is growing rapidly and wants to expand its business to Europe, which has unique data residency and other compliance requirements. Further down the road, when Klodars Corporation acquires other companies, it can onboard the data platforms of the companies they acquired as domains to its existing data mesh.
