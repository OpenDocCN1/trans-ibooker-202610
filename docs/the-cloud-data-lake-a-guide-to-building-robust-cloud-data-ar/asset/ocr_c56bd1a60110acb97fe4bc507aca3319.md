+   - You want to migrate your data lake from either your on-premises systems or other cloud providers to the cloud. In this case, you already have an existing implementation, either as a data warehouse or a data lake, that you will move to the cloud.

Your first steps on your journey to the cloud largely remain the same in either case. You will choose the cloud provider, you will choose the services, and you will set up your infrastructure. The cloud encompasses a wide variety of offerings that are available for you to choose from, each having its strengths and opportunities, so before you move to the cloud, the first thing to remember is that there is no silver bullet or prescribed 12-step process when it comes to making this transition. However, having worked with many customers as well as on cloud migrations myself, I have condensed the customer journey into a decision framework, which consists of the following key steps, as illustrated in Figure 3-1:

+   1. Identify your goals.
+   2. Plan your architecture and deliverables.
+   3. Implement the cloud data lake, either from scratch or by migrating your existing system to the cloud.
+   4. Operationalize and release.
