Written specifically for business and technology leaders hoping to get a grip on the implications of artificial intelligence for their business and the world, *Reimagining Businesses with AI* acquaints readers with both the challenges and opportunities presented by this burgeoning technology. The authors provide an industry-agnostic approach to the topic of AI in business, covering sectors as varied as healthcare, education, citizen services, transportation, financial services, and smart infrastructure.

*Reimagining Businesses with AI* shows you how to recognize and understand your firm’s opportunities for growth and change. You’ll learn to build a technology foundation to guide your firm’s future decision-making and design a framework for business transformation your executives will actually use. The book explains how managers and executives can manage the disruptive nature of this powerful technology while simultaneously harnessing its immense potential.

Beginning with a brief history of the development of artificial intelligence, the book goes on to provide actionable AI strategies for modern firms. It concludes with an insightful examination of the broader consequences of the spread of AI, including security, ethics, and privacy concerns.
