# 01 Rules-Based Maintenance

Supporting clients to identify specific maintenance rules around common asset conditions in need of parts or service

+   - Better understand the historical maintenance needs of a given asset
+   - Learn from past issue that arose
+   - Build rules around common issues with the asset, and others like it

# 02 Automated and Remote

Connecting equipment enables operators to partner with service providers to perform scheduled remote performance checks

+   - Operators can reduce the number of scheduled, manual asset checks per fiscal year
+   - Remote checks from the service provider will produce a detailed report, based on the pre-agreed conditional rules

# 03 Budget Efficiency

Managing asset compliance standards and being aware of parts needs/usage forecast; OPEX budgets are less stressed

+   - Mitigate compliance risks with effective audit reports, which will be predefined and customized by asset use type

# 04 Operational Performance

Ensuring asset performance, and removing down-time can ensure an optimized operational cost per asset within the building

+   - Rules based maintenance, or probabilistic asset assessment, can transform into predictive asset management after enough data has been collected
+   - Predictive maintenance can guarantee no down-time
