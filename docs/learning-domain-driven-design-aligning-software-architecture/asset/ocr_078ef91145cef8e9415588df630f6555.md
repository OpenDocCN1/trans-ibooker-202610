To implement the publishing process, the saga has to listen to the CampaignActivated event from the Campaign aggregate and the PublishingConfirmed and PublishingRejected events from the AdPublishing bounded context. The saga has to execute the Submit Advertisement command on AdPublishing, and the TrackPublishing Confirmation and TrackPublishingRejection commands on the Campaign aggregate. In this example, the TrackPublishingRejection command acts as a compensation action that will ensure that the advertising campaign is not listed as active. Here is the code:
