# 6.3 Structural Pattern of Semantic Search

In the general domain, the identification of semantic connections among entities utilized either statistical methods or machine learning techniques based on the form of linguistic model [36] or combined use of both methods. In the field of medicine, Relation Extraction (RE) system usually utilizes, as knowledge base, UMLS (unified Medical language system) [37]. A proposed system was used to extract a different kind of relations namely detect, treat, sign, and cure among medical entities [38].
