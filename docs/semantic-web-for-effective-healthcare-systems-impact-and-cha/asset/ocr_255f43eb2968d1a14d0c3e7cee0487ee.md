Doctors/healthcare workers build up their particular languages and vocabularies to store and impart general clinical information and patient-related data proficiently. Such terminologies are described by a significant amount of implicit knowledge and optimized for human processing.
Healthcare information systems, on the other hand, need to be able to unambiguously communicate complex and detailed healthcare concepts.
It is a challenging task and requires a significant investigation of the structure and the ideas of healthcare terminologies. However, it tends to be accomplished by developing the healthcare domain ontologies for representing healthcare terminologies. Ontologies are stored in Knowledgebase.
The knowledgebase contains different ontologies from domain ontologies. These domain ontologies mostly include disease ontology, application ontologies, medical terminologies [1]. Not only these even the Task ontologies such as diagnostic and treatment ontologies are stored in the knowledge base. Structure and Unstructured information consist of information generated from the Hospital Information Systems (HIS). This can be patient-related data, his/her medical data, laboratory reports, and Electronic Medical Records (EMR). External information comprises guidelines, international and national protocol, case studies, research articles, and/or research papers.
