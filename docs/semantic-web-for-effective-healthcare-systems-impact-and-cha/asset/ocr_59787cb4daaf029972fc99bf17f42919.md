# 5.1 Introduction

Healthcare data is collected and analyzed to improve the quality of health of patients, reduce costs of treatment, and also to generate meaningful insights for patients and researchers. The clinical decisions taken by the healthcare system are driven by all the available information on the medical data and also patient history for specific kinds of illnesses. This requires validation by medical practitioners [34]. The best care can, however, be guaranteed by providing evidence-based medical information. For this Health professionals need “actionable content.”

The question remains as to how the actionable content should be included in the practice and be made efficient for use by aiding in clinical decision making and administrative capability. As an example, suppose an oncologist has an appointment scheduled with a 44-year-old premenopausal woman.

After some primer examination, it was discovered that the woman has an early stage of breast cancer. It was found that it is triple-negative (ER, PR Her-2 negative), and the woman has a history of breast cancer, in her family. Although this was crucial in diagnosis, she has not gone through genetic testing for a BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutation that would affirm (or dismiss) an inclination to breast or ovarian malignancy.

At the time when the appointment between the patient and the oncologist is going on, details about the options in treatment regimens, health insurance plans for the patient, altering clinical traits to be administered to the patient should be made available, which is just at the click of a button. In other words, the workflow should be generated. The challenges in the processing are multi-fold. Not only the information should be made available, but it has to be filtered and processed for actionable content. There is a need for high-quality data and good metadata which is valid and comparable with other resources. Also, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and clinical decision making play an important role in this scenario. The AI model with ontologies will help to access information from multiple sources and transform it into a stepwise procedure. This would influence doctors or medical practitioners in the decision-making process.

“Actionable content is meaningful actions obtained by analyzing any type of raw data. They help organizations make well-informed decisions and they are obtained as a result of data analytics.” Not all insights are actionable though.
