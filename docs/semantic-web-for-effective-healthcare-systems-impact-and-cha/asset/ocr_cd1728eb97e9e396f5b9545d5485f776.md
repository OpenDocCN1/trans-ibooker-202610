In summary, the current actionable content keyword gets its root and refinement for the last two decades as shown in Figure 5.2. The word Action Plan was coined by [6] Cleveland in 2001, which then was mostly the knowledge extracted from the information churned from the data using different data mining techniques [16]. The outputs of those data mining techniques had many patterns, but they were not informative and transparent to business people because the users were not aware of the most suitable pattern satisfying the business needs. The technicalities and knowledge put forward by researchers were not being implemented as a workable project because it was failing to meet the goals of industries. A lacuna was developed which necessitated the action plan to get converted to actionable intelligence by taking into consideration the different players. However, the involvement of actionable knowledge with artificial intelligence and machine learning has only taken place in recent years as shown by the apex of the triangle in Figure 5.3.
