
During this pandemic, another important issue is the management for bed allocation; AI could be of help to us in deciding a cost-effective manner to do the task. Along with the above mentioned importance, AI can be useful for the protection of personal healthcare issues. This can be done by using various Artificial Intelligence algorithms and models, which include silico screening, mobile based surveys in quarantined areas and many more [24].

Management of COVID positive patients admitted in hospitals/healthcare centers is not a new challenge as it is just a new model for an old problem which occurred even before this pandemic. Usually, several therapies are taken concurrently by patients who have been admitted in healthcare centers, which is probably due to their health conditions. Sometimes, fixed guidelines are absent and this encourages the doctors/healthcare practitioners to make some decisions from his/her acquired knowledge, common belief and mostly experience. But, all this can be resolved with the advent of Artificial Intelligence due to the fact that it has the potential to compute large volumes of data, make models on it and analyze the right choices so that no wrong practice is done.

Finally, AI and AI based methods will make the answering of difficult questions with good validity and reliability for each individual patient more probable. This has been the main reason why focus of studies has shifted to Artificial Intelligence in the fields of respiratory problems and also in managing of COVID patients. This is mainly done as AI can assist in creating an effective approach for these problems. Large datasets of heterogeneous and complex data are used for decision making by many healthcare professionals. If Artificial Intelligence is used effectively and efficiently in this sector, it can significantly reduce the complexity of the datasets by conversion of this data into more useful and relevant information [25–27].

Besides, AI is well known for having a defining role in determination of respiratory infectious diseases along with the determination of some serious obstructive lung disease. Still, we require studies at a higher scale with hard proofs for validating the AI findings. Also, we need proof for increasing the acceptance of AI by the healthcare sector as even the slightest of mistakes in these approaches can prove to be very fatal in such an industry.

Looking alternatively, making slight errors in medicine are always very costly and can be largely and more efficiently prevented with the help of Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, and various Machine Learning algorithms and models. The role of AI in decision making can be of great interest especially when sufficient data or physical evidence is unavailable.

According to a study, AI has the potential to reduce medical treatment expenses by approximately 50% and also medical errors by around 35% [28]. According to WHO, the two factors, which are, faulty diagnosis and medical errors are among the top causes of fatalities around the globe and around 10% of hospital admissions can lead to a medical error and around 0.3% admissions result in death as a result of these medical errors. So given this current scenario, AI is unarguably the most efficient way forward for healthcare institutions to eliminate these errors and make healthcare more safe and efficient [29].

Deep learning algorithms and models are now being used extensively in bioinformatics and classification. This is also being considered as a very impactful tool for analysis of infectious diseases. Considering another case which has provided some brilliant results is the use of ANN (artificial neural networks) for creation of models for classifying patients with respect to their respiratory problems [30].

This technology can further be utilized for the monitoring of the various trends in the healthcare sector, this will make the following aspects plausible in day-to-day practice:

- facial recognition
- facial detection
- facial action unit detection
- posture recognition
- sound pressure level detection
- visitation frequency detection
- light level detection

The use of Artificial Intelligence based decision making engines to efficiently manage patients with COVID-19 admitted in the healthcare institutes will provide assistance to the healthcare practitioners. The availability of accurate and comprehensive data is required for the design of this argument engine. This means that collection and storage of records on COVID prone patients has been seen as being of utmost importance for the making of models. Furthermore, disease registry systems need to be present for patients dealing with COVID.

As of now, healthcare sectors of most of the countries’ are focused on controlling the pandemic along with properly treating the affected patients. Another concern is the production and manufacturing of the right vaccine and that too as quickly as possible. Along with the above, focus should also be on having quicker implementation of a complete and faster system for registry of the infected people. These efforts done by the whole world in defeating COVID-19 could be effectively aided with the introduction of decision-making system which use AI models. This would help in easier and better management of COVID patients.

COVID-19 will not only effect the lifestyle of people for a short term, but, will have a long and persistent impact on the society both mentally as well as economically. Each country has taken an economic hit, due to COVID-19, in a different way. This can be seen with respect to the GDP (Gross Domestic Product) of each country which has declined drastically over the first quarter of 2020 [31]. It will take years if not decades to recover these losses.

Nevertheless, COVID-19 has had its own positives also. This pandemic has led to a public which is more willing to accept and act on public health messages. Sources have also claimed a significant reduction in air pollution (20–30%) in many major cities around the world [32].

Also the enhancements made in the healthcare industry across the world during these times would help in the betterment of healthcare facilities in the longer run. Perhaps the most prominent change, which has been encountered is the widespread adoption of telehealth services and technologies which require the use of video calling to connect patients with both urgent and preventive care centers without their having to leave home. These Telehealth services has the potential to drastically expand healthcare to patients in remote areas where the access might be limited or extinct. It also has the potential to let other doctors or healthcare workers to handle patient screening and post-care follow-ups when a local facility is overloaded.

Also, the pandemic has emphasized on the importance of looking ahead and preparing for a crisis for the healthcare providers. If the spikes in COVID-19 cases was not controlled, it had the potential to overwhelm and overburden the hospitals and at the same time creating drug and equipment shortages. That’s the reason these healthcare professionals maneuvered new and innovative ways for predicting the location and severity of future rise in COVID-19 cases. This technological advancement in the healthcare industry can be of utmost importance in predicting and restricting any pandemic beforehand in the future.

## 14.5 Conclusion

All the efforts that the healthcare industry all around the world is putting in to defeat this COVID-19 could have a real benefit with the inclusion of an AI-based decision-making system. This would not only help in increasing the rate of COVID-19 testing but also help in creating a system which manages the patients with ease and a higher efficiency and especially the ones in critical care during this pandemic.
