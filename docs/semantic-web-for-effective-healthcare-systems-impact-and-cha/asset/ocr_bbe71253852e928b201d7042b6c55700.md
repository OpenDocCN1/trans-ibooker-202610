# 7.2 The Necessity for Surgical Robots

Minimally Invasive Surgery has been subjected to various forms, however, Robotic Surgery can be said to be the most advanced of it all. The earliest works of robotics and automation trace back to 400 B.C., however, its application in the field of surgery has only been 20 years old [3].

A major role towards the establishment and development of the field has been a result of the pioneering efforts of surgeons like Archytas of Arentum and visionaries like Vinci, and Jaquet-Droz. They were the first of all to envision the idea of Minimally Invasive Surgery which acted as a founding stone toward the rise of robotic surgery.
