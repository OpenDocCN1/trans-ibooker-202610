# 5.5.1 Ontology-Driven Actionable Content Discovery in Healthcare Domain

Actionable content is a type of information that assists in providing a choice of medical solutions to patients. It may provide the procedure or conceptual theory or description to act or take a medical decision. There are six attributes for any actionable content [33] Alignment, Context, Relevance Specify, Novelty, and Clarity. To generate actionable content that has these attributes, it needs big and diverse data sources, which need to align or establish a coherent link among them. It also needs to understand the context and relevance of the information which needs to be provided.
