Although the complexity of these diseases still exists, this disease can be caused by multiple factors and reasons (genetic, non-genetic, infectious agents, toxic agents, etc.). Today the list of an organization working on rare diseases is pretty wide [9]. Some non-profit organization that works on rare disease research are Orphanet, Rare Disease International (RDI), International Classification of Diseases (ICD), etc. Also, there is a regional independent organization such as Europe, Asia, the USA that has its organizations some linked with international some not. India has a Centre of Excellence for Rare Disease (COERD) organization for the rare diseases.

All these organizations have been working on research, diagnosis, and cure of rare disease. The main objective of all these organizations is to study the natural history of the disease, its cause, and ways for improving the quality of life of patients. Even after the correct diagnosis, 95% of these rare diseases still lack FDA approved treatments. Some organization like NORD (NORD’s Rare Disease Research Grant Program), ORD, GeRaD, Rare Disease Fund, Every Life Foundation for rare diseases, etc. encourages physicians and researchers worldwide to study and grant funding. The rate of problems faced by rare diseases, increasing diagnostic odysseys, and prevailing for long decades, thousands of rare diseases led to an insight of global cooperation and collaboration, to capitalize and maximize the efforts on research of Rare Disease around the world.
