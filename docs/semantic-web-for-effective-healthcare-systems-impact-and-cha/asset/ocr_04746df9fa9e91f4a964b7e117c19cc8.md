**Table 9.1** Different terms used in computational examination [13].

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| Terminology | Group of ideal or conventional terms in a field that might be a standard name endorsed by a central body or merely a frequent use originating in a particular practice |
| Thesaurus | Terminology witch groups equivalent words into classes |
| Index | A roster of crucial terms picked automatically from a body of the semi-structured or unstructured text. Upon searching, an index can speed up reaching pertinent results |
| Controlled Vocabulary | Group of ideal terms generated particularly for a body of text or field |

| Classification | Controlled vocabulary which thoroughly defines, from a theoretical point of view, a field or subject and is not described exclusively by the body of the text |
| Statistical Classification | Classification where each principle is reciprocally unique for preventing duplicate calculation.
