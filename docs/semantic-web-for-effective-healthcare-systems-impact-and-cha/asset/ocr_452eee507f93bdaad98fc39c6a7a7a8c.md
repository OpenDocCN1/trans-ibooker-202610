The characteristics of rare diseases depend on distinct factors. Several factors such as disease history, clinical research, available disease monitoring, treatment evaluation, etc. are often considered for the characterization of rare diseases. Moreover, geographical factors (geographic area, geographic, country, geographic international, etc.) also plays a major role [9, 10]. There might be diseases rare in one part of the world but common in the other part. For example, tuberculosis, a rare disease, is common in less economically developed countries but rare in developed countries. Thus the range of rarity may change from rare to extremely rare or rare to common, and vice versa.
