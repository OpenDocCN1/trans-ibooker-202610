She also has radiology reports and images of Mammography/ sonography, the biopsy reports indicating the histopathological confirmation of malignancy with the associated immunotherapy reports of estrogen/ progesterone receptors along with her reports. There may be detailed radiological reports and images as well to rule out any metastatic disease. These reports are automatically stored in a Laboratory Information System (LIS).

The oncologist reviews the same by retrieving from the repository layer and after detailed medical examination records his findings. He also suggests any pending medical investigations to be done and reviewed. All the reports and findings are fed into the Hospital Information System (HIS) and the system evaluates them and provides the detailed diagnosis and staging of the patient. The system also provides the various treatment options with regards to the available treatment options of oncosurgery, medical oncology, radiation oncology, hormone therapy, and immunotherapy provided in different combinations best suitable [1]. These recommendations help the oncologist in deciding the best possible workflow of treatment and recommend it to the patient. These recommendations are discussed with the patient at length, with the results of trials supporting them. The best option is chosen by the patient with the support of the oncologist and her family. If the insurance plan is available is discussed with the accepted option and the treatment dates are arranged.
