## 14.4 Discussion

### 14.4.1 What is the Use of AI in Healthcare?

For Healthcare, AI is considered to be a set of computerized applications that imitates human cognition for complex medical information and data with the help of various algorithms and software. AI's main objective is to perform a detailed analysis of the connections between the techniques used for prevention, diagnosis or treatment of diseases and present those results [17]. Application of these Artificial intelligence (AI) programs can be seen during medical procedures such as treatment protocol development, diagnosis processes, patient monitoring, drug development, and various others.

### 14.4.2 How to Use AI for Critical Care Units

Seeing the above advancements which have been made in Healthcare industry with the help of Artificial Intelligence, we can say that, it is very well possible to develop an Artificial Intelligence model to predict if a patient has a higher risk of having a health issue or not based on their past health records [18]. Also, providing software services, which possess the ability to automate the process of categorizing the risks involved in pandemics such as that of COVID on different patients around the world, is considered as a possible solution to this issue [19]. Furthermore, reduction in the frequency of lab testing due to increasing cases is an important issue.

With the help of AI, we can predict the tests performed in laboratories worldwide with even higher accuracy and precisely estimate the probable causes. Also, various surveillance systems have been developed which can monitor the emergence of respiratory tract infections [20].

#### 14.4.2.1 Input Stage

All the items that could be classified under Input stage are briefly summarized here. Almost all the symptomatic patients could be flagged based on their signs and symptoms of COVID even before clinical testing. This is an early alarming system, which can be created with the help of AI. In fact, the detection of COVID Positive patients in clinical testing will take considerable time and also visiting facilities will put the patient under higher risk because of being in the vicinity of other high-risk patients due to the lack of space available to each facility. Also, data mining approaches can be used for the prediction of epidemiological data.

Building on this method, pandemics could be flagged even before healthcare industries discover them. This would be a beneficial option as it can save many other people who might come in contact with them. In addition to the above method, hotspots can be defined (places with high number of cases) along with some other places which will be the emerging epicenters in the near future with the help of mobile phone contacts, like the ones used in Arogya setu app in India, data mining approaches, social media data, air traffic maps and many other such possible ways.

Another kind of approach which can be made useful is that of having AI assisted techniques for defining the patterns by which the virus can spread across the community along with telling the most probable effects on the health of patients beforehand on the upcoming steps of the outbreak. Setting an example, COVID-19 outbreak in China was effectively controlled by finding active cases through mobile phones [21]. This was an AI-based method which was used to a community’s effect. AI could also boost personalized medicine by identifying potential and most susceptible people based on each person’s genetics and physiological characteristics. All these approaches will lead to the discovery of therapeutics and diagnostics; which are also, part of the output.

#### 14.4.2.2 Process Stage

All the items that could be classified under Process stage are briefly summarized here. Deep learning can be a very useful tool in differential diagnosis of COVID-19 from various different diseases. Along with this, variations in genetics of severe, mild or even asymptomatic patients of COVID-19 can be used and machine learning analysis can be performed on them to predict potential patients in the future.

This approach would be performed based on keeping in mind how well these patients resist the potential COVID-19 infection. Studies have shown that critically ill patients can be monitored with the help of AI with especial focus on deep learning and pervasive sensing. Alternative approaches include the use of AI for the improvement in the risk communication strategies in patients who are affected by COVID based on their location and timing.

#### 14.4.2.3 Output Stage

All the items that could be classified under Output stage are briefly summarized here. Regarding output stage, the following items could be briefed as follows. COVID-19 being a systemic disease, affects the lungs with its most lethal complications. However, a confirmatory diagnosis of the patient is mandatory, before starting treatment. This relies on patients’ past records, physical examination records and other clinical data. One of the most sensitive items still remains the Chest CT scans [22, 23].

Adding to the above, we can include CRISPR roles for detection of COVID-19 and enhance the speed of diagnosis and also the accuracy of the tests. An AI based system for patients that require critical care can also analyze the efficacy of lab tests for diagnosis of COVID and other related diseases and therefore, enhance its efficiency. Based on past experiences, AI based methods would help improve therapeutic approaches for COVID-19 patients.

One of the challenges that critical patients may face is the absence of proper methods and models for early detection and better management of critically ill patients undergoing perpetuated tracheostomy and mechanical ventilation. Here, a probable solution is brought forward with the use of AI. Along with the above Artificial Intelligence has paved out approaches which build upon algorithms like the Random Forest to manage and predict difficult airway for patients.

During the current times, production of COVID-19 vaccine is a challenge for the healthcare systems all over the world. In these times, Artificial Intelligence has come to the rescue and a new vaccinology tool has been proposed which integrates the healthcare with machine learning; this has come out as one of the major candidates in the field. Another mode of vaccine production which is influenced by in silico models has also been claimed useful in this field. These are the ones which again rely on Artificial Intelligence, databases and also on in silico tools.

The difficulties in the decision-making processes have been the major aspect, which appears whenever we have discussions about COVID patients. Artificial Intelligence along with its related models and algorithms could prove very effective in enabling better decision making. This process starts with estimation of the risks involved. This is followed by diagnosis of dataset, forecasting of medical and survival trends, then, the risks involved are classified and according to various categories specified followed by predicting the risk on the dataset provided. Finally, we predict the survival percentage according to the model formed.
