# 8.1 Introduction

Smart Health is an on growing technology that has been used in Healthcare Industry for the avoidance, identification, treatment and follows up of the patient anywhere and at any time with the help of connecting IoT devices [1], patients and doctors altogether with each other. Smart Health basically involves the digitalization of healthcare by implementing all time connectivity, using intelligent techniques and applying technology [2]. IoT helps the devices to gather the data and exchange that automatically. Doctors are now capable of providing early diagnosis, preventive methods of care, and better treatment options more efficiently. Patients can get the solution of their problem in real time environment [3]. In this research paper, the major focus is on IoT devices that can be used in healthcare for collection of patient data and communicating that data to healthcare professionals for evaluation. IoT in Healthcare Industry is beneficial for patient, doctor, hospitals, patient’s family members, and even healthcare insurance companies also [4].

a) Patient Care by IoT—Nowadays, a lot of wearable IoT devices [4] are available and is being used by patients that have been used to monitor vital parameters like Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, etc. These devices can also be used to send regular reminders to patients [5] eg water consumption reminder, calorie counter, variation in parameters if any etc. People’s lives has been changed with the invention and usage of IoT in healthcare specially for old age people [6] and patient’s living alone are much benefitted with the advent of this technology [4]. Further, these IoT devices also transmit signals to the patient’s family members as well if find something different in routine parameter or daily activities [7].

b) Usage of IoT for Doctors—Using IoT doctors are now able to keep an eye on the patient’s health all the times where a patient can use wearable and other sensors or monitoring devices to get in touch with the doctor [8]. Doctors can check the patient’s adhesiveness toward treatment also [9]. IoT capacitate the healthcare workers to be more vigilant and connect with the doctor dynamically in case of any emergency situation. Apart from this, the data collected from IoT, also assist the doctor to find the best possible treatment, which can be given to the patient for the better cure [10].
