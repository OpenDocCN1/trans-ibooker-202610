# 10.6 Discussion for Further Research and Development

A fully automated knowledge base curation may not be currently possible because of certain connected issues. First of all, the Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) does not allow full literature to be freely obtained from the web sources for use in the system. Secondly, there are contradictory literature available that could undermine the reliability of the knowledge base even if it is automatically populated. The third reason is that the literature need not be complete that could cause problems in curating the knowledge base. So, an expert is required to monitor the curation process of the knowledge base if the application has to be robust. Any possible method of a fully automatic curation process will be of great use to create an independent system.

Another possible research and development will be on effectively making use of extended RDF Knowledge Graph to rank the knowledge in the knowledge base. It could help remove the obliterated knowledge from the knowledgebase and also help in the rank-based evaluation. There are some studies already done in adding weights to RDF Triples [94] but further study and research will be useful.

# 10.7 Conclusion

RDF Knowledge Graphs have several benefits when it comes to making a reliable and efficient knowledgebase that is of utmost importance to systems like the CDSS. It provides a concise and intuitive abstraction of the knowledge from structured, semi-structured and even unstructured sources. For a system such as the knowledgebase CDSS, the accurate and recent knowledge has to be in the knowledgebase for the system to be useful for any healthcare personal. At the same time, the knowledgebase, although scalable, has a limitation in its space complexity. To provide a budgeted space requirement, the proposed model of using RDF Knowledge Graph to build the knowledgebase of a system such as a CDSS is advantages since the entire graph of an unstructured source is not held within the system permanently. Moreover, the ability of the model to abstract knowledge from unstructured sources such as medical literature that hold the recent and relevant knowledge makes the proposed model important.
