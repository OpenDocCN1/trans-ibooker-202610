# 12.6.6  Medical Subject Headings: MeSH

MeSH is a precise vocabulary by the US National Library of Medicine (NLM) used to organize the database. It is also used for searching the required data on PubMed, and also some other databases where information is fitted into a hierarchy. The MeSH is available on PubMed’s homepage and it allows the users to locate the information for any disease and find its position in the MeSH hierarchy. Some of the subheadings found on the homepage for rare diseases are analysis, classification, metabolism, statistics and numerical data, etc. [21].
