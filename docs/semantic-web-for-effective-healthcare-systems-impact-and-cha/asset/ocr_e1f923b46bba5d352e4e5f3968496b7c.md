Their focus was more pivoted towards designing and deploying a dexterous telemanipulator that was capable of performing the hand surgery. As their work continued, US Army observed the potential valuation of their research and realized a need for a similar (or same) system for their armed forces. The pain point that stood out the US Army was the possibility of preventing any form of exsanguinating of wounded soldiers with the help of these advancements and mobile surgical robots [1–6].
