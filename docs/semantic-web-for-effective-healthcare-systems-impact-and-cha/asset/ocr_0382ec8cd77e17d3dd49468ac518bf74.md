# 4.1.1 Key Benefits and Usage of Technology in Healthcare System

Due to ever growing data on the web and in context of globalization, health information technology is also much in demand [36]. Stakeholders of this information access it to share, to comparing with other alternative sources of information, transfer to others using network [2]. All developments in the health services are done with this thing in mind. So, use of technology has become must to facilitate such extensive use of information. +   - Data management systems for keeping patient’s data automatically retrieve data corresponding to a patient from the patients’ requirements in terms of medical equipment, medication, etc. [3].
