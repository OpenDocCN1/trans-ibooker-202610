# 9.9 Ontology in the Contexts of Gene Identification Research

HPO has been used in many ways in research on disease pharmacokinetics, diagnostic methods, and gene discovery programs. It was used to offer clusters of genes involved with one or more HPO terms to filter candidate gene clusters [31], to prioritize candidate genes in Exome sequencing research through PhenIX, Phevor, or Exomiser [32], and to identify understood or new disease genes or to examine large cohort structural variations [33]. The Deciphering Development Disorders (DDD) study investigated 4125 families with various developmental disorders and identified four new disease gene links by combining genotype analysis and phenotypic resemblance in almost the same candidate gene in patients with recessive variants [34].
