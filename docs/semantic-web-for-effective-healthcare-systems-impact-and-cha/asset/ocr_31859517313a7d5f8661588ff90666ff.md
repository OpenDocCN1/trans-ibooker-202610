# 5.3.2  Semantic Technology for Prescriptive Health Analytics

As quoted above, the traditional artificial intelligence (AI)/ML techniques do not address the issue of heterogeneity, interpretability, and sharing of information between multiple systems. However, all these issues can be addressed by semantic technologies and which will also be useful for discovering actionable content from the data. Semantic technology is a tool to establish a semantic link among the various underlined data sources, which will help meaningful ways of a coherent understanding of information that can be converted in terms of actionable information.

Semantic technologies are “meaning-centered.” Semantic Technology develops its languages to express rich self-describing interrelation of the data and linking it in a form that is processable by the machine, despite it being in a long string of characters and index tons of data [32]. It also identifies the meaning and logical relationship of the data and can store manage and restrict its information.
