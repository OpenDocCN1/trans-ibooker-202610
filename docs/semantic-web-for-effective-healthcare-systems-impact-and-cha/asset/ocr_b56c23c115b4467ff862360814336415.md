[23] survey literature for a global way to develop taxonomy as the lack of a proven method to design a domain-specific industry taxonomy hinders development of a complete, encompassing, and error-free domain-specific industry taxonomy. They present a method called the Industry Taxonomy Engineering Method (ITAXEM), to design Industry Taxonomies in a structured manner. In this method, the team borrows from existing methods, and adds the missing on top of it taking into account stakeholder viewpoints. This approach makes the ITAXEM method applicable to any industry.

Because of the complexities of medical terminologies and the fact that a medical term can meaning differently in different contexts, lay people easily misunderstand terms commonly used by physicians. Cosic et al. [24] tested how well patients understand terminology commonly used with them in inpatient and emergency settings of an orthopedic department. Two key findings from this study are that patients (1) do not completely understand healthcare terms commonly used by the professionals, and (2) were ignorant of this fact that they have a poor understanding of orthopedic terms. The findings of this study highlight the seriousness of having clear terminology in use to enable (a) effective communication between healthcare professionals and patients, (b) transparent and informed dialog with patient to get consent to care, and (c) patient satisfaction regarding health outcomes.
