**c) Usage of IoT for Hospitals**—Not only doctors and patients but hospitals are also get affected by the use of IoT devices. Sensors used in IoT devices can be used to keep track of location of various medical tools and appliances in real time, e.g., Wheelchair, oxygen cylinders, stretcher, etc. [11]. Other than this, hospitals can also decide that where to locate the hospital staff. Resource management of the hospitals resources can also be done using the IoT devices [12], e.g., Drugstore catalog record management, temperature control of the whole or partial area, etc. [13].

**d) Usage of IoT for Insurance Corporations**—Health Insurance Corporations can also get better opportunities of their business activities using IoT devices. These organizations can use the data collected from IoT devices for the verifications of the claims made by the patients [14]. Using the data, company can verify whether the claim is genuine or fraudulence is there [15]. Further, Insurance companies also offer various types of perks to their customers upon usage of IoT devices and sharing data with the company [16]. These companies pay off the customer for using IoT devices in order to monitor the daily activities of the patient. This helps insurance companies to minimize the claims [17].
