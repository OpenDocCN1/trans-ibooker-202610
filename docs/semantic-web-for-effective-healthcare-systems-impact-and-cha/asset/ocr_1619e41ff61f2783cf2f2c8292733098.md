The contribution of this approach is that it presents a formal framework for the discovery of actionable knowledge, action ability of patterns, and deliverables. But the main limitation of this approach is that it does not consider characteristics and diversity in data. As the volume and velocity of data inflow increased last decade by many folds the heterogeneity in data in terms of syntactic, semantic have also to be considered which is not the case.

Fantuzzo et al. [15] have used the term actionable intelligence for actionable knowledge. The word Actionable Intelligence is involved in both data and research. It is derived from the combined contributions of various key contributors such as executive leaders, practitioners, researchers, and citizens [14]. The output of data mining is getting data with some meaning in it which would help in formulating a theory of change and is further tested. Hence the name Actionable Intelligence because of the contributions it makes in churning the intelligence from data.
