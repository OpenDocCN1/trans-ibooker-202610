COVID-19 will not only effect the lifestyle of people for a short term, but, will have a long and persistent impact on the society both mentally as well as economically. Each country has taken an economic hit, due to COVID-19, in a different way. This can be seen with respect to the GDP (Gross Domestic Product) of each country which has declined drastically over the first quarter of 2020 [31]. It will take years if not decades to recover these losses.

Nevertheless, COVID-19 has had its own positives also. This pandemic has led to a public which is more willing to accept and act on public health messages. Sources have also claimed a significant reduction in air pollution (20–30%) in many major cities around the world [32].

Also the enhancements made in the healthcare industry across the world during these times would help in the betterment of healthcare facilities in the longer run. Perhaps the most prominent change, which has been encountered is the widespread adoption of telehealth services and technologies which require the use of video calling to connect patients with both urgent and preventive care centers without their having to leave home. These Telehealth services has the potential to drastically expand healthcare to patients in remote areas where the access might be limited or extinct. It also has the potential to let other doctors or healthcare workers to handle patient screening and post-care follow-ups when a local facility is overloaded.

Also, the pandemic has emphasized on the importance of looking ahead and preparing for a crisis for the healthcare providers. If the spikes in COVID-19 cases was not controlled, it had the potential to overwhelm and overburden the hospitals and at the same time creating drug and equipment shortages. That’s the reason these healthcare professionals maneuvered new and innovative ways for predicting the location and severity of future rise in COVID-19 cases. This technological advancement in the healthcare industry can be of utmost importance in predicting and restricting any pandemic beforehand in the future.
