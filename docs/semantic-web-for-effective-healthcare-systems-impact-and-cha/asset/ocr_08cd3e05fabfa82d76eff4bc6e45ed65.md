# 7.12 Conclusion

From the above comparative study, we can safely conclude that the applications of robotic surgery have spanned to multi-dimensional areas. There stands no doubt that robotic surgery has various key advantages over traditional surgical procedures like VATS, or laparoscopic PD. The traditional approaches might appear a suitable choice in case of severe complications or circumstances arising on an in-depth analysis of the patient’s history and conditions on a case-to-case basis. However, the consensus always lies in the favor of robotic automation of surgical procedures.

# 7.13 Future Work

Medically, robotic surgeries are the favored choice. Given the fact that they provide less blood loss, less postoperative complications, and most importantly for the patients a shortened hospital stay. However, the question arises is that given the advantage that robotic-assisted surgeries have to offer to offer to the patients, there lies a sense of paranoia and skepticism when given the choice to opt for it arises among the patients as well as the surgeons. Is it the underlying failures and faulty cases associated with robotic surgical procedures or the lack of skill to handle the complex system among the surgeons? The further chapters in this series shall deal with the same.
