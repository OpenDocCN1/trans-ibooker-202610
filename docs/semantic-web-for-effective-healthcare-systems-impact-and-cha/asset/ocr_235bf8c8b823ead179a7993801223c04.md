The concept of semantic web was introduced in order to make the information understood and processed not only by machines but also by humans. The semantic web will help in connectivity of online data, efficient searching and browsing, effective communication and human interaction. SW technologies are tools that facilitate the management and sharing of knowledge between systems. The chapter aims to highlight the role that semantic web technologies can play in development of improved searching solutions in healthcare domain. The importance of incorporating semantics, how to utilize them, and different types of resources available have been discussed thoroughly.
