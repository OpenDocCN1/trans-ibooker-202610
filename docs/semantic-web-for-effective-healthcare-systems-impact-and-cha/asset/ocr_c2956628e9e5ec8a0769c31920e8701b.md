# 8.10 Conclusion

Healthcare with IoT is an emerging area nowadays and is a major topic of research among researchers. The usage of IoT approach has mechanized the treatment process and patient’s care. IoT based devices has established strategies to deal with patient, doctor and hospitals and started providing smarter and faster services. IoT devices makes easy for patient to inform the doctor about their health status anywhere and anytime, for doctor to monitor the patient in real time environment even at the remote location In this research work, we have proposed an IoT based architecture for monitoring the health of remote patients. IoT devices can use sensors to check the status of body vitals like ECG, BP, Blood Sugar, etc. This architecture is very useful for the entire healthcare world and will add on more solutions for monitoring the health.
