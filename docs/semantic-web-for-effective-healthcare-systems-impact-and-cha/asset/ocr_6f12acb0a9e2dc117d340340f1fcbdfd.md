# 3.2.2 Ontology in Patient Monitoring System

The decision making process is very difficult in the field of medicine. The researcher must analyze a lot of input from the heterogeneous knowledge sources. It is also a collaborative process where the experts used to share their information about the clinical data to make the correct decision. The communication level must be semantic rather than the syntactic and also the context of the information does not affect the meaning [2].

Lee et al. proposed a recommendation system for patient’s diet menu using ontology. Diet is considered as an important factor for all people, particularly for diabetic patients. This system gives valuable suggestions for the patient’s diet menu by considering their clinical information and also it recommending a meal plan for diabetic patients [23].

Beale and Heard developed a framework for a clinical information ontology-based model. All kind of clinical information is stored based on the ontology including the number of categories of requirements, ranging from the semantic to the performance of the software at runtime. The classification clinical information is processed by Clinical Investigator Record (CIR) ontology which helps in the problem solving process [26].

Jayaweera developed a Clinics Management System (CMS) based on Patient Centered Process Ontology. It constantly created new specialties, different roles, public and private organizations in patients’ health recommendation system supported in critical issues. It also provides solutions related to IT for various areas of health recommendation systems [28]. Jayaweera and Johannesson created a patient’s ontology repository for information retrieval in the field of healthcare system. This system provides better recommendation on the patient’s health issue. It also focused on the patient’s health history and visualizations can be made based on history [30].

Kumar et al. implemented a clinical guidelines ontology knowledge repository in the field of healthcare system [31]. This research work gave the brief glimpses of ontology related to healthcare guidelines. This framework helps to take decision on patient’s health issue by analyzing their health condition and symptoms on a particular disease.
