
- **Optimization:** Helps in synchronizing between the model, problem, data, and environment by providing suitable optimization techniques.
- **Problem:** This is the knowledge discovery target, which inputs data, business protocols, and the environment and aims at contributing to corresponding knowledge discovery objectives and business analytics goals, along with evaluation and validation of knowledge discovery findings.
- **Data:** Features corresponding to a problem, which reflects the mapping between the business problem and the decisions. This mapping is crucial in selecting the appropriate data.
- **Environment:** The problem and its associated data are enclosed in a certain environment, embodied through organizational or social factors.
- **Model:** A model is required to transform data into proper decisions pertaining to the problem at hand within the constraints of the environment. Model, if biased, is not fully reflective of the problem, data environment, or decision.
- **Decision:** A decision is the knowledge discovered and is presented in terms of identified patterns.

### 5.2.2 Actionable Content in Practice

The three major components of Actionable insight are analytic, synergic, and prognostic insights. Analytic insight concentrates on the understanding of analytical results. It is the output of some database query or some computations that converts raw data into some information. For the patient table, it can be a query that counts the number of patients on a particular day. The next level is the synergic insight which consolidates the output of all analytic insights based on all the objectives and constraints. The user can make synergic insight if we can understand all the analytical insights and establish some sort of relationship between them. An example of synergic insight about healthcare is the decrease of competitive advantage in a particular health department of a hospital. Other prominent examples include employment of a new doctor, some new tool, or instruments of world-class repute purchased in a particular hospital department, which is observed from data visualizations and text mining about the new instrument purchased. Prognostic insight happens when the user can generate a hypothesis and predict how the problem reacts to the hypothesis under different scenarios. For each scenario supporting the hypothesis, the risk involved has also got to be analyzed.

The terms actionable content and actionable insights are often used interchangeably in the literature. However, in our opinion, the level of Actionable content is a level higher than the actionable insights [2, 27]. Actionable content is a verified content by aligning diverse sources and analyzing interrelation among them. However, actionable insights can be the subjective interpretation of data that is useful for the end-user and has value to them. It uses procedural knowledge and consolidates the different insights obtained from the raw data and provides the content accordingly [25]. As per the definition, the understandings which the users get at different stages of the data analytics process, help in arriving at different alternative solutions. From these sets of alternative solutions, the best suited as per the user’s scenario and objectives anticipated are the actionable content.

In the case of the healthcare sector, this cohesive set of understanding includes clinical guidance in terms of:

- Recommendations which give alternative solutions
- Link to the relevant context
- Actionable items

**Recommendations which give alternative solutions:** In this case, there can exist comparisons of different treatment plans. The treatment options can be optimized for personalized medications or treatment implantations.
