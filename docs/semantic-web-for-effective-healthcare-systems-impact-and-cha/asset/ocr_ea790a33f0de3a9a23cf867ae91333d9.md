Semantic technology includes various standards/terminologies such as taxonomy, graphs (triple store), semantic query language (SPARQL), lightweight ontology (RDF, knowledge graph), and heavyweight ontology (OWL) [30].

Among these different types of standards, ontologies are the ones central to semantic technology. An ontology represents encode/decode knowledge which is formalized using descriptive logic and expressed in terms of axioms. Researchers have also used ontologies in the healthcare domain [28].

Ontologies were developed to enable knowledge sharing and reuse in the field of Artificial Intelligence. More recently, the notion of Ontology is also becoming widespread in the fields of intelligence retrieval, electronic commerce, and knowledge management. “An Ontology is a formal explicit specification of a shared conceptualization” [13].
