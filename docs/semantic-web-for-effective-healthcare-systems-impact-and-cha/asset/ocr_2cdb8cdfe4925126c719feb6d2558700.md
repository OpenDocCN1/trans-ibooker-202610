Another advantage of the SPARQL is its portability across multiple programming languages and user interfaces. The difficulty that most knowledgebase systems face is its compatibility with existing systems. With the use of SPARQL on RDF knowledge graph-based systems, the compatibility issue can be reduced to a great extent.

Clinical Decision Support Systems or CDSS are systems that render decision support in healthcare. The knowledgebase CDSS makes use of the knowledge base that is created from multiple sources. The knowledgebase is the backbone of any knowledgebase CDSS. The efficiency, reliability, and robustness of the CDSS depend on the knowledge base. Therefore, the knowledge representation in the knowledge base of the CDSS is the biggest challenge in the creation of a fully functional CDSS. Using RDF knowledge graphs for knowledge representation in the CDSS and using SPARQL for information retrieval has two big advantages over other systems. Firstly, the knowledge representation will be pretty straightforward and visualization of such knowledge will not be difficult. Secondly, such systems will be highly portable. Therefore, the compatibility of such a system with the already existing systems will not become a hindrance to the operation of the systems.
