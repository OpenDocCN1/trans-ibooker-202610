
## 7.12 Conclusion

From the above comparative study, we can safely conclude that the applications of robotic surgery have spanned to multi-dimensional areas. There stands no doubt that robotic surgery has various key advantages over traditional surgical procedures like VATS, or laparoscopic PD. The traditional approaches might appear a suitable choice in case of severe complications or circumstances arising on an in-depth analysis of the patient’s history and conditions on a case-to-case basis. However, the consensus always lies in the favor of robotic automation of surgical procedures.

## 7.13 Future Work

Medically, robotic surgeries are the favored choice. Given the fact that they provide less blood loss, less postoperative complications, and most importantly for the patients a shortened hospital stay. However, the question arises is that given the advantage that robotic-assisted surgeries have to offer to offer to the patients, there lies a sense of paranoia and skepticism when given the choice to opt for it arises among the patients as well as the surgeons. Is it the underlying failures and faulty cases associated with robotic surgical procedures or the lack of skill to handle the complex system among the surgeons? The further chapters in this series shall deal with the same.

## Abstract

The growth of the IoT technology is playing a major role in the development of the medical field and healthcare industry as it becomes very easy and economical for the patients to get connected to the doctors and healthcare professionals at any time virtually. Now there is no need to get physical consultation from the doctor all the times and all this has been done only by the development of Internet of Things. In this research work, the applications of IoT in healthcare are defined. Further how these applications can be used with the help of various sensors is also mentioned. The data that has been collected from these sensors is shared on the cloud for the storage purpose. Patients can use various wearable devices and sensors also to get connected with the doctor all the times. Doctors can also suggest treatments to the patients even at the remote locations. This whole system is much beneficial for critically ill patients as when sensor data provides some extraordinary information about the patient, the doctors can immediately send the notification to the patient or his family about what action is needed to be taken immediately.

Keywords: Cloud, healthcare, sensor, Internet of Things

## 8.1 Introduction

Smart Health is an on growing technology that has been used in Healthcare Industry for the avoidance, identification, treatment and follows up of the patient anywhere and at any time with the help of connecting IoT devices [1], patients and doctors altogether with each other. Smart Health basically involves the digitalization of healthcare by implementing all time connectivity, using intelligent techniques and applying technology [2]. IoT helps the devices to gather the data and exchange that automatically. Doctors are now capable of providing early diagnosis, preventive methods of care, and better treatment options more efficiently. Patients can get the solution of their problem in real time environment [3]. In this research paper, the major focus is on IoT devices that can be used in healthcare for collection of patient data and communicating that data to healthcare professionals for evaluation. IoT in Healthcare Industry is beneficial for patient, doctor, hospitals, patient’s family members, and even healthcare insurance companies also [4].

- Patient Care by IoT—Nowadays, a lot of wearable IoT devices [4] are available and is being used by patients that have been used to monitor vital parameters like Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, etc. These devices can also be used to send regular reminders to patients [5] eg water consumption reminder, calorie counter, variation in parameters if any etc. People’s lives has been changed with the invention and usage of IoT in healthcare specially for old age people [6] and patient’s living alone are much benefitted with the advent of this technology [4]. Further, these IoT devices also transmit signals to the patient’s family members as well if find something different in routine parameter or daily activities [7].

- Usage of IoT for Doctors—Using IoT doctors are now able to keep an eye on the patient’s health all the times where a patient can use wearable and other sensors or monitoring devices to get in touch with the doctor [8]. Doctors can check the patient’s adhesiveness toward treatment also [9]. IoT capacitate the healthcare workers to be more vigilant and connect with the doctor dynamically in case of any emergency situation. Apart from this, the data collected from IoT, also assist the doctor to find the best possible treatment, which can be given to the patient for the better cure [10].
