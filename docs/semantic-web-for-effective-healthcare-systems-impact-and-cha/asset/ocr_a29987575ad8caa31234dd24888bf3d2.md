# 10.1 Introduction

Knowledgebase systems using relational databases have been popular in recent years especially since the incorporation of artificial intelligence into such a system is an easy task. But the multiple tables and identities linking them have been cumbersome. Even to run artificial intelligence algorithms such as machine learning and deep learning algorithms are found to be difficult on such complicated relational database systems. When it comes to complex decision-making systems such as the clinical decision support systems, relational databases have to be an intricate maze. The information retrieval tasks from the knowledge bases of such a system will be a herculean process beyond the possibility of creating an efficient and reliable system. Graph databases on the other hand make use of the semantic structure of knowledge to link various concepts using its relationship edges. Such a structure is much easier to visualize and so, information retrieval will be more straightforward. It will therefore be less resource-hungry than the relational database systems.

Resource Description Framework or RDF makes use of graph framework to represent information in form of triples. The RDF graphs are directed graphs that consist of the concepts for its nodes and the relationship between the concepts for the edges. RDF graphs make use of semantic networks represented using ontology. The RDF graphs are useful to represent complex information in knowledge bases and the simplicity of its structure makes it highly portable across applications in any system.

Representation of knowledge in any knowledge base is a challenge. The knowledge graphs are pragmatic models of knowledge representation that make use of an ontology to form new knowledge. The interlinking of the entity relationships in a knowledge graph contributes to the search efficiency using knowledge graphs. RDF knowledge graphs are machine-readable and there it can be processed in machines. The RDF knowledge graphs are highly interlinked in a logically consistent form permitting machines to reason using it to search and retrieve relevant information. That is why RDF knowledge graphs can be highly efficient in knowledgebase systems that require robust and reliable information retrieval systems such as the Clinical Decision Support Systems.
