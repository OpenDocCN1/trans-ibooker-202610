# 12.6.10 SNOMED-CT: Systematized Nomenclature of Human and Veterinary Medicine—Clinical Terms

SNOMED CT is a designated system of electronic exchange of information by the US Federal government. It is used for obtaining the clinical health information and also generates specifications of the US Healthcare Information Standards Panel. SNOMED CT is a very comprehensive and multilingual healthcare database used for rare diseases with scientific validated clinical data. It is used in more than 80 countries and is mapped according to international standards [24]. SNOMED CT is a growing and upcoming platform that supports the development of quality clinical data in electronic form and allows its interpretation.

SNOMED CT supports the following tools and software on its webpage

+   I. Change or add to SNOMED CT
+   II. SNOMED CT tooling
+   III. Software development
+   IV. SNOMED CT browser
+   V. SNOMED International Managed Service

The webpage also explains Concepts, Descriptions, and Relationships, how to use the tool, and the benefits of the tool to the individuals and population [24].
