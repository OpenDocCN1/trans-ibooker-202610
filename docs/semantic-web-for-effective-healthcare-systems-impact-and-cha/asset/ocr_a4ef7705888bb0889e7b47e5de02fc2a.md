# 12.6.4 Orphanet-INSERM (Institut National de la Santé et de la Recherche Médicale)

Orphanet is a very distinctive information portal for collecting the resources and improving the knowledge about rare diseases to develop its diagnosis and treatment of the affected patients. It also maintains its nomenclature called ORPHAcode, which helps in improving the perceptibility of rare diseases in the healthcare and research information retrieval systems [19]. Orphanet has been established in 1997 by the INSERM i.e. French National Institute for Health and Medical Research in France. But since 2000, it gained much importance by the European Union, and the initiative was supported by a grant from the European Commission and was eventually grown to a Consortium of 40 countries all over the world [19].

It has a platform called “Orphadata” that provides scientific professionals with high-quality data sets regarding rare diseases, also the drugs that are in a reusable form [19].

The webpage of orphaned INSERM consists of the following features:

+   I. It gives permission to access the classification, inventory, and encyclopedia of rare diseases along with its genes involved.
+   II. It has an inventory of all the orphan drugs.
+   III. It consists of a directory of patient organizations.
+   IV. It has a directory of professionals and institutions.
+   V. It also consists of a directory of expert cultures and medical laboratories providing diagnostic tests.
+   VI. It gives access to the collection of thematic reports and the directory of currently ongoing research projects, registries, clinical trials, and biobanks.
