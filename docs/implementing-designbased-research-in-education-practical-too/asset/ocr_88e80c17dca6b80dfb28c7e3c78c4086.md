# Shared Decision-Making

Shared decision-making can be an outcome of well-organised collaborative research meetings. These meetings can take place among some or all team members during the various phases of the study, providing an opportunity for discussion and the coordination of data as they become available.

Research meetings serve as a collaborative platform, primarily focused on generating diverse insights from team members while also fostering trustworthy relationships. This collaborative approach facilitates shared decision-making, enabling the co-defining of problems and solutions. These meetings should be conducted professionally and reciprocally, ensuring that all participants have an opportunity to express their perspectives openly and without judgement.
