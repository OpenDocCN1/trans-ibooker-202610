# DBR and Other Educational Research Approaches

This chapter has set out why DBR has developed as a valuable methodological approach in education and what the key mindsets are that underpin it. For all its benefits, however, it may not always be the most appropriate approach. Research is all about making decisions. Almost always, there is not only one correct approach to address a research question. There are multiple approaches, each of which comes with its own advantages and disadvantages, its own biases and blind-spots, its own principles and processes. The researcher must decide which approach aligns best with who they are, what they aim to achieve, and what they can do within the context in which they work. The researcher must find that sweet spot between what is feasible and what is desirable while always maintaining rigour. No choice will be perfect, but many choices could be appropriate in context.
