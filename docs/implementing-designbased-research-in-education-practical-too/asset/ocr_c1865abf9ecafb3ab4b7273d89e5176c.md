# EXAMPLE IN CONTEXT

Ada and Leo are working as researchers in the area of emergent literacy in the UK. Over the course of their work, they have visited many early years education (EYE) settings and have observed that some of the evidence-based early literacy practices are not in use within these settings. The research evidence to support the effectiveness of these early literacy activities for the long-term literacy outcomes of three- and four-year-old children is very strong. They want to focus their research on improving the literacy outcomes of three- and four-year-olds in early years settings. This could seem like a problem with a simple solution: roll out the most effective early literacy practice in a sample of settings and evaluate its effectiveness. However, from their extensive experience in the field, Ada and Leo are aware that most of the research evidence is from children of middle-class parents in the USA.
