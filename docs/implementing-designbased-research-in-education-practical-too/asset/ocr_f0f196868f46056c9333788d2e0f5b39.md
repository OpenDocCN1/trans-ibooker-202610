In practice, evaluation measures should be closely aligned with the design principles. For example, if a design principle emphasises improved student collaboration, suitable evaluation methods might include recording the amount of time spent in collaborative work or analysing the quality of students’ interactions.

A conjecture map is a particularly useful tool for this process. It visually represents the relationships between contextual factors (e.g., cultural, institutional, technological, interpersonal), design features, and the expected learning outcomes. Unlike design principles, general guidelines refined across multiple DBR cycles, design conjectures are context-specific hypotheses tested within particular settings.
