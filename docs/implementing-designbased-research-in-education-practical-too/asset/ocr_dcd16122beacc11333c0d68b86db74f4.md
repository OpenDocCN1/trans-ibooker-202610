# PREFACE

The idea for this book germinated with the authors as they worked together and separately through a number of DBR projects. We have engaged through this work with the tension between practical real-world application and the goal of generating theory. Our research approach has always sought to develop theory that can do work in the world, aligning ourselves with Lewin, who states that “there is nothing as practical as a good theory” (Lewin, 1943, p. 8).

With each new project came new realisations and new learning about the process: how to develop mutually beneficial and sustainable partnerships with communities of learners, educators, and other stakeholders; how to develop a deep understanding of the lived experiences and needs of these research partners; how to facilitate creative and productive design workshops; and how to ground all of this activity within a powerful theoretical framework.

This book is the book we wanted on our own shelves. Like DBR itself, it aims to tackle the tension between the practical and the theoretical. It deals in-depth with how to work through the phases of DBR in detail and also addresses the theoretical grounding for the approach and its components. Newcomers to the methodological approach could use the book as a step-by-step guide to the process.
