# EXAMPLE IN CONTEXT

Sam and Freddy are researchers in AI. They have built a really great chatbot tool that generates self-reflective prompts and responses for university students on placement because the research literature says that students need to reflect on their practice on placement in a dialogic way in order to develop and improve their practice. They want to test the effectiveness of this tool with a group of student primary teachers out on their first placement. So, they design a study to get student teachers to use their chatbot during the day for just ten minutes to reflect on their teaching and to measure the students’ self-efficacy before and after the study and run a few focus groups. The university lecturers are interested in trying this tool, so they allow Sam and Freddy to run their intervention.
