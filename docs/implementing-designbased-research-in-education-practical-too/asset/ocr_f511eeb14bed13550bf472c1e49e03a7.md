# PRACTICAL TIPS: YOUR DATA PROTECTION CHECKLIST

# Admin

+   - Submit a review form to your institution’s data protection committee (if required).
+   - Confirm requirements before submitting your ethics application to avoid delays.
+   - Contact your ethics committee or research board for guidance.
+   - Record details of any data protection training completed.

# Study Details

+   - Clearly outline ethical measures for working with human participants.
+   - Specify how participants’ personal data will be safeguarded.
+   - Provide written information for participants, including aims, data use, and storage procedures.
+   - Use clear, jargon-free language to support informed consent.
+   - Secure all data with restricted access to maintain confidentiality.

Adhering to these requirements and others outlined by your institution not only protects participants but also strengthens the ethical foundation of your research, reinforcing trust and accountability within the research team and community.

# PAUSE AND REFLECT

Before moving on, ask yourself:

+   - Am I clear on the ethical application requirements of my institution?
+   - Have I drafted a clear data protection protocol for handling sensitive data?
+   - Has my team received appropriate data protection training?
+   - Have I prepared and shared all necessary data protection forms?
