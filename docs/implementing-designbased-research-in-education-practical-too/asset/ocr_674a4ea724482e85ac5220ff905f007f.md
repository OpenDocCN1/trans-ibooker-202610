# Collaboration and Feedback

Purposeful questioning is central to DBR. It drives collaborative feedback, stimulates critical thinking, and supports reflection, empowering teams to generate innovative solutions to educational challenges. Integrated into dialogue, questioning strategies not only sharpen ideas but also ensure that emerging solutions remain both practical and theoretically grounded. In this way, questioning forms the foundation of collaboration, creating spaces where diverse perspectives are valued and where collective knowledge leads to stronger design outcomes.
