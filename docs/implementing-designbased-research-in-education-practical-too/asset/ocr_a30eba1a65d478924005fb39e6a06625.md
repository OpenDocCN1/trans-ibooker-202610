# PRACTICAL TIP: PARTNER MAPPING TEMPLATE

This tool is helpful in identifying key partners and planning engagement strategies for a potential scale-up.

| Partner Group | Role in the Intervention | Preferred Communication | Potential Contributions | Support Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Educators | Implementers, refiners | Workshops, staff meetings | Practical insights, feedback | Planning time, continuous professional development CPD) |
| School Leaders | Decision-makers, enablers | Leadership briefings | Strategic alignment, resourcing | Data on impact |
| Parents/ Guardians | Supporters, context experts | Information sessions, newsletters | Insights on home learning | Clear communication |
| Learners | Users, feedback providers | Surveys, focus groups | Feedback on relevance, motivation | Inclusion in planning |

# PAUSE AND REFLECT ...

+   - Who are the key influencers in this new setting?
+   - How will you support those who will implement the project day-to-day?
+   - What strategies are in place to maintain communication and trust?

# KEY TAKEAWAY

Scaling without partner investment is likely to fail. Sustained and inclusive engagement builds the trust, capacity, and shared ownership needed to grow and sustain an intervention.
