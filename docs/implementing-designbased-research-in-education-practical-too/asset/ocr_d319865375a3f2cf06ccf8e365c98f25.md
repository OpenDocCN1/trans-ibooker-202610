+   - Educators contribute practical, day-to-day classroom experience and understand the real-world challenges learners face.
+   - Learners offer feedback and perspectives that shape the human-centred design of educational tools.
+   - Principals/administrators provide insight into policy and logistical considerations that influence implementation.

# Benefits of Collaborative Engagement

Collaboration in DBR ensures that interventions are designed with a deep understanding of the educational environment and the needs of its participants. This inclusive approach helps create solutions that are more likely to be adopted and sustained.

Advantages of Collaborative Engagement:

+   - **Enhanced Relevance**: By involving those who will use the intervention, DBR ensures that outcomes are closely aligned with classroom realities.
+   - **Shared Ownership**: Educators and other partners who contribute to the design process are more invested in the intervention's success.
+   - **Adaptive Problem-Solving**: Real-time feedback from practitioners enables researchers to identify and address practical challenges quickly during the development phase.

# Strategies for Effective Collaborative Engagement

Successful collaborative engagement in DBR requires intentional strategies to foster communication and shared decision-making.

PRACTICAL TIPS
- Establish clear roles and expectations: Define the responsibilities of researchers and participants from the start to ensure everyone knows their part in the process.
- Create open channels for communication: Regular meetings, focus groups, and feedback sessions help maintain a two-way dialogue.
