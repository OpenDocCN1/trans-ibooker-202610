# Positionality and Research

Every research project is shaped not only by questions, methods, and analysis but also by the researcher’s positionality. It influences design, collaboration, and interpretation, as well as how we navigate challenges and disclosures (Secules et al., 2021). Awareness of positionality highlights the need to avoid assuming stability in our own or others’ perspectives (Holmes, 2020). In essence, it means recognising that we do not possess all the answers and that some of our answers may be incomplete or biased.

Brady (2021) extends this conversation by arguing that educators and professionals must first understand their dispositions in order to hold themselves accountable.
