**PRACTICAL TIP**

One key point to remember: *people don’t always say what they do/mean, and they don’t always do what they say.* As researchers we need to allow for this and expect this in our methodologies, seeking out ways to dig into what we hear and observe for surprises, contradictions, and patterns.

# *Ethics*

Remember, this is a research project, not a design project. If you plan to do anything with human subjects (talk to them, record them, observe them, etc.), you need ethical approval from your institution. You will need to follow the guidance from your institution on good research practices. You will need to think about consent: who needs to provide consent and how they can do this. Bear in mind that you may be in a setting where not all of the individuals are directly involved in the research. You will need to design your use of observations or videos or other data capture to adapt to this. As regards data protection, you need to be compliant with the legislative requirements in your jurisdiction. Again, you will need to design your data generation and storage to take account of this.
