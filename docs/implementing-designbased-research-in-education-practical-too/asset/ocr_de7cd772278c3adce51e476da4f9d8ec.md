# Notetaking

Set aside 15–20 minutes for this simple yet powerful reflection task.

+   **PRACTICAL TIPS**
+   1. Draw yourself at the centre of a blank page.
+   2. List the roles you hold in your DBR project (e.g., researcher, designer, teacher, co-learner).
+   3. For each role, note:
+      a. The beliefs or assumptions underpinning this role are...
+      b. The potential influence of this role on how I interpret data is...
+      c. The tensions or contradictions I experience in this role are...
