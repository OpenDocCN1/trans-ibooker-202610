# Challenges in Scaling DBR Interventions

While scaling up DBR interventions holds great promise, it is not without its challenges. Preparing for and anticipating these challenges is critical to ensuring a smooth and effective scale-up process. These challenges often arise from the complex, variable nature of educational settings and the dynamic relationships between partners, resources, and policy environments.

# Contextual Variability

One of the primary challenges in scaling DBR interventions is the sheer diversity of educational contexts. Differences in school culture, infrastructure, educator readiness, and learner demographics can significantly affect how an intervention is received and implemented. What proves effective in one setting may not align with the values or capacities of another, necessitating continuous adaptation.
