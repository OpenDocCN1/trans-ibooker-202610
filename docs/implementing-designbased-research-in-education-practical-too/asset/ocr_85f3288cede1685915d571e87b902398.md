**Phase 3 Pilot Implementation, Test, and Redesign**
*Objective: Pilot the DBR study with a select group to test and refine the processes.*

| Key Questions to Consider | Supporting Actions |
|---|---|
| What initial pilot goals have you set?
What activities/resources/IT are required to support the pilot?
What open communication has taken place with the DBR team conducting the pilot?
What is the tone of the interactions the DBR facilitator will adopt? E.g. with children it will be playful.
How will the success of the pilot be measured? | Ensure all details of the pilot have been clearly discussed with the DBR team and they have been able to ask questions. Ensure the roles are clear.
Select the most appropriate activities/resources for the task.
Design the environment to fit the requirements of the study and the participants. For example, you may need a quiet room without distractions.
Measure success and report results as you have reported in your methodology section. |

**Reflection and Feedback**
*Objective: Engage the DBR team in collaborative learning and knowledge generation while creating a cycle of participation and contribution.*

| Key Questions to Consider | Supporting Actions |
|---|---|
| What are the emerging findings?
What do team members think about their developing roles?
What educational insights can individual members of the team contribute to support learning?
How do you communicate to members an appreciation for their insights and contribution?
What emerging needs may support the study progression?
What are the most important elements of feedback discussed within the team? | Facilitate team meetings to encourage open communication and feedback.
Discuss emerging roles and find out how the team feels in their current DBR role.
Provide opportunities for team members to contribute their insights, speak freely, and share emerging ideas.
Conduct numerous collaborative research meetings as necessary. Document all meetings for reference and transparency purposes.
Facilitate discussions about all elements of the DBR study to date. |
