PRACTICAL TIPS
Adding the date on which you complete a task may be useful when you review your work at a later stage.

With this groundwork in place, the next step is to initiate communication with participants through email, phone, or in-person meetings. Approach these interactions with professionalism and composure, demonstrating that you have prepared thoroughly and secured the necessary permissions. Open, respectful communication lays the foundation for strong researcher-participant relationships. Just as reflexivity and data protection reinforce ethical responsibility, so too does recognising the people who make DBR possible and the roles they play in shaping its outcomes.
