# PHASE 3 IMPLEMENTATION OF A DESIGN-BASED RESEARCH INTERVENTION

# EXAMPLE IN CONTEXT: SETTING THE SCENE

In the first semester of a large university’s teacher education programme, a group of lecturers introduced a new approach to developing pre-service educators’ skills in maths lesson design. Rather than delivering content through traditional lectures, they trial a series of small, practice-based workshops where students collaboratively plan, micro-teach, and reflect on short maths lessons. The sessions are co-designed by faculty and school-based mentors to ensure alignment with classroom realities. As implementation begins, some students thrive in the active, hands-on format, while others struggle with the ambiguity of open-ended tasks. These early tensions mark the start of an iterative process that will shape the intervention’s refinement over time.
