> Treat each implementation as a learning opportunity. Plan for multiple cycles and allocate time for analysis and redesign. By cycling through implementation and refinement, you increase the likelihood that your intervention will be effective, resilient in the face of real-world complexity, and grounded in a well-tested theory of how and why it works.

# Balancing Flexibility and Fidelity in Implementation

A central challenge in the implementation phase of DBR is navigating the balance between fidelity, which involves remaining faithful to the core design of an intervention, and flexibility, which involves adapting to the realities of diverse educational contexts. Rather than treating these as competing priorities, DBR frames them as complementary. Success often depends on preserving the essential features of a design while allowing for thoughtful adaptations that ensure it works effectively in practice (McKenney & Reeves, 2019; Penuel et al., 2011).
