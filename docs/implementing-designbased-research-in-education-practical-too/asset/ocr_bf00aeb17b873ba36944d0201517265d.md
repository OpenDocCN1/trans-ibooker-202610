# SCALING UP DESIGN-BASED RESEARCH INTERVENTIONS

# EXAMPLE IN CONTEXT: SETTING THE SCENE

At St Brigid’s Primary School, educators and researchers worked side by side to develop *The Literacy Links Project*, a co-designed early literacy intervention rooted in phonemic awareness and explicit decoding instruction. Following strong outcomes over two years, the research team faced a new question: how could they bring this success to other schools without losing what made it work?
