# Table 1.1 Decision Matrix for Selecting Your Research Approach

| Explicit Focus On: | DBR | Action Research | Participatory Action Research | Formative Evaluation Research | Experimental Studies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Is the solution realised through action or implementation? | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Is there a focus on usefulness/usability? | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Is the approach responsive to the context of study? | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Is there a focus on collaboration with research partners? | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Is the approach iterative/non-linear? | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Is there a focus on theory and theory building? | Yes | No | No | No | Yes |
| Is there a commitment to social justice? | Not necessarily | Yes | Yes | No | No |
