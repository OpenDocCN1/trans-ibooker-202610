# ABOUT THE AUTHORS

Dr Ann Devitt is Associate Professor of Language Education at the School of Education and a Fellow of Trinity College Dublin, Ireland. She currently serves as the Head of School in the School of Education and the Academic Director of The Learnovate Centre, an Enterprise Ireland-funded centre of excellence for the future of work and learning. Her research interests lie at the intersection of technology and education, in particular language and literacy education.

Dr Jen O’Sullivan is a Lecturer in Literacy Education at Marino Institute of Education, Ireland, where she teaches across undergraduate and postgraduate teacher education programmes. Her PhD research, grounded in DBR, explored the role of phonemic awareness in supporting early literacy in a lower socioeconomic school context. She is a past president of the Literacy Association of Ireland and a Fulbright Scholar.

Dr Catherine O’Reilly is a Research Fellow in the School of Education at Trinity College Dublin, Ireland. She completed her PhD at Trinity College Dublin in 2024, where her research focused on early childhood education and the development of critical thinking skills in young children through storytelling. Her current research investigates the interplay and learning of young children with technology in the digital age, an area that harmonises her practical experience with her academic expertise.
