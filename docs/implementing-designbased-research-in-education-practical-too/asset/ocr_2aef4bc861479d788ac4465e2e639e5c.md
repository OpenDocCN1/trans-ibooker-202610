# RQ4: Implications for Research or Practice

# Characteristics of the Literature

Across the reviewed studies, implications included:

+   - The need for sustained professional development, especially for technology-rich environments (Agustiningrum, 2024; Jita & Dhliwayo, 2024).
+   - Better understanding of barriers and enablers for student-centred teaching (Soforon, 2024).
+   - Inclusive solutions through technologies such as eye-tracking (Donmez & Cagiltay, 2024).
+   - Methodological advances such as improved diagnostics for giftedness (Peperkorn & Wegner, 2024).
+   - Cross-sector and interdisciplinary applications of DBR (Kramer, 2024; Martel & Garcias, 2024).

# Summary of Findings

A consistent theme is the value of long-term, context-specific professional development to support teachers. DBR functions not only as a research methodology but also as a vehicle for professional growth and collaborative learning.

# Significance of Findings

The implications highlight DBR’s dual role in advancing educational research and empowering practitioners. For DBR to be effective, interventions must be sensitive to context, responsive to challenges, and supportive of sustained professional learning. This ensures that innovations are not only tested but also embedded in educational communities, leading to lasting change.
