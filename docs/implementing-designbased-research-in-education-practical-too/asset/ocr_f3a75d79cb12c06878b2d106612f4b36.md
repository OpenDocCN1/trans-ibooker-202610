# FRAMEWORKS AND MODELS FOR CONDUCTING DESIGN-BASED RESEARCH

# Introduction

Design-Based Research (DBR) is a methodology that integrates theoretical and practical insights to develop, test, and refine educational interventions in real-world settings. Various frameworks and models for conducting DBR have emerged, each emphasising different aspects of the research process and contributing uniquely to the field of education. This chapter outlines several prominent frameworks, highlighting the significance of each and how they guide the development of effective educational practices.

# PROMINENT FRAMEWORKS AND MODELS FOR CONDUCTING DBR

+   1. Design-Based Research Collective (DBRC) Framework (2003)
+   2. Barab and Squire’s Framework (2004)
+   3. Collins, Joseph, and Bielaczyc’s Design Experiments (2004)
