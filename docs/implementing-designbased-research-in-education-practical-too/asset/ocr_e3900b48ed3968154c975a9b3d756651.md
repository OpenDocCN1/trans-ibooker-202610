By focusing on producing interventions that are both scalable and evidence-based, the framework supports the development of sustainable educational innovations. Additionally, the generation of theoretical insights enriches the academic understanding of the teaching and learning process, fostering continuous improvement and innovation. This dual focus makes the EDR Framework an invaluable tool for advancing both practice and research in education.
