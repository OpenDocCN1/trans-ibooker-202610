# PHASE 1 BUILDING THE FOUNDATIONS

# THE LITERATURE REVIEW

# EXAMPLE IN CONTEXT

Imagine walking into a vast, sunlit library. Shelves stretch in every direction, filled with the voices of those who came before, educators, researchers, and authors of fiction and real-world stories. It isn’t silent, not really. If you pause, you’ll sense the quiet hum of ideas in conversation: what’s been tried, what’s failed, what has sparked change. Jerome Bruner, a key figure in educational theory, argued that the purpose of teaching is to go beyond the information given. He believed education should open possibilities for what could be and not just pass on what is. In a sense, that same principle guides the literature review. This is where your research begins. The literature is not just background reading. It’s a map of the territory: where others have ventured, what they’ve discovered, where the gaps and tensions lie.
