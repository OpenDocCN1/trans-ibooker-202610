# PRACTICAL TIP: CONTEXTUAL ANALYSIS PLANNING TEMPLATE

Before implementing an intervention in a new setting, use the following template to guide your planning:

| Area of Analysis | Key Questions | Evidence to Gather | Implications for Design |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learner Profile | What are the language, cultural, and socio-economic backgrounds of the learners? | Enrolment data, interviews | Modify content for accessibility and relevance |
| Infrastructure | What resources are available (tech, time, space, etc.)? | Site audit, educator feedback | Adjust delivery format and pacing |
| Curriculum Alignment | How does the intervention map onto existing curricula? | Curriculum documents, educator planning records | Clarify purpose and reduce redundancy |
| Educator Capacity | What training or support will be needed? | Surveys, professional development logs | Provide targeted training or mentoring |

# PAUSE AND REFLECT ...

+   - What assumptions are you making about the transferability of your intervention?
+   - How does the new context differ in terms of culture, resources, or policy?
+   - Are you listening to local voices in shaping how the intervention might be adapted?
