# Chapter Summary

In DBR, the human element is paramount. This chapter has highlighted how ethical awareness, effective communication, shared responsibilities, and reflective engagement with positionality form the foundation of successful research. By recognising the complexity of human interaction, researchers can build strong professional relationships, foster meaningful collaboration, and design interventions that are both context-sensitive and inclusive.

For early career researchers, this means embracing the emotional and intellectual labour that DBR entails. Rather than viewing these challenges as obstacles, they can be seen as opportunities for growth, moments where personal development and evolving worldviews enrich the knowledge co-created with others. Ultimately, DBR is not only about generating solutions for educational practice; it is also about transforming the researchers, educators, and learners who take part in the process.
