This process strengthens the theoretical robustness of the intervention and enhances its adaptability. For example, a project that involves scaling a collaborative writing strategy might find that, across all contexts, structured peer feedback is critical to success regardless of whether it is delivered digitally or on paper. As these principles emerge, it is important to document them clearly, with examples and rationales grounded in data. This supports both future implementation and theoretical refinement.
