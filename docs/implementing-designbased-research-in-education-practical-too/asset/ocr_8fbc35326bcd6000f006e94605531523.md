Sustainability is also not something that happens by default; it must be purposefully built into the design from the very beginning. This means embedding practices within everyday routines, aligning the intervention with broader organisational priorities, and investing in those who will carry the work forward. When these elements are thoughtfully integrated, scaling becomes an opportunity to generate lasting, practice-informed knowledge that is both relevant across contexts and resilient over time.
